My research focuses on modelling human interaction and designing decision-making methods for intelligent vehicles.

Socially compatible driving and interaction trust

National Key R&D project · Subtask Lead · February 2023–March 2026

Risk Perception, Quantitative Assessment and Safe Decision-Making for Highly Automated Vehicles in Complex Traffic.

  • Developed socially compatible game-theoretic interaction models and learned driving styles from NGSIM lane-change data.
  • Designed hierarchical multi-agent inverse reinforcement learning for model calibration.
  • Developed interaction-trust measurement and validated it through human-in-the-loop simulation.
  • Contributed six technical definitions to group standard T/ITS 0302-2025 on automated-vehicle risk perception and assessment terminology.

L4 autonomous-driving decisions

Dongfeng RoboTaxi · Decision Module Lead · December 2020–July 2022

Developed decision algorithms for road segments and intersections using decision trees and the COSIM lane-change model. Proposed a free–protected–forced three-stage pre-intersection model and delivered a workflow from MATLAB/Simulink to ROS/C++ and SCANeR validation.

Cooperative eco-driving in mixed traffic

Research project · Principal Investigator · September 2019–December 2021

Studied coordination between autonomous and human-driven vehicles at ramps. Developed cooperative eco-driving control using deep reinforcement learning and improved the commercial-vehicle energy model.

Vehicle dynamics and active safety

Research projects · 2019–2021

Investigated tyre-pressure regulation, tyre slip, friction, and optimisation-based energy-saving control. Built a seven-degree-of-freedom vehicle model with UniTire and supported drive-by-wire chassis testing, CAN-bus design, and GPS/IMU calibration.
