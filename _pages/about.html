Doctoral Researcher · College of Automotive Engineering · Jilin University

I study how autonomous vehicles can make safe, socially compatible decisions when interacting with human drivers. My research brings together driver behaviour modelling, multi-agent inverse reinforcement learning, and trust-aware interaction.

I am pursuing a PhD in Vehicle Engineering at Jilin University, with completion expected in March 2027. My work spans human-driving data analysis, algorithm development, and software-in-the-loop and human-in-the-loop validation.
