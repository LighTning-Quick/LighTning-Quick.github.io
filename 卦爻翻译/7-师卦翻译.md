《师》
军队 (Military) - 老师 (Mentor)

贞丈人吉，无咎。
翻译：坚守正道，任用贤明长者，吉利，没有过错。
英文：Maintain uprightness, employ wise elders, auspicious, no blame.
参考义项：军队 / 太师

《象》曰：地中有水，《师》。君子以容民畜众。
相容：大地中藏有水源，这就是师卦。君子用此革新百姓，培养兵众。
Compatibility: Water is stored within the earth; this is Shi. Therefore the noble one adopts this to renovate the people and cultivate the armed multitude.

初六：师出以律，否臧凶。
众多：军队出动要按照纪律，出言否定或赞同都凶险。
Multitude / Informed: When the army goes forth, it must follow strict discipline; speaking to negate or affirm is dangerous.
希腊文图式：eunomia
英文直译：good order / law-abiding

九二：在师中吉，无咎，王三锡命。
率领：拥戴忠厚和善的统帅，没有过错，君王三次赐予诏命。
Leadership / Reformer: Upholding a loyal and kind commander: no blame; the king grants edicts three times.
希腊文图式：typos
英文直译：pattern / example / mark


六三：师或舆尸，凶。
遵循：军队惑乱干预主持，凶险。
Capitulation / Deformation: The army in tumult meddles with the helm; ominous.
希腊文图式：spodas
英文直译：riot / clash

六四：师左次，无咎。
尊崇：将领佐助趋奉，没有过错。
Veneration / Conform: The commander flanked the left, attending with the utmost deference (of a disciple), there is no blame.
希腊文图式：parembole
英文直译：encampment

六五：田有禽，利执言，无咎。长子帅师，弟子舆尸，贞凶。
引导：田野中有擒获（俘虏），利于抓住而进行讯问，没有过错。长子统率军队，（他死后）弟弟运载神主牌（继续前行），保持这种状态会有凶险。
Conduct / Uniform: There are captives in the fields; it is favorable to seize and interrogate them, no blame. The eldest son leads the army with true command, (after he dies) the younger brother carries onward with his spirit tablet; if this persists, there will be danger.
希腊文图式：mimesis
英文直译：imitation / emulation

上六：大君有命，开国承家，小人勿用。
表率：大君富有声名，开创拯救国与家，小人不要这样。
Exemplar / Transform: The great ruler is highly prestigious: to found and save states and houses; petty people should not act like this.
希腊文图式：hegemonia
英文直译：leadership / supremacy / command
