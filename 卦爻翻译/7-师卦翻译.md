《师》
军队 (Military) - 顺应 (Compliance)

贞丈人吉，无咎。
翻译：坚守正道，任用贤明长者，吉利，没有过错。
英文：Maintain uprightness, employ wise elders, auspicious, no blame.
参考义项：军队 / 太师

《象》曰：地中有水，《师》。君子以容民畜众。
包容：大地中藏有水源，这就是师卦。君子用此包容百姓，蓄养兵众。
Containment: Water is stored within the earth; this is Shi. Therefore the noble one by this contains the people and nurtures the armed multitude.

初六：师出以律，否臧凶。
舆论：军队出动要按照纪律严明，出言否定或赞同都凶险。
Discourse / Information Environment: When the army goes forth, it must follow strict discipline; speaking to negate or affirm is dangerous.
希腊文图式：eunomia
英文直译：good order / law-abiding

九二：在师中吉，无咎，王三锡命。
表率：在军队的核心位置，吉利，没有过错，君王三次赐予诏命。
Example / Informer: Being at the core of the army brings good fortune, no blame, the king grants edicts three times.
希腊文图式：typos
英文直译：pattern / example / mark


六三：师或舆尸，凶。
械斗：军队有时运载尸体，凶险。
Conflict / Deformation: The army sometimes carries corpses; this is dangerous.
希腊文图式：spodas
英文直译：riot / clash

六四：师左次，无咎。
驻扎：军队暂时撤退，在左侧驻扎，没有过错。
Garrison / Platform: The army temporarily withdraws, stationed on the left; there is no blame.
希腊文图式：parembole
英文直译：encampment

六五：田有禽，利执言，无咎。长子帅师，弟子舆尸，贞凶。
效法：田野中有擒获，利于执而进行讯问，没有过错。长子统率军队，弟子（效法则）运载尸体，坚守这种状态会有凶险。
Emulation / Uniformity: There is capture in the fields; it is favorable to seize and interrogate, no blame. The eldest son leads the army, the younger brother (emulates then) carries corpses; if this persists, there will be danger.
希腊文图式：mimesis
英文直译：imitation / emulation

上六：大君有命，开国承家，小人勿用。
率领：大君有命令，开国承家，小人不要这样。
Leadership / Performance: The great ruler has commands, to open states and inherit houses; petty people should not act like this.
希腊文图式：hegemonia
英文直译：leadership / supremacy / command
