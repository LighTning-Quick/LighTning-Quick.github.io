《乾》
茎秆 (Stalk) - 井垣 (Bulwark)

元亨，利贞。
大通亨达，利于守持正固。
Great flourishing and free passage. It is favorable to remain upright and steadfast.
参考义项：上出，斗转/斡旋。

《象》曰：天行健，君子以自强不息。
自强：天道刚健运行，这就是乾。君子因此不断奋发、劝勉自己。
Self-Strengthening: The way of heaven moves with firm vigor; this is Qian. Therefore the noble one continually strives on and exhorts himself.

初九：潜龙，勿用。
空乏：龙潜藏未出，此时不宜施用。
Depletion / Subact: The dragon lies hidden; it is not yet the time to put it to use.

九二：见龙在田，利见大人。
建立：龙已出现在田野之上，利于见大人。
Foundation / Act: The dragon has appeared in the field. It is favorable to see the people in high positions.

九三：君子终日乾乾，夕惕若。厉，无咎。
干求：君子整日勤勉奋发，到了夜晚仍警惕戒惧。虽有危险，终无咎害。
Aspiration / Enact: The noble one labors on vigorously all day and remains watchful into the night. There is danger, yet no blame.

九四：或跃在渊，无咎。
干犯：或可跃起于深渊，没有过错。
Encroachment / Transact: One may leap up from the deep. There is no blame.

九五：飞龙在天，利见大人。
治理：龙飞腾于天，利于见大人。
Governance / Coact: The dragon soars in heaven. It is favorable to see the great person.

上九：亢龙，有悔。
捍卫：倔强的龙，有悔。
Safeguarding / Overact: A stubborn dragon. There is regret.

用九：见群龙无首，吉。
通九 主管：看到群龙而无首领，吉。
All Nine (in Charge): One sees a host of dragons with no leader. This is auspicious.
