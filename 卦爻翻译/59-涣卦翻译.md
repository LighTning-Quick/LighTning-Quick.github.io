《涣》

涣涣 (Gush) - 涣衍 (Spread)

亨。王假有庙。利涉大川，利贞。
通达。君王来到宗庙。适合涉过大河，适合守持正固。
Accomplishment. The king comes to the ancestral temple. It is fitting to cross the great river, and fitting to hold fast to what is right.
参考义项：（𡨡）宗庙祭礼。宗庙之中，先进行盛大仪式，再发扬传承精神。

《象》曰：风行水上，《涣》。先王以享于帝，立庙。
祭享：风吹行在水面上，这就是涣卦。古代君王以此祭享上帝，建立宗庙。
Sacrificial Offering: Wind moves across the surface of the water; this is Huan. Earlier kings adopted it to offer sacrifices to the High God and establish ancestral temples.

初六：用拯马壮，吉。
改换：用阉割的马，强壮，吉祥。
Malleability / Reflux: Use a gelded horse, strong and vigorous; auspicious.

九二：涣奔其机[^1]，悔亡。
炳焕：璀璨华美的光明，悔恨消失。
Conspicuous / Efflux: Brilliant and splendid radiance appears, and regret vanishes.

六三：涣其躬，无悔。
畔换：彪炳自身，没有悔恨。
Rampant / Superflux: One makes oneself conspicuous, and there is no regret.

六四：涣其群，元吉。涣有丘，匪夷所思。
涣散：散开群体，大吉。涣散田垄，这不是平常人容易想到的。
Dispersion / Difflux: Scatter the group; greatly auspicious. Scatter the field ridges as well; this is not something ordinary people would readily think of.

九五：涣汗其[^2]大号，涣王居，无咎。
奂-众多：大旱，大声哭嚎，发散王的囤积，没有过错。
Multitude / Afflux: Great drought and loud cries, spread out the king's stored reserves; there is no blame.

上九：涣其血，去逖出，无咎。
涣泮：发散他的血，使忧惧离去而脱出，没有过错。
Dissolution / Transflux: Disperse his blood, let dread depart and withdraw, and so break free; there is no blame.
# 注疏

[^1]: 倒装句，机涣奔，机通几，是明的异体字；涣通焕，奔通贲，表示华美
[^2]: 帛书本作涣其肝。涣作奂，大，汗通旱。
