《观》
观阙 (Watchtower) - 台榭 (Terrace-Spectacle)

盥而不荐。有孚颙若。
洗手灌祭而不献上牲牢。肃敬的样子使人信从。
Wash hands for the libation but do not present the sacrificial offerings. Reverent demeanor inspires compliance.
参考义项：灌祭（祼礼）

《象》曰：风行地上，《观》。先王以省方观民设教。
《象》传说：风行在地上，这就是观卦。先王因此省视四方、观察民情、设立教令。
The Xiang commentary says: The wind moves over the earth; this is Guan. The former kings, accordingly, inspected the regions, observed the people, and established edicts.

初六：童观，小人无咎，君子吝。
勤勉：劳作操持，小人没有错咎，君子有鄙吝。
Diligence: Labor and management; for the petty person there is no blame, but for the noble one there is regret.
《象》曰："初六童观"，"小人"道也。
《象》传说："初六童观"，是小人的道。
The Xiang commentary says: "The bottom six, childlike observation" is the way of the petty person.

六二：窥观，利女贞。
观察：窥看，利于女子占卜。
Observation: Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's divination.
《象》曰："窥观女贞"，亦可丑也。
《象》传说："窥观女贞"，也是可丑的。
The Xiang commentary says: "Peeping observation; favorable for a woman to persevere" can also be shameful.

六三：观我生，进退。
借鉴/鉴戒：观察我，反省，进退。
Benchmark / Reflection: Observe and examine myself; advance or withdraw.
《象》曰："观我生进退"，未失道也。
《象》传说："观我生进退"，没有失去正道。
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe my own life; advance or withdraw" means not having lost the Way.

六四：观国之光，利用宾于王。
辅助：鼓励国家的宽宏，利于做君王的宾客。
Assistance: Encourage the state's broad-mindedness; favorable to serve as a guest to the king.
《象》曰："观国之光"，尚"宾"也。
《象》传说："观国之光"，推崇"宾客"。
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe the splendor of the realm" means esteeming the "guest."

九五：观我生，君子无咎。
示范：借鉴我，赞许，君子没有错咎。
Exemplarity: Borrow from me, approve; for the noble one there is no blame.
《象》曰："观我生"，观民也。
《象》传说："观我生"，是说观察人民。
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe my own life" means observing the people.

上九：观其生，君子无咎。
劝导：辅助他省悟，君子没有错咎。
Exhortation: Assist him to awaken; for the noble one there is no blame.
《象》曰："观其生"，志未平也。
《象》传说："观其生"，是说志向尚未平定。
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe others' lives" means the will is not yet content.
