《习坎》
反复炼 (Training) - 惯囚槛 (Confinement)

习坎：有孚维心，亨。行有尚。
翻译：真诚，听从心念，通达。行为有常法。
英文：Sincerity, heeding the heart's intent, free passage. Actions follow constant principles.
参考义项：练，拣选，操练

《象》曰：水洊至，习坎。君子以常德行，习教事。
训练：水流相续而至，这就是习坎。君子以此崇尚德行，训练教化之事。
Training: Water flows continuously, one after another; this is The Abyss. The noble one thereby reveres virtue and trains in matters of education.

初六：习坎，入于坎窞，凶。
陷落：练习挖坑，却跌入深坑，凶险。
Submersion / Trouble: Practicing excavation, yet falling into deep pits; there is misfortune.

九二：坎有险，求小得。
坎壈：坑中有险阻，寻求小有所得。
Rugged / Distress: The pit contains perils; one seeks small gains while enduring compression.

六三：来之坎坎，险且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。
掘坑：来时咣咣（挖坑），艰难且深陷，落入坎坑深处，不可有所作为。
Excavation / Entrap: Came to dig with a thud, perilous and sinking deep; falling into the depths of the pit. one cannot take action.

六四：樽酒簋贰用缶，纳约自牖，终无咎。
欿-不自满：一樽酒、两簋食，使用瓦器盛装，通过窗户放进绳子。终无过错。
Hollow / Tribulation: One vessel of wine, two baskets of grain, using earthenware containers, delivering it with rope through the window. ultimately there is no blame.

九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。
敛藏：瘗坎不盈满，（填埋后的）土坻仅得持平，无过错。
Containment / Turbulence: The sacrificial pit is not filled; the earthen mound after burial is merely level; there is no blame.

上六：系用徽纆，寘于丛棘，三岁不得，凶。
忧恨：被绳索捆绑，放置在荆棘丛中，三年不得解脱，凶险。
Remorse / Trough: Bound with ropes and placed among thorns; unable to escape for three years. there is misfortune.
