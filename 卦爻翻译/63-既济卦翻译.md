《既济》
既䍤-过滤 (Filtration) - 既成 (Finality)

既济：亨。小利贞。初吉终乱。
既济：亨通，宜于小处守正。开始时行吉礼，终将治理。
When matters are already settled, all proceeds. Small persistence is favorable. Initially performing auspicious rites, ultimately bringing order.
参考义项：斋戒。

《象》曰：水在火上，《既济》。君子以思患而豫防之。
方正：水在火上，既济。君子由此伺察隐患并预先防堵。
Normal: Water rests upon fire; this is Settlement. The superior person, contemplating this, watches for potential calamities and guards against them in advance.

初九：曳其轮，濡其尾，无咎。
增补助益：车轮拖曳，使其边缘柔软湿润，没有灾咎。
When the wheels drag, makes the rim soft and moist; no blame.

六二：妇丧其茀，勿逐，七日得。
贯通：妇人失去了车上的帷障，不必追赶，七日后自然会得回。
The woman loses her carriage curtain; do not pursue it—within seven days it will be restored.

九三：高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。
成功：高宗征伐鬼方，三年才战胜，小人不可用此。
King Wuding attacked the Ghost Regions; three years were required to conquer them. Petty persons should not employ this.

六四：有衣袽，终日戒。
忧愁：寒衣有破旧的衣絮，整日警戒。
Winter garments have worn-out cloth rags; vigilant all day.

九五：东邻杀牛，不如西邻之禴祭，实受其福。
斋-庄敬：东邻杀牛（盛祭），不如西邻的（简约）禴祭，却真正受福。
The eastern neighbor slaughters an ox (grand sacrifice), yet it is not as good as the western neighbor's (simple) spring offering, which truly receives blessing.

上六：濡其首，厉。
停止：头部没入水中，危险。
The head becomes soaked; danger.
