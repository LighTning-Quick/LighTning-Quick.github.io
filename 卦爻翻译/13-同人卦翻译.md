《同人》
严明 (Strictness) - 和平(Peace)

同人于野，亨。利涉大川。利君子贞。
在屋檐下随同他人，通达。利于涉过大河，利于君子守持正固。
Following others under the eaves, brings success. It is favorable to cross the great river, and favorable for the noble one to hold fast to what is upright.
参考义项：佣役。虽然不同，依然随同。

《象》曰：天与火，同人。君子以类族辨物。
辨类：天与火相与，这就是同人。君子以此遵循族属治理万物。
Discernment of Kinds: Heaven together with fire forms Tongren. Therefore the noble one governs things according to their clan affiliation..

初九：同人于门，无咎。
威重：在大门通告人，没有过错。
Gravity / Accord: One announces the people at the gate; there is no blame.

六二：同人于宗，吝。
通-交往：只在宗族施行亲仁，会有局促与憾惜。
Intercourse / Concord: If one practices kin-centered humaneness only within the clan, there will be constraint and regret.

九三：伏戎于莽，升其高陵，三岁不兴。
通-开辟：在草莽中驾驭兵车，要登上高陵，长久不能兴兵。
Pioneering / Discordance: One drives war chariots through the overgrowth and seeks to ascend the high ridge, yet for a long time cannot raise the troops.

九四：乘其墉，弗克攻，吉。
慎重：登上对方的城墙，却没能攻占，吉。
Prudence / Record: One climbs the other side's wall yet fails to capture it; this is auspicious.

九五：同人先号咷而后笑，大师克，相遇。
统一：聚集人，先有哭号，后有欢笑；大军克敌之后，终得相遇。
Unification / Coordinator: People are gathered; first there is crying out and weeping, then laughter. After the great army overcomes the enemy, they finally meet.

上九：同人于郊，无悔。
种-培植：在郊外操作锄头，没有悔恨。
Cultivation / Cardiac Transplant: One works the hoe in the outskirts; there is no regret.
