《剥》
柞树 (Plane Tree) - 服马(Wheel Horse)

不利有攸往。
不利于主动前往做事。
It is not favorable to take the initiative and go forward with affairs.
参考义项：停殡（仆/朴）

《象》曰：山附于地，《剥》。上以厚下安宅。
厚待：山贴附在地上，这就是剥卦。居上位的人因此应当厚待下方的人，使居处安定。
Generous treatment: The mountain clings to the earth; this is Bo. Therefore, those above should treat those below generously and make their dwelling secure.

初六：剥床以足蔑，贞凶。
依凭：击打床坐来制止轻蔑，这样下去会有凶险。
Reliance / Suppose: Strike the seat to check contempt; if this continues, there will be danger.

六二：剥床以辨蔑，贞凶。
朴实：掊挖底部来辨明污蔑，这样下去会有凶险。
Plainness / Propose: Dig into the base to distinguish and expose slander; if this continues, there will be danger.

六三：剥之，无咎。
相异：背离它，没有错咎。
Divergence / Compose: Deviate from it; there is no blame.

六四：剥床以肤，凶。
承担：附着于床来仆服，凶险。
Bearing / Depose: One relies on the bed and lies prostrate in submission; this is dangerous.

六五：贯鱼以宫人，宠，无不利。
服御：射中鱼靠宫人，宠劳，没有不利。
Harnessing / Dispose: Because of the people in the palace, the ruler is able to shoot the fish in ordered sequence and therefore bestows favor; there is nothing unfavorable.

上九：硕果不食，君子得舆，小人剥庐。
裸露：如大果子还没有被吃掉。君子（暴露）能得到车可乘载，小人却如同把自己的房屋也剥除了。
Denudation / Expose: It is like a large fruit not yet eaten. The noble one can obtain a carriage to ride in, while the petty person strips away even his own hut.
