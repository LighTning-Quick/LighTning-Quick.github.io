《节》
竹节 (Bamboo-Node) - 符节 (Tally / Credential)

亨。苦节，不可贞。
通达。病痛结节，就不可以守为常道。
Success. A painful knotting sickness cannot be kept as a constant norm.
参考义项：疖子（小疮）。节不是一味收紧，而是立界、分流、适度。

《象》曰：泽上有水，《节》。君子以制数度，议德行。
节制、适宜：泽上蓄水，这就是节卦。君子因此制定数目法度，审议德行的边界。
Regulation and Fitness: Water stands stored above the marsh; this is Jie. Therefore the noble one sets numerical measures and deliberates the boundaries of conduct.

初九：不出户庭，无咎。
节度：不走出户庭，没有过错。
Regulation / Stricture: Do not go out of the household court; there is no blame.

九二：不出门庭，凶。
分明：不走出门庭，就有凶险。
Demarcation / Restrict: If one does not go out of the gate-court, there is danger.

六三：不节若，则嗟若，无咎。
管束：若不能适度，就会因此嗟叹。还不至于有咎。
Restraint / Constrict: If one cannot keep moderation, one will sigh in lament; it still does not amount to blame.

六四：安节，亨。
适度：安适之节，所以通达。
Moderation / Strictness: Ease within due measure, and therefore there is success.

九五：甘节，吉，往有尚。
证验：甘美而合宜的节，吉祥；前往会受到推重。
Verification / Instrict: Sweet and fitting due measure is auspicious; going forward will bring honor.

上六：苦节，贞凶。悔亡。
操守：竭力的自我压迫，固守下去就有凶险；但悔恨可亡。
Integrity / Overstrict: Strenuous self-oppression, persisting in it brings danger; yet regret can disappear.
