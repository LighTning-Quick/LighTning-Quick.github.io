《临》
面对 (Confrontation) - 临视 (Oversight)

元亨，利贞。至于八月有凶。
大为通达，适宜正固。到了八月会有凶险。
Originally abundant and smooth; favorable to persist in rectitude. Come the eighth month, there will be misfortune.
参考义项：汛期（瀶，山谷水出）

《象》曰：泽上有地，《临》。君子以教思无穷，容保民无疆。
《象》传说：泽上有地，这就是临卦。君子因此竭力服务职守而不穷尽，劳苦安定百姓而不划分疆界。
The Xiang commentary says: Above the marsh is the earth; this is Lin. The noble one, accordingly, exhausts himself in service without end, and labors to settle the people without delimiting borders.

初九：咸临，贞吉。
守卫：函守，贞正则吉祥。
Vigilance: Contained; persevering brings auspiciousness.
《象》曰："咸临贞吉"，志行正也。
《象》传说："咸临贞吉"，是说志向践行端正。
The Xiang commentary says: "Fully felt approach; persevering brings auspiciousness" means the will moves in rectitude.

九二：咸临，吉，无不利。
攻伐：减伤，吉祥，没有什么不利。
Assault: Reducing; auspicious, nothing is disadvantageous.
《象》曰："咸临吉无不利"，未顺命也。
《象》传说："咸临吉无不利"，是说不顺从命运。
The Xiang commentary says: "Fully felt approach; auspicious, nothing is disadvantageous" means not yet submissive to the command.

六三：甘临，无攸利；既忧之，无咎。
蒞止：酣畅，无所利益；既然忧虑它，就没有错咎。
Arrival: Being uninhibited, brings no benefit; once one worries about it, there is no blame.
《象》曰："甘临"，位不当也。"既忧之"，"咎"不长也。
《象》传说："甘临"，位置不恰当。"既忧之"，是说咎害不会长久。
The Xiang commentary says: "Greedy approach" means the position is inappropriate. "Once one worries about it" means the "blame" will not endure.

六四：至临，无咎。
给予：给与（以）质询，没有错咎.
Giving: Questioning; there is no blame.
《象》曰："至临无咎"，位当也。
《象》传说："至临无咎"，位置恰当。
The Xiang commentary says: "Direct approach; there is no blame" means the position is appropriate.

六五：知临，大君之宜，吉。
临摹：记录，大君的适宜，吉祥。
Copying: Recording—appropriate for the great ruler; auspicious.
《象》曰："大君之宜"，行中之谓也。
《象》传说："大君之宜"，是说践行中道。
The Xiang commentary says: "Appropriate for the great ruler" means practicing the Mean.

上六：敦临，吉，无咎。
监理：敦促，吉祥，没有错咎.
Supervision: Urging; auspicious, there is no blame.
《象》曰："敦临"之"吉"，志在内也。
《象》传说："敦临之吉"，是说志向在内。
The Xiang commentary says: The "auspiciousness" of "sincere approach" means the will is directed inward.
