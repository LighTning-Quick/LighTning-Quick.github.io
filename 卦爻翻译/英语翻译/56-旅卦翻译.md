《旅》
札甲 (Lamellar Armour) - 稆穭 (Wildling)

小亨。旅贞吉。
小有通达。行旅之中，守持其正则吉。
Small success. In travel, if one keeps to what is right, there is good fortune.
参考义项：去国离家。此卦不主“大成”，而主异地求存中的谨慎、自持与暂时安顿。


《象》曰：山上有火，旅。君子以明慎用刑，而不留狱。
得力：山上有火，这就是旅。君子因此明察而谨慎地用刑，不使狱案久拖不决。
Gaining Force: There is fire on the mountain; this is Lu. Therefore the noble one uses punishments with clarity and caution, and does not let court cases drag on unresolved.

初六：旅琐琐，斯其所，取灾。
笨拙：细碎地讲述，失去适宜，因此自取其灾。
Clumsiness: One speaks in a petty and fragmented way, losing what is fitting, and thus brings disaster on oneself.

六二：旅即次，怀其资，得童仆，贞。
奉养：行旅之人就近住下，怀藏资财，又得到童仆可用；守正则可安。
Sustenance: The traveler settles into a lodging nearby, keeps his goods in his care, and gains a young servant to use; if he remains upright, he can be secure.

九三：旅焚其次，丧其童仆，贞厉。
众多：军队焚烧他的旅舍，（他）又失去童仆；即使守正，也有危险。
Multitude: The army burns his lodging, and he also loses his young servant; even if he holds to what is right, there is danger.

九四：旅于处，得其资斧，我心不快。
旅居：羁旅而暂处一地，也得到了资财器用，但我心中并不畅快。
Sojourn: One sojourns and temporarily stays in one place, and also obtains goods and tools, yet in my heart there is no gladness.

六五：射雉，一矢亡，终以誉命。
膂力：射中雉鸟，虽失一箭，最终却因此得到称誉与任命。
Prowess: One shoots a pheasant; though a single arrow is lost, in the end one gains praise and appointment because of it.

上九：鸟焚其巢，旅人先笑后号咷。丧牛于易，凶。
粗鲁：鸟焚其巢，旅人先前欢笑，后来却号啕痛哭；（如）在易地失去牛，凶。
Brutality: A bird burns its nest. The traveler first laughs, then later howls and weeps; it is like losing an ox in the land of Yi. Ominous.
