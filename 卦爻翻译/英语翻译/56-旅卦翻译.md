《旅》<br>
膂：脊骨 (Backbone) - 札甲 (Lamellar Armour)<br>
<br>
小亨。旅贞吉。<br>
小心，通达。（望楼示警以）报告，守持其正则吉。<br>
Careful, there is passage. (The watchtower signaled warnings to) Report, steadfastness brings good fortune.<br>
参考义项：橹，望楼。<br>
<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山上有火，旅。君子以明慎用刑，而不留狱。<br>
慎-忧惧：山上有火，这就是旅。君子因此力争公正刑罚，不使狱案久拖不决。<br>
Apprehension: There is fire on the mountain; this is Lu. The noble one therefore strives for just punishment,and does not prolong legal cases.<br>
<br>
初六：旅琐琐，斯其所，取灾。<br>
鲁钝：细碎地讲述，失去适宜，因此自取其灾。<br>
Reticence: One speaks in a petty and fragmented way, losing what is fitting, and thus brings disaster on oneself.<br>
<br>
六二：旅即次，怀其资，得童仆，贞。<br>
奉养：行旅之人就近住下，怀藏资财，又得到童仆可用，征收。<br>
Sustenance: The traveler reaches a lodging, carries his goods, and gains the service of a young attendant. Collection.<br>
<br>
九三：旅焚其次，丧其童仆，贞厉。<br>
陈列：奉养，焚烧他的旅舍，（他）又失去童仆；即使守正，也有危险。<br>
Exposure: Sustenance. (Resulting in) burns his lodging and loses his young attendant. Even with steadfastness, there is danger.<br>
<br>
九四：旅于处，得其资斧，我心不快。<br>
客处：羁旅而暂处一地，也得到了资财器用，但我心中并不畅快。<br>
Sojourn: One sojourns and temporarily stays in one place, and also obtains goods and tools, yet in my heart there is no gladness.<br>
<br>
六五：射雉，一矢亡，终以誉命。<br>
膂力：射中雉鸟，虽失一箭，最终却因称誉轻慢。<br>
Prowess: One shoots a pheasant and loses a single arrow; in the end, one one became dismissive because of the praise.<br>
<br>
上九：鸟焚其巢，旅人先笑后号咷。丧牛于易，凶。<br>
粗鲁：鸟焚其巢，旅人先前欢笑，后来却号啕痛哭；（如）在易地失去牛，凶。<br>
Brutality: A bird burns its nest. The traveler first laughs, then howls and weeps; oxen are lost in the land of Yi. Misfortune.<br>
