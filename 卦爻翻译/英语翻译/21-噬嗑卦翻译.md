《噬嗑》[^1]
挫折 (Blunt) - 哲：明智 (Wise)

亨。利用狱。
通达。利于决断刑狱。
Accomplishment. It is favorable to use for judicial decisions.
参考义项：折，裁决；裁断刑狱

《象》曰：雷电，《噬嗑》。先王以明罚敕法。
显明：雷电，《噬嗑》。先王以承受惩罚诫敕律法。
Manifest: Thunder and lightning combine; this is Shi Ke. The earlier kings used this to bear penalties and admonish the laws.

初九：屦校灭趾，无咎。
折-阻止：戴上脚枷遮住脚趾，没有灾咎。
Prevent: Put on the cangue that covers the toes; there is no blame.

六二：噬肤灭鼻，无咎。
折损：吞食柔肉，没过鼻子，没有灾咎。
Damage: Swallow soft flesh, submerging the nose; there is no blame.

六三：噬腊肉遇毒，小吝，无咎。
谨慎：吃腊肉遇到毒物，小有吝惜，没有灾咎。
Caution: Eat cured meat and encounter poison; slight regret, but no blame.

九四：噬干胏，得金矢。利艰，贞吉。
磕-敲击：咬干肉带骨，得到金属箭。利于跟随，守正则得吉祥。
Knock: Bite dried meat with bone, obtain a metal arrow. Favorable to follow; maintaining rectitude brings auspiciousness.

六五：噬干肉，得黄金。贞厉，无咎。
盟誓：咬干肉，得到黄色青铜器。继续有危厉，没有灾咎。
Pledge: Bite dried meat, obtain yellow bronzes. Perseverance amidst danger; there is no blame.

上九：何校灭耳，凶。
折-裁决：承受绞刑隐没耳朵，凶。
Verdict: Bear the execution cangue that submerges the ears; dangerous.

# 注疏

[^1]: 噬嗑：颐中有物曰噬嗑。
