《噬嗑》[^1]
誓：谨慎 (Prudent) - 哲：明智 (Wise)

亨。利用狱。
通达。利于决断刑狱。
Accomplishment. It is favorable to use for judicial decisions.
参考义项：折，裁决；裁断狱案

《象》曰：雷电，《噬嗑》。先王以明罚敕法。
饰-显示：雷电，《噬嗑》。先王以严明惩罚诫敕律法。
Manifest: Thunder and lightning combine; this is Shi Ke. The earlier kings used this to enforce strict and impartial penalties, to admonish the laws.

初九：屦校灭趾，无咎。
誓-告诫：戴上脚枷遮住脚趾，没有灾咎。
Warn: Put on the cangue that covers the toes; there is no blame.

六二：噬肤灭鼻，无咎。
折服：吞食柔肉，没过鼻子，没有灾咎。
Convince: Swallow soft flesh, submerging the nose; there is no blame.

六三：噬腊肉遇毒，小吝，无咎。
挫折：吃腊肉遇到毒物，小有吝惜，没有灾咎。
Blunt: Eat cured meat and encounter poison; slight regret, but no blame.

九四：噬干胏，得金矢。利艰，贞吉。
哲-了解：咬带骨的干肉，得到金属箭。利于跟随，守正则得吉祥。
Grasp: Bite into dried meat on the bone and obtain a metal arrow. Favorable to follow; maintaining rectitude brings auspiciousness.

六五：噬干肉，得黄金。贞厉，无咎。
发誓：咬干肉，记录于黄色青铜器。坚持则有危厉，没有灾咎。
Swear: Biting into dried meat, recording on yellow bronze. Perseverance brings danger; there is no blame.

上九：何校灭耳，凶。
界-划分：紧勒着敛尸束带，消除了圈耳（系死结），凶。
Demarcate: Pull the burial shroud ties tightly, eliminating loops (to tie dead knots) ; omen of misfortune.

# 注疏

[^1]: 噬嗑：颐中有物曰噬嗑。
