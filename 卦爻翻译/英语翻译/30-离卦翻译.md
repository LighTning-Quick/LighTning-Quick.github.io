《离》<br>
利：贪爱 (Crave) - 丽：施加 (Impose)<br>
<br>
利贞。亨。畜牝牛吉。<br>
利于守持正固。亨通。畜养母牛，吉祥。<br>
(Magnificent) It is favorable to remain steadfast. Success. (Fence) Rearing a cow, brings good fortune.<br>
参考义项：华丽/篱笆<br>
<br>
《象》曰：明两作，《离》。大人以继明照于四方。[^4]<br>
劭-劝勉：光明两次升起，这就是离卦。伟大的人将继承名位昭示于四方。（日月互相延续光明，照射四方）<br>
Encourage: Brightness rises twice; this is Li. The great person therefore succeeds to a title and proclaims it to all directions. (The sun and moon continue each other's radiance, illuminating every direction.)<br>
<br>
初九：履错，然敬之，无咎。<br>
罗-约束：勘察措施，认可或梗阻它们，没有灾咎。<br>
Contain: Investigate the measures taken, and approve or obstruct them.; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：黄离，元吉。<br>
猛厉：潢潢威武，凶恶，吉。<br>
Ferocious: Valiant, violent; greatly auspicious.<br>
<br>
九三：日昃之离，不鼓缶而歌，则大耋之嗟，凶。<br>
迟疑：太阳西斜时的光，如不击缶而歌，则有大龄老人的叹息，凶。<br>
Hesitating: In the light of the declining sun, if one does not beat the earthen vessel and sing, there will instead be the lament of extreme old age; misfortune.<br>
<br>
九四：突如其来如，焚如，死如，弃如。<br>
利-迅疾：赐予他们就劝勉啊，如奔跑，如拼死，如厌弃。<br>
Quick: Bestowing brings their submission: like running, straining, or discarding.<br>
<br>
六五：出涕沱若，戚嗟若，吉。<br>
利-适宜[^5]：停止泪如雨下，靠近赞叹，吉祥。<br>
Favorable: Ceasing the streaming of tears, getting close to offer praise, auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：王用出征，有嘉折首，获匪其丑[^3]，无咎。<br>
丽-执法：君王用于出征，用嘉礼使人屈从伏罪，法度和原则是手铐，没有灾咎。<br>
Enforce: The king sets out on campaign. Using civil ceremonies to make people submit and confess crimes. The law and principles are handcuffs. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
# 注疏<br>
<br>
[^3]: 折首，屈从、伏罪。获丑（俘获敌众）<br>
[^4]: 照有昭示的意思，此外通劭，为关键字。继承名位，昭告四方。此外明与照都与光相关。<br>
[^5]: 适宜，不利而能励。离王公，利于前往，能使富裕，而劝勉、照四方。<br>
