《离》[^1]<br>
黎：黍胶 (Millet Paste) - 罗网 (Clap Net)<br>
<br>
利贞。亨。畜牝牛吉。<br>
利于守持正固。通达。畜养母牛，吉祥。<br>
It is favorable to remain steadfast. There is passage. Rearing a cow brings good fortune.<br>
参考义项：燎祭/篱笆<br>
<br>
《象》曰：明两作，《离》。大人以继明照于四方。[^4]<br>
照明：光明两次升起，这就是离卦。伟大的人以此缔结盟约昭告四方。（日月互相延续光明，照射四方）<br>
Illumination: Brightness rises twice; this is Li. The great person clinches an alliance and proclaims it to the four quarters. (The sun and moon continue each other's radiance, illuminating every direction.)<br>
<br>
初九：履错，然敬之，无咎。<br>
罗致：执行追捕，竟然尊敬他，没有灾咎。<br>
Gathering: Execute the pursuit, unexpectedly respect him; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：黄离[^2]，元吉。<br>
配对：黄鹂，大吉。<br>
Pairing: The yellow oriole; greatly auspicious.<br>
<br>
九三：日昃之离，不鼓缶而歌，则大耋之嗟，凶。<br>
迟疑：太阳西斜时的光，如不击缶而歌，则有大龄老人的叹息，凶。<br>
Hesitating: In the light of the declining sun, if one does not beat the earthen vessel and sing, there will instead be the lament of extreme old age; misfortune.<br>
<br>
九四：突如，其来如，焚如，死如，弃如。<br>
求取：如换毛，他们的归服啊，如覆败，如陈尸，如厌弃。<br>
Seeking: Like molting, their submission: overthrowing, exposing (corpses), and discarding.<br>
<br>
六五：出涕沱若，戚嗟若，吉。<br>
广布：泪如雨下，忧戚嗟叹，吉祥。<br>
Spreading: Tears pour down in torrents; sorrow and sighing—auspicious.<br>
《象》曰："六五"之"吉"，离王公也。[^5]<br>
六五的吉祥，（是因为）广布王公（以封国）。<br>
The good fortune of the six in the fifth place lies in spreading the princes for the expanse of feudal territories.<br>
<br>
上九：王用出征，有嘉折首，获匪其丑[^3]，无咎。<br>
执法：君王用于出征，用嘉礼使人屈从伏罪，法度和原则是手铐，没有灾咎。<br>
Enforce: The king sets out on campaign. Using civil ceremonies to make people submit and confess crimes. The law and principles are handcuffs. There is no blame.<br>
