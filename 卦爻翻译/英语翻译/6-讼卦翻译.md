《讼》<br>
包容 (Hold) - 酬庸 (Reward)<br>
<br>
有孚，窒，惕，中吉，终凶。利见大人。不利涉大川。<br>
（防箭蔽障）有帮助，遏制了（箭矢），（仍需）警惕；适可而止则吉，最终则凶。适宜求见大人，不适宜渡过大河。<br>
The arrow shield helps by stopping the arrows, yet vigilance is still required. Stopping at the appropriate point brings good fortune, but the end brings misfortune. It is favorable to see the great person, but not favorable to cross the great river.<br>
参考义项：容，射礼唱获者防箭蔽障<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天与水违行，《讼》。君子以作事谋始。<br>
作-兴起：天向上运行，水向下流动，彼此背离，这就是讼卦。君子以其职务诅誓，来谋划开始。（不成功就离职）<br>
Arise: Heaven moves upward while water flows downward; their courses diverge. This is Song. The noble one vows upon their office before laying out plans (pledging to step down should they fail).<br>
<br>
初六：不永所事，小有言，终吉。<br>
争讼：若从事不延长，虽略有责言，最终吉祥。<br>
Contention: Do not prolong if engaged in; though there may be reproach, in the end there is good fortune.<br>
<br>
九二：不克讼，归而逋，其邑人三百户，无眚。<br>
共同：不能供给，封邑的三百户惭愧而逃离，没有灾祸。<br>
Together: Unable to provide, their three hundred households in their fiefdom fled in shame, spared from disaster.<br>
<br>
六三：食旧德，贞厉，终吉。或从王事，无成。<br>
申冤：背弃旧有的行为，坚持有危险，最终吉祥；如果随从君王职事，不定罪。<br>
Vindication: Abandon the former conduct. Persistence brings danger, but in the end there is good fortune. If one follows the king in official service, there is no conviction.<br>
<br>
九四：不克讼，复即命渝，安，贞吉。<br>
通融：不能责备，宽宥（违法）就食，采用变通，(妥善)安置；守正则吉。<br>
Accommodation: Don't blame them, granting leniency for (illegally) getting meals, and adopting alternative methods, proper resettlement. Constancy brings good fortune.<br>
<br>
九五：讼元吉。<br>
功绩：赏赐，愿慕，吉。<br>
Achievement: Awarded, admired, good fortune.<br>
<br>
上九：或锡之鞶带，终朝三褫之。<br>
使用：有时赐予大带，却在一日之内三次被夺去。<br>
Appurtenance: At times, one is granted a ceremonial belt, yet within a single day it is taken away three times.<br>
