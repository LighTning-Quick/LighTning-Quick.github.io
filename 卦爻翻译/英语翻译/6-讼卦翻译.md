《讼》<br>
包容 (Tolerate) - 酬庸 (Reward)<br>
<br>
有孚，窒，惕，中吉，终凶。利见大人。不利涉大川。<br>
（防箭蔽障）有帮助，阻隔（箭矢），仍使人警惕惧怕；适中则吉，终竞则凶。适宜求见大人，不适宜渡过大河。<br>
(A barrier against arrows) It helps and blocks (incoming arrows), yet it still keeps one in a state of vigilance and fear. A moderate approach brings good fortune; to persist to the very end leads to misfortune. It is favorable to seek an audience with a person of high standing, but unfavorable to cross a great river.<br>
参考义项：容，射礼唱获者防箭蔽障；恫，推动，惕惧<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天与水违行，《讼》。君子以作事谋始。<br>
作-兴起：天向上运行，水向下流动，彼此背离，这就是讼卦。君子以其职务诅誓，来谋划开始。（不成功就离职）<br>
Arise: Heaven moves upward while water flows downward; their courses diverge. This is Song. The noble one vows upon their office before laying out plans (pledging to step down should they fail).<br>
<br>
初六：不永所事，小有言，终吉。<br>
雇佣：不延长所从事的，虽略有责言，最终吉祥。<br>
Employment: Do not extend the current engagement; despite some minor criticism, the final outcome will be auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：不克讼，归而逋，其邑人三百户，无眚。<br>
供给：不能供役，封邑的三百户惭愧而逃离，没有灾祸。<br>
Supply: Unable to fulfill service obligations, their three hundred households in their fiefdom fled in shame; no calamity ensued.<br>
<br>
六三：食旧德，贞厉，终吉。或从王事，无成。<br>
使富裕：亏蚀旧有的恩德，坚持有危险，最终吉祥；如果放纵过去的职守，没有收获。<br>
Vindication: If one risks losing the benefits previously gained, persisting may bring the danger, ultimately lead to a fortunate outcome; (conversely) if one neglects one’s established duties, nothing of value will be reaped.<br>
<br>
九四：不克讼，复即命渝，安，贞吉。<br>
通融：不能责备，宽宥（违法）就食，采用变通，(妥善)安置；守正则吉。<br>
Accommodation: Don't blame them, granting leniency for (illegally) getting meals, and adopting alternative methods, proper resettlement. Constancy brings good fortune.<br>
<br>
九五：讼元吉。<br>
功绩：赏赐，愿慕，吉。<br>
Achievement: Awarded, admired, good fortune.<br>
<br>
上九：或锡之鞶带，终朝三褫之。<br>
庸-劳苦：有时赐予大带，却在一日之内三次被夺去。<br>
Toil: At times, one is granted a ceremonial belt, yet within a single day it is taken away three times.<br>
