《师》<br>
军队 (Military) - 老师 (Mentor)<br>
<br>
贞丈人吉，无咎。<br>
翻译：坚守正道，任用贤明长者，吉利，没有过错。<br>
英文：Maintain uprightness, employ wise elders, auspicious, no blame.<br>
参考义项：军队 / 太师<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地中有水，《师》。君子以容民畜众。<br>
公-共同：大地中藏有水源，这就是师卦。君子用此革新百姓，培养兵众。<br>
Commonality: Water is stored within the earth; this is Shi. Therefore the noble one adopts this to renovate the people and cultivate the armed multitude.<br>
<br>
初六：师出以律，否臧凶。<br>
众多：军队出动要按照纪律，出言否定或赞同都凶险。<br>
Multitude / Informed: When the army goes forth, it must follow strict discipline; speaking to negate or affirm is dangerous.<br>
希腊文图式：eunomia<br>
英文直译：good order / law-abiding<br>
<br>
九二：在师中吉，无咎，王三锡命。<br>
率领：拥戴忠厚和善的统帅，没有过错，君王三次赐予诏命。<br>
Command / Reformer: Upholding a loyal and kind commander: no blame; the king grants edicts three times.<br>
希腊文图式：typos<br>
英文直译：pattern / example / mark<br>
<br>
<br>
六三：师或舆尸，凶。<br>
遵循：军队惑乱干预主持，凶险。<br>
Capitulation / Deformation: The army in tumult meddles with the helm; ominous.<br>
希腊文图式：spodas<br>
英文直译：riot / clash<br>
<br>
六四：师左次，无咎。<br>
尊崇：将领佐助趋奉，没有过错。<br>
Veneration / Conform: The commander flanked the left, attending with the utmost deference (of a disciple), there is no blame.<br>
希腊文图式：parembole<br>
英文直译：encampment<br>
<br>
六五：田有禽，利执言，无咎。长子帅师，弟子舆尸，贞凶。<br>
引导：田野中有擒获（俘虏），利于抓住而进行讯问，没有过错。长子统率军队，弟弟运载神主牌，保持这种状态会有凶险。<br>
Lead / Uniform: There are captives in the fields; it is favorable to seize and interrogate them, no blame. The eldest son leads the army with true command, the younger brother carries the spirit tablet; if this persists, there will be danger.<br>
希腊文图式：mimesis<br>
英文直译：imitation / emulation<br>
<br>
上六：大君有命，开国承家，小人勿用。<br>
表率：大君富有声名，开创拯救国与家，小人不要这样。<br>
Exemplar / Transform: The great ruler is highly prestigious: to found and save states and houses; petty people should not act like this.<br>
希腊文图式：hegemonia<br>
英文直译：leadership / supremacy / command<br>
