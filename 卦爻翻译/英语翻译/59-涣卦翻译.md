《涣》<br>
![[v2-7125f0cf6015e3d9129939537b9ade59~resize_0_q75.png]]<br>
奂：盛大 (Numerous) - 欢乐 (Amused)<br>
<br>
亨。王假有庙。利涉大川，利贞。<br>
通达。君王来到宗庙。适合涉过大河，适合守持正固。<br>
Accomplishment. The king comes to the ancestral temple. It is fitting to cross the great river, and fitting to hold fast to what is right.<br>
参考义项：（𡨡）宗庙祭礼。宗庙之中，先进行盛大仪式，再发扬传承精神。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行水上，《涣》。先王以享于帝，立庙。<br>
向-引导：风吹行在水面上，这就是涣卦。古代君王以此祭献上帝，建立宗庙。<br>
Guide: Wind moves across the surface of the water; this is Huan. Earlier kings adopted it to offer sacrifices to the High God and establish ancestral temples.<br>
<br>
初六：用拯马壮，吉。<br>
改换：用阉割的马，庄重，吉祥。<br>
Malleability / Reflux: Use a gelded horse, solemn and dignified; auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：涣奔其机，悔亡。<br>
炳焕：厌恶顺从其讥刺（璀璨华美的光明[^1]），悔恨消失。<br>
Illuminated / Efflux: Abhorring but complying with its mockery (brilliant and splendid radiance), regret vanishes.<br>
<br>
六三：涣其躬，无悔。<br>
忧患：心忧困穷而不能言，没有悔恨。<br>
Distress / Superflux: Worrying about hardship and poverty, unable to speak; there is no regret.<br>
<br>
六四：涣其群，元吉。涣有丘，匪夷所思。<br>
涣散：群众欢乐，玩乐，吉。用苟且使人欢乐，这不是能作为常态的快乐。<br>
Dispersion / Difflux: The multitude rejoices and makes merry; auspicious. Gladdening people through expediency is not a joy that can serve as the norm.<br>
<br>
九五：涣汗其大号，涣王居[^2]，无咎。<br>
呼唤：重要的号令涣畔猛悍，彪炳王的治理，没有过错。<br>
Calling / Afflux: An important proclamation spreading rampantly like floods and fiercely like droughts, it illuminates the king's governance; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
上九：涣其血，去逖出，无咎。<br>
欢-尽力：发散他的血，使忧惧离去而脱出，没有过错。<br>
Utmost Effort / Transflux: Disperse his blood, let dread depart and withdraw, and so break free; there is no blame.<br>
# 注疏<br>
<br>
[^1]: 取倒装句，机涣奔，机亦通几，是明的异体字；涣通焕，奔通贲，表示华美<br>
[^2]: 汗通旱，第二个涣通焕。<br>
