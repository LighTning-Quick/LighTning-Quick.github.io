《涣》<br>
![[v2-7125f0cf6015e3d9129939537b9ade59~resize_0_q75.png]]<br>
讙：喧哗 (Clamor) - 欢：融洽 (Harmony)<br>
<br>
亨。王假有庙。利涉大川，利贞。<br>
通达。君王借助于宗庙。适合涉过大河，适合守持正固。<br>
Accomplishment. The king relies on the ancestral temple (to gain power). It is fitting to cross the great river, and fitting to hold fast to what is right.<br>
参考义项：权威，由顺从喧嚣到引导融洽。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行水上，《涣》。先王以享于帝，立庙。<br>
向-引导：风吹行在水面上，这就是涣卦。古代君王以此祭献上帝，建立宗庙。<br>
Guide: Wind moves across the surface of the water; this is Huan. Earlier kings adopted it to offer sacrifices to the High God and establish ancestral temples.<br>
<br>
初六：用拯马壮，吉。<br>
改换：用阉割的马，庄重，吉祥。<br>
Alteration / Reflux: Use a gelded horse, solemn and dignified; auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：涣奔其机，悔亡。<br>
炳焕：厌恶顺从其讥刺（璀璨华美的光明[^1]），悔恨消失。<br>
Resplendent / Efflux: Abhorring but complying with its mockery (brilliant and splendid radiance), regret vanishes.<br>
<br>
六三：涣其躬，无悔。<br>
忧患：心忧困穷而不能言，没有悔恨。<br>
Distress / Superflux: Worrying about hardship and poverty, unable to speak; there is no regret.<br>
<br>
六四：涣其群，元吉。涣有丘，匪夷所思。<br>
涣散：群众欢乐，玩乐，吉。用苟且使人欢乐，这不是能作为常态的快乐。<br>
Dispersion / Difflux: The multitude rejoices and makes merry; auspicious. Gladdening people through expediency is not a joy that can serve as the norm.<br>
<br>
九五：涣汗其大号，涣王居，无咎。<br>
呼唤：改换或捍卫个别的号令，彪炳王的治理，没有过错。<br>
Calling / Afflux: Alter or defend a particular order, brilliantly illuminating the king's governance; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
上九：涣其血，去逖出，无咎。<br>
眷恋：回头看他的血亲，远远离开，流露，没有过错。<br>
Attachment / Transflux: Looking back at his blood kin, departing far away, letting the feelings flow forth; there is no blame.<br>

<br>
