《涣》<br>
![[v2-7125f0cf6015e3d9129939537b9ade59~resize_0_q75.png]]<br>
涣涣 (Gush) - 涣衍 (Spread)<br>
<br>
亨。王假有庙。利涉大川，利贞。<br>
通达。君王来到宗庙。适合涉过大河，适合守持正固。<br>
Accomplishment. The king comes to the ancestral temple. It is fitting to cross the great river, and fitting to hold fast to what is right.<br>
参考义项：（𡨡）宗庙祭礼。宗庙之中，先进行盛大仪式，再发扬传承精神。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行水上，《涣》。先王以享于帝，立庙。<br>
祭享：风吹行在水面上，这就是涣卦。古代君王以此祭享上帝，建立宗庙。<br>
Sacrificial Offering: Wind moves across the surface of the water; this is Huan. Earlier kings adopted it to offer sacrifices to the High God and establish ancestral temples.<br>
<br>
初六：用拯马壮，吉。<br>
改换：用阉割的马，强壮，吉祥。<br>
Malleability / Reflux: Use a gelded horse, strong and vigorous; auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：涣奔其机[^1]，悔亡。<br>
炳焕：璀璨华美的光明，悔恨消失。<br>
Illuminated / Efflux: Brilliant and splendid radiance appears, and regret vanishes.<br>
<br>
六三：涣其躬，无悔。<br>
畔换：彪炳自身，没有悔恨。<br>
Rampant / Superflux: One makes oneself illuminated, and there is no regret.<br>
<br>
六四：涣其群，元吉。涣有丘，匪夷所思。<br>
发散：种类鲜明，大吉。田垄离散，这不是平常人容易想到的。<br>
Dispersion / Difflux: Distinct group; greatly auspicious. The field ridges scatter; this is not something ordinary people would readily think of.<br>
<br>
九五：涣汗其大号，涣王居[^2]，无咎。<br>
呼唤：重要的号令涣畔猛悍，彪炳王的治理，没有过错。<br>
Calling / Afflux: An important proclamation spreading rampantly like floods and fiercely like droughts, it illuminates the king's governance; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
上九：涣其血，去逖出，无咎。<br>
涣泮：发散他的血，使忧惧离去而脱出，没有过错。<br>
Dissolution / Transflux: Disperse his blood, let dread depart and withdraw, and so break free; there is no blame.<br>
# 注疏<br>
<br>
[^1]: 倒装句，机涣奔，机通几，是明的异体字；涣通焕，奔通贲，表示华美<br>
[^2]: 汗通旱，第二个涣通焕。<br>
