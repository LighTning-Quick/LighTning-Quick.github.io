《大过》<br>
楇-收丝 (Spindle) - 輠-承膏 (Lubrication)<br>
<br>
栋桡，利有攸往，亨。<br>
天栋星闪耀。利于有所前往，亨通。<br>
The Arcturus is twinkling. It is favorable to have somewhere to go; there is success.<br>
参考义项：榾，用同滑，纷乱<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽灭木，《大过》。君子以独立不惧，遁世无闷。<br>
遁-放纵：泽水淹没树木，这就是大过。君子由此独立而不畏惧，退避世外而不苦闷。<br>
Wanton: The marsh submerges the tree; this is Da Guo. Thus the noble one stands alone without fear and retreats from the world without dejection.<br>
<br>
初六：藉用白茅，无咎。<br>
给予：用洁白的茅草铺垫承托，没有过错。<br>
Giving: Use white rushes as a mat and support. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：枯杨生稊，老夫得其女妻，无不利。<br>
过活：枯老的杨树生出嫩芽，年老的男子得到年轻女子为妻，无所不利。<br>
Survival: A withered poplar puts forth a young shoot; an old man takes a young woman as wife. Nothing is unfavorable.<br>
<br>
九三：栋桡，凶。<br>
祸害：屋梁挠曲，凶险。<br>
Harm: The ridgepole is bent under pressure. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
九四：栋隆，吉。有它，吝。<br>
转移：屋梁向上隆起，吉祥；长久驮负，憾惜。<br>
Transfer: The ridgepole arches upward. Auspicious. Bearing the load for too long brings regret.<br>
<br>
九五：枯杨生华，老妇得其士夫，无咎无誉。<br>
过分：枯老的杨树开花，年老的妇人得到年轻男子为夫；没有过错，也没有赞誉。<br>
Excess: A withered poplar puts forth flowers; an old woman takes a young man as husband. There is no blame and no praise.<br>
<br>
上六：过涉灭顶，凶。无咎。<br>
失去：涉水过深，水没头顶；凶险，但没有过错。<br>
Loss: One wades too far and the water rises over the head. Misfortune, but no blame.<br>
