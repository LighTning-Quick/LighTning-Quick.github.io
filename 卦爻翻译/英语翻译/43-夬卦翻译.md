《夬》<br>
抉择 (Strategic Choice) - 决断 (Definitive Judgment)<br>
<br>
扬于王庭，孚号。有厉，告自邑，不利即戎，利有攸往。<br>
举扬（弓箭）于王庭之上，（落点）符合记号。有危厉，从城邑求告，不宜立即动武。利于有所前往。<br>
Raise the bow in the royal court, with the arrow landing on its mark. There was danger, so people came from the city to plead for help. It is not favorable to resort to arms at once; it is favorable to have somewhere to go forward.<br>
参考义项：抉择/决断。校场选拔；开战决断。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽上于天，《夬》。君子以施禄及下，居德则忌。<br>
弛-舍弃：泽水上越于天，这是夬卦。君子因此散布俸禄给下民，而囤积所得则有所顾忌。<br>
Letting go: The marsh rises above heaven; this is Guai. The noble one extends benefits to those below, but hoarding virtue invites misgivings.<br>
<br>
初九：壮于前趾，往不胜，为咎。<br>
缺-废弛：撞到前趾，前往却不能胜任，咎过。<br>
Decrepitude: Hitting the forward toes; going forth without being qualified, brings blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："不胜"而"往"，"咎"也。<br>
"不胜"而"往"，是"咎"。      <br>
"Going forth without being able to prevail" is blame.<br>
<br>
九二：惕号莫夜，有戎勿恤。<br>
决-判断：寂静的夜里警惕呼号，有人相助（于兵戎）不要忧虑。<br>
Judgment: Be alert and call out in the quiet night. Someone will come to your aid, do not worry.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："有戎勿恤"，得中道也。 <br>
"有戎勿恤"，因为得守中道。<br>
"Someone will come to your aid, do not worry," because one holds to the middle way.<br>
<br>
九三：壮于頄，有凶。君子夬夬独行，遇雨若濡，有愠无咎。<br>
决裂：颧骨强壮，局限之，凶险。君子刚决独行，遇雨如沾湿，虽有愠怒但无咎过。<br>
Severance: Strong in the cheekbones, to confine them, there is danger. The noble one resolves resolutely and walks alone; encountering rain and becoming drenched, one feels resentment, yet there is no blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："君子夬夬"，终"无咎"也。<br>
"君子夬夬"，终将"无咎"。<br>
"The noble one resolves resolutely"; ultimately, there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九四：臀无肤，其行次且。牵羊悔亡。闻言不信。<br>
果决：臀部无肤，行走艰难，牵羊，悔恨消亡。听责言而不信服。<br>
Resoluteness: The buttocks are without flesh and walking is difficult. Lead the sheep and regret vanishes, yet reproachful words heard are not believed.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："其行次且"，位不当也。"闻言不信"，聪不明也。<br>
"其行次且"，位置不当。"闻言不信"，听力好但不明智。<br>
"Walking is difficult" because the position is improper. "Hearing words yet not believing" means one hears but does not understand.<br>
<br>
九五：苋陆夬夬，中行，无咎。<br>
决-断绝：细角山羊果决跳跃，衷心离开，无咎过。<br>
Break: Resolutely leaps the slender-horned goat, departing wholeheartedly; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："中行无咎"，中未光也。<br>
"中行无咎"，内心尚未广大。<br>
"No blame in the middle path," the mind is not yet broad.<br>
<br>
上六：无号，终有凶。<br>
诀别：不要哭嚎，朋友众多，凶险。<br>
Parting: Do not wail, (since you have) many friends, there is misfortune.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："无号"之"凶"，终不可长也。<br>
"无号"之"凶"，众人不可长久。<br>
The misfortune of "no wailing" is that the many cannot last forever.<br>
