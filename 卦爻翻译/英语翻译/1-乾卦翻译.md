《乾》<br>
旗杆 (Flagstaff) - 榦：井栏 (Well-curb)<br>
<br>
元亨，利贞。<br>
大亨通，利于守持正固。<br>
Great success. It is favorable to remain upright and steadfast.<br>
参考义项：日出，斗转/斡旋。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天行健，君子以自强不息。<br>
干_从事-图谋：天运行，这就是乾。君子通过开始劝勉来尊奉犒赏。<br>
Execute-Scheme: The heaven moves —this is *Qián* (the Creative). The noble one, through initializing exhortation, honors the bestowal.<br>
<br>
初九：潜龙，勿用。<br>
空乏：龙潜藏未出，此时不宜施用。<br>
Depletion / Subact: The dragon lies hidden; it is not yet the time to put it to use.<br>
<br>
九二：见龙在田，利见大人。<br>
招致：龙已出现在田野之上，利于见大人。<br>
incursion / Act: The dragon has appeared in the field. It is favorable to see the people in high positions.<br>
<br>
九三：君子终日乾乾，夕惕若。厉，无咎。<br>
榦-加强：君子整日勤勉奋发，到了夜晚仍警惕戒惧。砥砺，没有咎害。<br>
Strengthen / Enact: The noble one labors on vigorously all day and remains watchful into the night. Temper himself, no blame.<br>
<br>
九四：或跃在渊，无咎。<br>
干预：或可跃起于深渊，没有过错。<br>
Intervene / Transact: One may leap up from the deep. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：飞龙在天，利见大人。<br>
干求：龙升腾于天，利于见大人。<br>
Aspiration / Coact: The dragon soars in heaven. It is favorable to see the great person.<br>
<br>
上九：亢龙，有悔。<br>
捍卫：倔强的龙，有悔。<br>
Safeguarding / Overact: A stubborn dragon. There is regret.<br>
<br>
用九：见群龙无首，吉。<br>
通九 治理：向龙星进献，与其（时节）会和，不能变动，吉。<br>
All Nine (Governance): Offering sacrifices to the Dragon, in harmony with its (seasonal changes), should not be altered; This is auspicious.<br>
