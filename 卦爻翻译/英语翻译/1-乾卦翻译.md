《乾》<br>
键：鼎铉 (Tripod Carrying Bar) - 键：锁簧 (Locking Spring)<br>
<br>
元亨，利贞。<br>
（健壮不驯服）愚顽，亨通；（封建）利于守持正固。<br>
(Robust and untamed) Obstinate, prosperous; (in the establishment of a state) it is advantageous to maintain steadfastness and integrity.<br>
参考义项：健，健壮，勇猛；建，封立。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天行健，君子以自强不息。<br>
牵引-图谋：天运行，这就是乾。君子通过开始劝勉来尊奉犒赏。<br>
Draw-Scheme: The heaven moves —this is *Qián* (the Creative). The noble one, through initializing exhortation, honors the bestowal.<br>
<br>
初九：潜龙，勿用。<br>
键-闭藏：龙潜藏未出，此时不宜施用。<br>
Close / Subact: The dragon lies hidden; it is not yet the time to put it to use.<br>
<br>
九二：见龙在田，利见大人。<br>
引导：引荐龙星存问垦耕，利于见大人。<br>
Guide / Act: The dragon appears in the field. It is favorable to see the people in high positions.<br>
<br>
九三：君子终日乾乾，夕惕若厉，无咎。<br>
勌-劳累：君子整日敬慎，到了夜晚仍惕惧而砥砺，没有咎害。<br>
Industrious / Enact: The noble one remains respectful and cautious throughout the day, even at night, he stays vigilant and continues to refine himself; no blame.<br>
<br>
九四：或跃在渊，无咎。<br>
蜷曲：或许跃起于深渊，没有过错。<br>
Curled Up / Transact: One may leap up from the deep. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：飞龙在天，利见大人。<br>
引入：飞腾的龙运行于天穹，利于见大人。<br>
Bring In / Coact: The soaring dragon traverses the firmament; it is favorable to see the people in high positions.<br>
<br>
上九：亢龙，有悔。<br>
倾倒：迷乱的龙，有悔。<br>
Topple / Overact: An indulged dragon. There is regret.<br>
<br>
用九：见群龙无首，吉。<br>
通九 延续：进献、联合龙星，不要变动，吉。<br>
All Nine (Continuation): Presenting and aligning the Dragon (associated with different seasons); do not alter this arrangement. This is auspicious.<br>
