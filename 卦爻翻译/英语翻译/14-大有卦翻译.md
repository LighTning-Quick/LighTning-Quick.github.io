《大有》<br>
大囿 (Great Enclosure) - 大域 (Great Domain)<br>
<br>
元亨。<br>
大为通达。<br>
Great flourishing and free passage.<br>
参考义项：骖乘，强佑以尊崇。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：火在天上，大有。君子以遏恶扬善，顺天休命。<br>
休-荫庇：火在天上普照，这就是大有。君子用此抑止恶行，彰扬善行，顺应天道散布诰命。<br>
Shelter: Fire shines above heaven; this is Dayou. Therefore the noble one restrains what is harmful, exalts what is good, follows the way of heaven, and spread edicts.<br>
<br>
初九：无交害匪咎。艰则无咎。<br>
友-结交：不要骄傲妒忌；纷争比高，若有限度，则可免咎。<br>
Affiliation / Obtain: Do not be proud or envious; quarrelsome or competitive, if remain limited, then there is no blame.<br>
希腊文图式：philos<br>
英文直译：friend / beloved<br>
<br>
九二：大车以载，有攸往，无咎。<br>
占有：用大车来装载，有所前往，没有过错。<br>
Exhortation / Contain: A great wagon is used to bear the load; there is a place to go, and no blame.<br>
<br>
九三：公用亨于天子，小人弗克。<br>
侑-劝勉：天子宴请，公侯享用，小人则不能如此。<br>
Exhortation / Sustain: The Son of Heaven hosts the feast, and the lords partake of it; a petty person cannot do so.<br>
<br>
九四：匪其彭，无咎。<br>
佑助：防止其偏颇，没有过错。<br>
Assistance / Detain: To prevent its bias, and there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六五：厥孚交如，威如，吉。<br>
为-治理：使人信从，激发主动，又有威仪，故而吉祥。<br>
Governance / Maintain: One causes others to trust and follow, stirs their own initiative, and also bears dignity; therefore this is auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：自天祐之，吉，无不利。<br>
宽宥：即使上天都保佑他，吉祥，没有不利。<br>
Clemency / Retain: Help comes even from heaven. Auspicious, with nothing unfavorable.<br>
