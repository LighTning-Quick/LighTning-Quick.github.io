《小畜》<br>
小牲畜 (Flock) - 稍蕃息 (Gradual Reproduction)<br>
<br>
亨。密云不雨，自我西郊。<br>
亨通。浓云密布，却尚未降雨；云气从西郊而来。<br>
Prosperity. Dense clouds gather, yet no rain falls; they come from our western outskirts.<br>
参考义项：滀，水聚、郁结<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行天上，《小畜》。君子以懿文德。<br>
抑-强迫：风行于天上，这就是小畜。君子以美好来纹饰德行。<br>
Compulsion: Wind moves across heaven; this is Xiao Chu. Thus the noble one adorns conduct with beauty.<br>
<br>
初九：复自道，何其咎，吉。<br>
守-奉行：因引导而履行，哪里会有过错？吉利。<br>
Abiding by: Carry out under guidance—how could there be blame? Auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：牵复，吉。<br>
守护：牵引而返回，吉利。<br>
Guarding: Drawn back, it returns. Auspicious.<br>
<br>
九三：舆说輹，夫妻反目。<br>
守-请求：车舆脱落了輹木，夫妻彼此反目。<br>
Ask: The carriage loses its axle-brace; husband and wife turn against each other.<br>
<br>
六四：有孚，血去，惕出，无咎。<br>
治理：有帮助，血液散去，戒惧解除，没有过错。<br>
Treatment: It helps. The blood drains away and fear is released. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：有孚挛如，富以其邻。<br>
孝顺：使人信从，如彼此紧密相连，因其邻里富足。<br>
Filial Devotion: One wins people's trust, as though all were closely bound together, and becomes prosperous through the neighbors.<br>
<br>
上九：既雨既处，尚德载。妇贞厉。月几望，君子征凶。<br>
守持：已经降雨已经停，所承恩惠已满载。妇人坚持会有危险；月亮达到十五望月，君子若出征则凶。<br>
Uphold: The rain has fallen and stopped; the favor received is borne to the full. Persistence brings danger for the woman. The moon has reached its fifteenth-day fullness; if the noble one marches forth, misfortune follows.<br>
