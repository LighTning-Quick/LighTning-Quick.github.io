《小畜》
小牲畜 (Flock) - 稍蕃息 (Gradual Reproduction)

亨。密云不雨，自我西郊。
亨通。浓云密布，却尚未降雨；云气从西郊而来。
Prosperity. Dense clouds gather, yet no rain falls; they come from our western outskirts.
参考义项：滀，水聚、郁结

《象》曰：风行天上，《小畜》。君子以懿文德。
抑-强迫：风行于天上，这就是小畜。君子以美好来纹饰德行。
Compulsion: Wind moves across heaven; this is Xiao Chu. Thus the noble one adorns conduct with beauty.

初九：复自道，何其咎，吉。
治理：因疏导而恢复，哪里会有过错？吉利。
Management: Restoration through dredging—how could there be blame? Auspicious.

九二：牵复，吉。
畜牧：牵引而返回，吉利。
Herding: Drawn back, it returns. Auspicious.

九三：舆说輹，夫妻反目。
对待：车舆脱落了輹木，夫妻彼此反目。
Treat: The carriage loses its axle-brace; husband and wife turn against each other.

六四：有孚，血去，惕出，无咎。
制止：有帮助，血液散去，戒惧解除，没有过错。
Stop: It helps. The blood drains away and fear is released. There is no blame.

九五：有孚挛如，富以其邻。
孝顺：使人信从，如彼此紧密相连，因其邻里富足。
Filial Devotion: One wins people's trust, as though all were closely bound together, and becomes prosperous through the neighbors.

上九：既雨既处，尚德载。妇贞厉。月几望，君子征凶。
养活：已经降雨已经停，所承恩惠已满载。妇人坚持会有危险；月亮达到十五望月，君子若出征则凶。
Sustenance: The rain has fallen and stopped; the favor received is borne to the full. Persistence brings danger for the woman. The moon has reached its fifteenth-day fullness; if the noble one marches forth, misfortune follows.
