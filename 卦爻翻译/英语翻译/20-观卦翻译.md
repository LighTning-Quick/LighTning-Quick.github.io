《观》<br>
观阙 (Gate Tower) - 台榭 (Terrace-Spectacle)<br>
<br>
盥而不荐。有孚颙若。<br>
洗手灌祭而不献上牲牢。肃敬的样子使人信从。<br>
Wash hands for the libation but do not present the sacrificial offerings. Reverent demeanor inspires compliance.<br>
参考义项：灌祭（祼礼）<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行地上，《观》。先王以省方观民设教。<br>
旌-表明：风行在地上，这就是观卦。先王通过省视四方，来示范于民、施用教化。<br>
Indicate: The wind moves over the earth; this is Guan. The former kings would inspect the regions to set the example for the people and disseminate moral instruction.<br>
<br>
初六：童观，小人无咎，君子吝。<br>
劝-勤勉：劳作操持，小人没有错咎，君子有吝惜。<br>
Diligence: Labor and management; for the petty person there is no blame, but for the noble one there is regret.<br>
《象》曰："初六童观"，"小人"道也。<br>
《象》传说："初六劳作操持"，是小人的道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "The bottom six, labor and management" is the way of the petty person.<br>
<br>
六二：窥观，利女贞。<br>
观察：窥看，利于女子占卜。<br>
Observation: Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's divination.<br>
《象》曰："窥观女贞"，亦可丑也。<br>
《象》传说："窥观女贞"，也是可丑的。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's divination" can also be shameful.<br>
<br>
六三：观我，生进退。<br>
借鉴：观察我，反省进退。<br>
Reference: Observe myself, reflecting on advance and retreat (my actions).<br>
《象》曰："观我生进退"，未失道也。<br>
《象》传说："观察我，反省进退"，没有失去正道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe myself, reflecting on advance and retreat (my actions)" means not having lost the Way.<br>
<br>
六四：观国之光，利用宾于王。<br>
劝说：鼓励国家的宽宏，利于做君王的宾客。<br>
Persuade: Encourage the state's broad-mindedness; favorable to serve as a guest to the king.<br>
《象》曰："观国之光"，尚"宾"也。<br>
《象》传说："鼓励国家的宽宏"，举荐"宾客"。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Encourage the state's broad-mindedness" means recommending the "guest."<br>
<br>
九五：观我生，君子无咎。<br>
劝-勉励：借鉴我，赞许，君子没有错咎。<br>
Encouragement: Take me as a reference, giving approval; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观我生"，观民也。<br>
《象》传说："借鉴我"，是说勉励人民。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Taking me as a reference" means encouraging the people.<br>
<br>
上九：观其生，君子无咎。<br>
示范：劝说他省悟，君子没有错咎。<br>
Demonstration: Persuade him to awaken; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观其生"，志未平也。<br>
《象》传说："劝说他省悟"，是说志向尚未平定。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Persuade him to awaken" means the will is not yet settled.<br>
