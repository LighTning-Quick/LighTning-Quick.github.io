《观》<br>
观阙 (Gate Tower) - 台榭 (Terrace-Spectacle)<br>
<br>
盥而不荐。有孚颙若。<br>
洗手灌祭而不献上牲牢。肃敬的样子使人信从。<br>
Wash hands for the libation but do not present the sacrificial offerings. Reverent demeanor inspires compliance.<br>
参考义项：灌祭（祼礼）<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行地上，《观》。先王以省方观民设教。<br>
教授：风行在地上，这就是观卦。先王因此省视四方、观察民情，设置考校。<br>
Instruct: The wind moves over the earth; this is Guan. The former kings, accordingly, inspected the regions, observed the people, and established examines.<br>
<br>
初六：童观，小人无咎，君子吝。<br>
勤勉：劳作操持，小人没有错咎，君子有鄙吝。<br>
Diligence: Labor and management; for the petty person there is no blame, but for the noble one there is regret.<br>
《象》曰："初六童观"，"小人"道也。<br>
《象》传说："初六劳作操持"，是小人的道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "The bottom six, labor and management" is the way of the petty person.<br>
<br>
六二：窥观，利女贞。<br>
观察：窥看，利于女子占卜。<br>
Observation: Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's divination.<br>
《象》曰："窥观女贞"，亦可丑也。<br>
《象》传说："窥观女贞"，也是可丑的。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's divination" can also be shameful.<br>
<br>
六三：观我，生进退。<br>
借鉴/鉴戒：观察我，反省进退。<br>
Benchmark / Reflection: Observe myself; reflect on my actions.<br>
《象》曰："观我生进退"，未失道也。<br>
《象》传说："观察我，反省进退"，没有失去正道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe myself; reflect on my actions" means not having lost the Way.<br>
<br>
六四：观国之光，利用宾于王。<br>
辅助：鼓励国家的宽宏，利于做君王的宾客。<br>
Assistance: Encourage the state's broad-mindedness; favorable to serve as a guest to the king.<br>
《象》曰："观国之光"，尚"宾"也。<br>
《象》传说："鼓励国家的宽宏"，举荐"宾客"。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Encourage the state's broad-mindedness" means recommending the "guest."<br>
<br>
九五：观我生，君子无咎。<br>
示范：借鉴我，赞许，君子没有错咎。<br>
Exemplarity: Learn from me, approve; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观我生"，观民也。<br>
《象》传说："借鉴我"，是说勉励人民。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Learn from me" means encouraging the people.<br>
<br>
上九：观其生，君子无咎。<br>
劝导：辅助他省悟，君子没有错咎。<br>
Exhortation: Assist him to awaken; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观其生"，志未平也。<br>
《象》传说："辅助他省悟"，是说志向尚未平定。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Assist him to awaken" means the will is not yet content.<br>
