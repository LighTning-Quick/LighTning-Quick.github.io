《临》<br>
面对 (Confrontation) - 临视 (Oversight)<br>
<br>
元亨，利贞。至于八月有凶。<br>
大为通达，适宜正固。到了八月会有凶险。<br>
Originally abundant and smooth; favorable to persist in rectitude. Come the eighth month, there will be misfortune.<br>
参考义项：汛期（瀶，山谷水出）<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽上有地，《临》。君子以教思无穷，容保民无疆。<br>
思考：泽上有地，这就是临卦。君子因此竭力服务职守而不穷尽，劳苦安定百姓而不划分疆界。<br>
Consider: Above the marsh is the earth; this is Lin. The noble one, accordingly, exhausts himself in service without end, and labors to settle the people without delimiting borders.<br>
<br>
初九：咸临，贞吉。<br>
守卫：函守，贞正则吉祥。<br>
Guard: Contained; persevering brings auspiciousness.<br>
《象》曰："咸临贞吉"，志行正也。<br>
《象》传说："咸临贞吉"，是说志向践行端正。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Fully felt approach; persevering brings auspiciousness" means the will moves in rectitude.<br>
<br>
九二：咸临，吉，无不利。<br>
碰上/攻伐：共同、减伤，吉祥，没有什么不利。<br>
Encounter: Together, one reducing another; auspicious, nothing is disadvantageous.<br>
《象》曰："咸临吉无不利"，未顺命也。<br>
《象》传说："咸临吉无不利"，是说不顺从命运。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Fully felt approach; auspicious, nothing is disadvantageous" means not yet submissive to the command.<br>
<br>
六三：甘临，无攸利；既忧之，无咎。<br>
照耀：酣畅，无所利益；既然忧虑它，就没有错咎。<br>
Shine: Being uninhibited, brings no benefit; once one worries about it, there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："甘临"，位不当也。"既忧之"，"咎"不长也。<br>
《象》传说："甘临"，位置不恰当。"既忧之"，是说咎害不会长久。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Greedy approach" means the position is inappropriate. "Once one worries about it" means the "blame" will not endure.<br>
<br>
六四：至临，无咎。<br>
给予：给与（以）质询，没有错咎。<br>
Giving/Probing: Questioning; there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："至临无咎"，位当也。<br>
《象》传说："至临无咎"，位置恰当。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Direct approach; there is no blame" means the position is appropriate.<br>
<br>
六五：知临，大君之宜，吉。<br>
临摹：记录，大君的适宜，吉祥。<br>
Copying: Recording—appropriate for the great ruler; auspicious.<br>
《象》曰："大君之宜"，行中之谓也。<br>
《象》传说："大君之宜"，是说践行中道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Appropriate for the great ruler" means practicing the Mean.<br>
<br>
上六：敦临，吉，无咎。<br>
监理：敦促，吉祥，没有错咎。<br>
Supervision: Urging; auspicious, there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："敦临"之"吉"，志在内也。<br>
《象》传说："敦临之吉"，是说志向在内。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: The "auspiciousness" of "sincere approach" means the will is directed inward.<br>
