《泰》<br>
泰：安定 (Peace) - 操持 (Manage)<br>
<br>
小往大来，吉，亨。<br>
（望祭泰山时）小的往大者靠近，吉祥，亨通。<br>
When sacrificing toward Mount Tai, the small goes to approach the great. Auspicious. Prosperous.<br>
参考义项：望岱；允称。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天地交，泰。后以财成天地之道，辅相天地之宜，以左右民。<br>
主宰：天地相交，这就是泰。君主由此裁度成就天地之道，辅助天地之宜，以此扶助民众。<br>
Dominate: Heaven and earth commune; this is Tai. Thus the sovereign regulates and completes the way of heaven and earth, assists what is fitting to heaven and earth, and thereby supports the people.<br>
<br>
初九：拔茅茹以其汇。征吉。<br>
美好：拔起茅草消弭其彙虫。出征吉祥。<br>
Beautiful: Pulling up the thatch to eliminate its swarms of insects. Going forth is auspicious.<br>
<br>
九二：包荒，用冯河，不遐。遗朋，亡，得尚于中行。<br>
支持：瓠葫芦大，用来渡河，不要太远；馈赠朋友，失去了，能够帮助正确的事情。<br>
Support: The bottle gourd is large and is used to cross the river; do not go too far. A gift made to a friend is lost, yet one is able to help a right thing.<br>
<br>
九三：无平不陂，无往不复。艰贞无咎。勿恤其孚，于食有福。<br>
代-隐息：没有平地不转为倾斜，没有前往而不再返回的。处艰难而守正，无咎。不必忧虑付出，于亏蚀中有福。<br>
Disappear: No level ground fails to become sloping; nothing sent forth fails to return. Remaining steadfast amid hardship brings no blame. Do not worry about what is given; there is blessing amid depletion.<br>
<br>
六四：翩翩，不富以其邻，不戒以孚。<br>
奢侈：欣喜自得，不使财富惠及邻里；不要凭借以相合。<br>
Opulence: Delighting in self-satisfaction, one does not extend wealth to the neighbors and; Do not rely on luxury to fit in.<br>
<br>
六五：帝乙归妹，以祉元吉。<br>
大-重视：帝乙嫁出妹妹，获得福祉，大吉。<br>
Esteem: Di Yi gives his younger sister in marriage. Blessing is thereby obtained. Supreme good fortune.<br>
<br>
上六：城复于隍，勿用师，自邑告，命。贞吝。<br>
骄纵：城墙覆盖城壕，迅速动用军队；从本邑求告，轻慢。固守则有憾惜。<br>
Arrogance: The city wall covers the moat, to swiftly deploy the army; appeal from one's own town, showing contempt. Holding fast brings regret.<br>
