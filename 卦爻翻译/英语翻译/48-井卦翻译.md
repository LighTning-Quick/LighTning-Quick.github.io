《井》
旁侧 (Sideways) - 傍：靠近 (Drawing Near)

改邑不改井，无丧无得。往来井井。汔至，亦未繘井，羸其瓶，凶。
（旁系）改变城邑而不改祠堂，不用服丧，不受恩德。（彼此）往来驰驱不息。
（靠近）干涸窒塞，整治疏通。未把井绳下到井中，反而毁坏了水瓶，凶。
(Distant Relative) The city is changed, but not the ancestral shrine; not required to observe mourning and do not receive favors. People come and go without cease.
(Well Repair) Dryness and obstruction, dredge it. The well rope has not yet been lowered when the water jar is broken—misfortune.
参考义项：旁系/修井；横生枝节，靠近中央。

《象》曰：木上有水，《井》。君子以劳民劝相。
同僚：木上有水，这是井卦。君子因此慰劳百姓，劝导彼此辅助。
Colleagueship: Water above wood; this is Jing. The noble one express his appreciation to the people (in their labor) and urges them to help one another.

初六：井泥不食。旧井无禽。
摒弃：井被泥阻塞，不能饮水。旧陷阱荒废，没有擒获。
Abandonment: The well is blocked by mud and cannot be used for drinking. The old pitfall is abandoned and captures nothing.

《象》曰：“井泥不食”，下也。“旧井无禽”，时舍也。
“井泥不食”，是因处在最下。“旧井无禽”，是时势已舍弃它。
"The muddy well is not drunk from" because it lies below. "The old well has no birds or game" because the times have cast it aside.

九二：井谷射鲋，瓮敝漏。
方-占据：井谷之中弹出蛤蟆，水瓮破敝而漏。
Occupation: In the well hollow, a frog springs out; the earthen jar is worn out and leaks.

《象》曰：“井谷射鲋”，无与也。
“井谷射鲋”，是因为无人相与。
"In the well hollow, a frog springs out" means there is no one to join with.

九三：井渫不食，为我心恻。可用汲，王明并受其福。
彷徨：井已疏通洁净，却仍无人取食，使我心中忧恻。若可用来汲取，王者结盟，则一同受其福。
Wandering: The well has been cleared, yet no one drinks from it; this grieves my heart. If it can be used for drawing water and the king forms an alliance, all will share in its blessing.

《象》曰：“井渫不食”，行“恻”也。求“王明”，“受福”也。
“井渫不食”，所以行而有忧恻。求得“王明”，则能“受福”。
"The cleared well is not drunk from" means one goes on in sorrow. Seeking "the king's alliance" means receiving blessing.

六四：井甃，无咎。
屏-捍蔽：修砌井壁，无咎。
Shielding / Armor: Get the well lined and repaired. No blame.

《象》曰：“井甃无咎”，修井也。
“井甃无咎”，是修治水井。
"Lining the well; no blame" means repairing the well.

九五：井洌寒泉，食。
发放：清冽如寒泉，饮用。
Distributing / Effluence: The well is clear, its cold spring fit for drinking.

《象》曰：“寒泉”之“食”，中正也。
“寒泉”可以饮用，是因为中正。
That the "cold spring" is fit for drinking is due to central correctness.

上六：井收勿幕，有孚元吉。
释放：井纠合绳索，不要遮盖，付与，大吉。
Release: Gathers the rope of the well together and do not cover it. There is trust; great good fortune.

《象》曰：“元吉”在“上”，大成也。
“元吉”在上位，是大成之象。
"Great good fortune" at the top means great completion.
