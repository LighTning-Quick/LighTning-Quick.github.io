《井》
房：箭筒 (The Quiver) - 水井 (The Water-Well)

改邑不改井，无丧无得。往来井井。汔至，亦未繘井，羸其瓶，凶。
迁改城邑而不改水井，无所丧失，也无所得益。往来之人井井有条。几乎到达，却还未把井绳下到井中，反而毁坏了水瓶，凶。
The settlement may be changed, but the well is not changed. There is no loss and no gain. Those who come and go remain ordered. Almost arriving, yet the rope has not reached into the well; the jar is damaged. Misfortune.
参考义项：迁邑，汲水、打井；可被汲取的给养作为开放的资源。

《象》曰：木上有水，《井》。君子以劳民劝相。
同僚：木上有水，这是井卦。君子因此劳勉百姓，劝导彼此辅助。
Colleagueship: Water above wood; this is Jing. The noble one labors with the people and encourages mutual assistance.

初六：井泥不食。旧井无禽。
摒弃：井被泥阻塞，不能饮水。旧陷阱荒废，没有擒获。
Abandonment: The well is blocked by mud and cannot be used for drinking. The old pitfall is abandoned and captures nothing.

《象》曰：“井泥不食”，下也。“旧井无禽”，时舍也。
“井泥不食”，是因处在最下。“旧井无禽”，是时势已舍弃它。
"The muddy well is not eaten from" because it is below. "The old well has no birds or game" because the time has cast it aside.

九二：井谷射鲋，瓮敝漏。
占据：井谷之中弹出蛤蟆，水瓮破敝而漏。
Occupation: In the well valley a frog springs out; the earthen jar is broken and leaks.

《象》曰：“井谷射鲋”，无与也。
“井谷射鲋”，是因为无人相与。
"Shooting small fish in the well valley" means there is no one to join with.

九三：井渫不食，为我心恻。可用汲，王明并受其福。
彷徨：井已疏通洁净，却仍无人取食，使我心中忧恻。若可用来汲取，王者结盟，则一同受其福。
Hessitation: The well has been cleared, yet no one drinks from it; this makes my heart ache. If it can be used for drawing water and the king forms an alliance, all will receive its blessing together.

《象》曰：“井渫不食”，行“恻”也。求“王明”，“受福”也。
“井渫不食”，所以行而有忧恻。求得“王明”，则能“受福”。
"The cleared well is not eaten from" means one proceeds with sorrow. Seeking "the king's alliance" means receiving blessing.

六四：井甃，无咎。
捍蔽：修砌井壁，无咎。
Shielding / Armor: Get the well lined and repaired. No blame.

《象》曰：“井甃无咎”，修井也。
“井甃无咎”，是修治水井。
"Get the well lined , no blame" means repairing the well.

九五：井洌寒泉，食。
释放：清冽如寒泉，饮用。
Release / Effluence: Clear and cold like a spring, adopted for drinking.

《象》曰：“寒泉”之“食”，中正也。
“寒泉”可以饮用，是因为中正。
That the "cold spring" can be drunk from is due to central correctness.

上六：井收勿幕，有孚元吉。
市井：井纠合绳索，不要遮盖，付与，大吉。
The Commons: The well gathers the rope together; do not cover it. Entrust and give it over. Great good fortune.

《象》曰：“元吉”在“上”，大成也。
“元吉”在上位，是大成之象。
"Great good fortune" at the top means great completion.
