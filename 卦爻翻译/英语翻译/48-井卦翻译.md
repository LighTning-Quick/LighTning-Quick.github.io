《井》<br>
旁侧 (Sideways) - 傍：靠近 (Drawing Near)<br>
<br>
改邑不改井，无丧无得。往来井井。汔至，亦未繘井，羸其瓶，凶。<br>
（旁系）改变城邑而不改祠堂，不用服丧，不受恩德。（彼此）往来驰驱不息。<br>
（靠近）干涸窒塞，整治疏通。未把井绳下到井中，反而毁坏了水瓶，凶。<br>
(Distant Relative) The city is changed, but not the ancestral shrine; not required to observe mourning and do not receive favors. People come and go without cease.<br>
(Well Repair) Dryness and obstruction, dredge it. The well rope has not yet been lowered when the water jar is broken—misfortune.<br>
参考义项：旁系/修井；横生枝节，靠近中央。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：木上有水，《井》。君子以劳民劝相。<br>
同僚：木上有水，这是井卦。君子因此慰劳百姓，劝导彼此辅助。<br>
Colleagueship: Water above wood; this is Jing. The noble one express his appreciation to the people (in their labor) and urges them to help one another.<br>
<br>
初六：井泥不食。旧井无禽。<br>
摒弃：井被泥阻塞，不能饮水。旧陷阱荒废，没有擒获。<br>
Abandonment: The well is blocked by mud and cannot be used for drinking. The old pitfall is abandoned and captures nothing.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“井泥不食”，下也。“旧井无禽”，时舍也。<br>
“井泥不食”，是因处在最下。“旧井无禽”，是时势已舍弃它。<br>
"The muddy well is not drunk from" because it lies below. "The old well has no birds or game" because the times have cast it aside.<br>
<br>
九二：井谷射鲋，瓮敝漏。<br>
方-占据：井谷之中弹出蛤蟆，水瓮破敝而漏。<br>
Occupation: In the well hollow, a frog springs out; the earthen jar is worn out and leaks.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“井谷射鲋”，无与也。<br>
“井谷射鲋”，是因为无人相与。<br>
"In the well hollow, a frog springs out" means there is no one to join with.<br>
<br>
九三：井渫不食，为我心恻。可用汲，王明并受其福。<br>
彷徨：井已疏通洁净，却仍无人取食，使我心中忧恻。若可用来汲取，王者结盟，则一同受其福。<br>
Wandering: The well has been cleared, yet no one drinks from it; this grieves my heart. If it can be used for drawing water and the king forms an alliance, all will share in its blessing.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“井渫不食”，行“恻”也。求“王明”，“受福”也。<br>
“井渫不食”，所以行而有忧恻。求得“王明”，则能“受福”。<br>
"The cleared well is not drunk from" means one goes on in sorrow. Seeking "the king's alliance" means receiving blessing.<br>
<br>
六四：井甃，无咎。<br>
屏-捍蔽：修砌井壁，无咎。<br>
Shielding / Armor: Get the well lined and repaired. No blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“井甃无咎”，修井也。<br>
“井甃无咎”，是修治水井。<br>
"Lining the well; no blame" means repairing the well.<br>
<br>
九五：井洌寒泉，食。<br>
发放：清冽如寒泉，饮用。<br>
Distributing / Effluence: The well is clear, its cold spring fit for drinking.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“寒泉”之“食”，中正也。<br>
“寒泉”可以饮用，是因为中正。<br>
That the "cold spring" is fit for drinking is due to central correctness.<br>
<br>
上六：井收勿幕，有孚元吉。<br>
释放：井纠合绳索，不要遮盖，付与，大吉。<br>
Release: Gathers the rope of the well together and do not cover it. There is trust; great good fortune.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“元吉”在“上”，大成也。<br>
“元吉”在上位，是大成之象。<br>
"Great good fortune" at the top means great completion.<br>
