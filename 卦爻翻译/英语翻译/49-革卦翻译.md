《革》<br>
鼓鞀 (Percussion) - 酒囊 (Wineskin)<br>
<br>
革：巳日乃孚。元亨，利贞，悔亡。<br>
革：到了上巳节就泛舟浮于水上。大为亨通，宜于守正，悔恨消亡。<br>
Ge: On the Double Third Festival one floats upon the water with a boat. Great success; favorable to persist in the right; regret vanishes.<br>
参考义项：祓除畔浴，洗濯去垢，消除不祥。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽中有火，《革》。君子以治历明时。<br>
辞别：泽中有火，这是革卦。君子因此治定历法，明晓时令。<br>
Farewell: Fire within the marsh; this is Ge. The noble one therefore orders the calendar and makes the seasons clear.<br>
<br>
初九：巩用黄牛之革。<br>
戒备：用黄牛皮革捆缚牢固。<br>
Vigilance: Bound fast with the hide of a yellow ox.<br>
<br>
六二：巳日乃革之，征吉，无咎。<br>
整饬：到了巳日才革除它，前往吉祥，没有咎害。<br>
Rectification: Only when the Double Third Festival arrives does one cast it off; going forward is auspicious—no blame.<br>
<br>
九三：征凶。贞厉。革言三就有孚。<br>
革除：征行凶险，守贞危厉。急迫地绕三匝（靳），令人信孚。<br>
Removal: Marching is dangerous; persistence is perilous. Urgently bound in three coils (the jin-strap), it compels trust.<br>
<br>
九四：悔亡。有孚改命吉。<br>
改换：悔恨消亡。有孚信而改换爵命礼数，吉祥。<br>
Replacement: Regret vanishes. With trust, rank-conferral and ritual forms are changed—good fortune.<br>
<br>
九五：大人虎变，未占有孚。<br>
革职：大人号令班师，未经验证就使人信孚。<br>
Dismissal: The great person issues the call to bring the troops home; unverified, it yet wins trust.<br>
<br>
上六：君子豹变，小人革面，征凶，居贞吉。<br>
亟-敬爱：君子乘戴豹皮帽的马，小人改换颜面；征行凶险，治理，守正吉祥。<br>
Reverence: The noble one rides a horse capped with the leopard-skin crest; petty people change their countenances. To march is dangerous; to govern, holding to what is right is auspicious.<br>
