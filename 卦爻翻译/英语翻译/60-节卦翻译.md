《节》<br>
竹节 (Bamboo-Node) - 符节 (Tally / Credential)<br>
<br>
亨。苦节，不可贞。<br>
通达。病痛结节，就不可以守为常道。<br>
Success. A painful knotting sickness cannot be kept as a constant norm.<br>
参考义项：疖子（小疮）。节不是一味收紧，而是立界、分流、适度。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽上有水，《节》。君子以制数度，议德行。<br>
节制、适宜：泽上蓄水，这就是节卦。君子因此制定数目度量，审议德行的边界。<br>
Regulation and Fitness: Water stands stored above the marsh; this is Jie. Therefore the noble one sets numerical measures and deliberates the boundaries of conduct.<br>
<br>
初九：不出户庭，无咎。<br>
节度：不走出户庭，没有过错。<br>
Regulation / Stricture: Do not go out of the household court; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：不出门庭，凶。<br>
分明：不走出门庭，就有凶险。<br>
Demarcation / Restrict: If one does not go out of the gate-court, there is danger.<br>
<br>
六三：不节若，则嗟若，无咎。<br>
管束：若不能适度，就会因此嗟叹。还不至于有咎。<br>
Restraint / Constrict: If one cannot keep moderation, one will sigh in lament; it still does not amount to blame.<br>
<br>
六四：安节，亨。<br>
适度：安适之节，所以通达。<br>
Moderation / Strictness: Ease within due measure, and therefore there is success.<br>
<br>
九五：甘节，吉，往有尚。<br>
证验：甘美而合宜的节，吉祥；前往会受到推重。<br>
Verification / Instrict: Sweet and fitting due measure is auspicious; going forward will bring honor.<br>
<br>
上六：苦节，贞凶。悔亡。<br>
操守：竭力的自我压迫，固守下去就有凶险；但悔恨可亡。<br>
Integrity / Overstrict: Strenuous self-oppression, persisting in it brings danger; yet regret can disappear.<br>
