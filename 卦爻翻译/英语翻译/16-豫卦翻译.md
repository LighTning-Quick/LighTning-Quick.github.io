《豫》<br>
居：治理 (Manage) - 举：攻克 (Occupy)<br>
<br>
利建侯行师。<br>
适宜（预先）建立诸侯、出动军队。<br>
It is favorable to establish lords and mobilize the army (in advance).<br>
参考义项：预先。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：雷出地奋，《豫》。先王以作乐崇德，殷荐之上帝，以配祖考。<br>
前进：雷迸出而振奋大地，这就是豫。先王因此制作音乐、尊崇德行，隆重进献于上帝，并配祭祖先。<br>
Advance: Thunder bursts forth from the earth and rocks it; this is Yu. Accordingly, the former kings made music to exalt virtue, offered it solemnly to the Supreme Deity, and joined their ancestors in the rite.<br>
<br>
初六：鸣豫，凶。<br>
欲望：萌生欲望，凶险。<br>
Desire: Desire begins to arise. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
六二：介于石，不终日，贞吉。<br>
与-等待：耿介如磐石，不用一整天，守正则吉祥。<br>
Waiting: Upright and unyielding as a rock, one does not need the whole day. Perseverance in rectitude is auspicious.<br>
<br>
六三：盱豫，悔，迟有悔。<br>
预备：仰视而喜悦，晦昧迟钝有悔恨。<br>
Preparation: Looking upward in delight; obscurity and dull delay bring regret.<br>
<br>
九四：由豫，大有得；勿疑，朋盍簪。<br>
与-亲附：想要有所成就，需要朋友投契；不要迟疑，朋友联合而亲近。<br>
Affiliation: To accomplish something, you need like-minded friends; don't hesitate, unite with your friends and grow closer.<br>
<br>
六五：贞疾，恒不死。<br>
举-纠正：承受非难，有恒心而不死板。<br>
Correction: Endure reproach; remain constant without becoming rigid.<br>
<br>
上六：冥豫，成有渝，无咎。<br>
倨傲：瞑目，以安定使人专诚，没有过错。<br>
Arrogance: Closing one's eyes, using peace and stability to make people devoted; there is no blame.<br>
