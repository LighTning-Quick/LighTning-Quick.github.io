《否》<br>
痞肿 (Lump) - 不：花萼 (Calyx)<br>
<br>
否之匪人。不利君子贞。大往小来。<br>
批评的不是（对的）人。不利于君子守持正固。（犯軷仪式）大者往小者靠近。<br>
The criticism is directed at the wrong person. It is unfavorable for the noble one to remain steadfast. In the rite of crossing the road sacrifice, the great draws near the small.<br>
参考义项：错批；犯軷。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天地不交，否。君子以俭德辟难，不可荣以禄。<br>
弭-安定：天地不相交，这就是否。君子由此约束恩惠以避开灾难，不可用俸禄使自己显荣。<br>
Subjugate: Heaven and earth do not commune; this is Pi. Thus the noble one restrains beneficence to avoid calamity and must not seek distinction through emolument.<br>
<br>
初六：拔茅茹，以其汇。贞吉，亨。<br>
贪吝：拔起茅草使其聚集。守正吉祥，亨通。<br>
Avarice: Pulling up cogon grass causes it to gather together. Remaining steadfast is auspicious. Prosperous.<br>
<br>
六二：包承，小人吉，大人否。亨。<br>
困厄：聚集欺凌，小人得吉，大人退处于野；亨通。<br>
Difficulty: Gathering to bully others, the petty person benefits, while the great person retreats to the wilderness; Prosperous.<br>
<br>
六三：包羞。<br>
鄙-羞耻：包取而羞愧。<br>
Shame: Shame of encompassing.<br>
<br>
九四：有命，无咎，畴离祉。<br>
丕-弘大：获得爵命，没有过错；筹划罗布福祉。<br>
Greatness—Vastness: One receives a noble mandate. No blame. One plans and spreads blessings.<br>
<br>
九五：休否，大人吉。其亡其亡，系于苞桑。<br>
尊奉：庇护，大人吉祥。“千万不要，千万不要”，系结在花苞桑叶上。<br>
Veneration: To shelter and protect. Auspicious for the great person. Never, never, tie it to flower buds or mulberry leaves.<br>
<br>
上九：倾否，先否后喜。<br>
邪僻：倾斜，先奉行，后嬉笑。<br>
Deviant: Inclined, first obeying, then laughing.<br>
