《益》<br>
臆：胸骨 (Sternum) - 翳：华盖 (Canopy)<br>
<br>
《益》：利有攸往。利涉大川。<br>
利于有所前往。利于涉越大河。<br>
Increase. It is fitting to go somewhere; it is fitting to cross the great river.<br>
参考义项：鹢，賹首，舟也。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风雷，《益》。君子以见善则迁，有过则改。<br>
友-顺从：风雷相益，这就是益卦。君子因此见善就迁而从之，有过就改正。<br>
Submissive: Wind and thunder, this is Yi. Therefore the noble one, on seeing what is good, moves toward it; on seeing his faults, he amends them.<br>
<br>
初九：利用为大作，元吉，无咎。<br>
隘-限制：利于用来（限制）强迫长时间劳作，谨慎老实，吉，没有过错。<br>
Restriction: It is fitting to use it to (restrict) compel long toil; prudent and honest—auspicious, no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：或益之十朋之龟，弗克违。永贞吉。王用享于帝，吉。[^1]<br>
增益：有人进献价值十朋的大宝龟，无法辞谢。长久守正吉祥。君王以此祭享上帝，吉祥。<br>
Augmentation: Someone presents him with a great tortoise worth ten strings of cowries; it cannot be declined. Lasting perseverance brings good fortune. The king offers it in sacrifice to the High God—auspicious.<br>
<br>
六三：益之用凶事，无咎，有孚。中行告公用圭。[^2]<br>
翳-隐匿：限制之，用于凶荒之事（不要瞒报），没有过错，秉持诚信。中行手执玉圭禀告公（以掩面）。<br>
Concealment: Restrict it, applying it to relief in calamity (do not conceal reports); there is no blame, holding fast to sincerity. In mid-course, jade tablet in hand, he reports to the prince (so as to veil his face).<br>
<br>
六四：中行告公，从，利用为依迁国。<br>
助益：中行禀告公，公听从，利于用来依此迁徙国都。<br>
Aid: He reports to the prince in mid-course, and the prince complies; it is fitting to rely on this to relocate the state.<br>
<br>
九五：有孚惠心，勿问，元吉。有孚，惠我德。[^3]<br>
慎（使顺笃）：有赐予，施惠关心，不必诘问，如愿，吉。使人信服，民众惠顺我的德泽。<br>
Graciousness: With bestowal, extend favor and care; no need to interrogate—in accord with wishes, good fortune. With convincing sincerity, the people graciously accord with my virtue.<br>
<br>
上九：莫益之，或击之，立心勿恒，凶。<br>
骄溢：没有人阻止他，就有人攻击他；立心疏忽固定，凶险。<br>
Pride-Overflow: No one restrains him, so someone attacks him; his resolve is fixed with heedlessness—danger.<br>
