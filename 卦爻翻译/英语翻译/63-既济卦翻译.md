《既济》
既䍤-过滤 (Already Filtered) - 确定成功 (Ensured Success)

既济：亨。小利贞。初吉终乱。
既济：亨通，小心，宜于守正。在吉日朔日（斋戒），终将治理。
all proceeds. Careful, persistence is favorable. (Fasting) on the auspicious new moon, eventually bringing order.
参考义项：斋戒。

《象》曰：水在火上，《既济》。君子以思患而豫防之。
方正：水在火上，既济。君子由此伺察隐患并预先防堵。
Upright: Water rests upon fire; this is Settlement. The superior person, contemplating this, watches for potential calamities and guards against them in advance.

初九：曳其轮，濡其尾，无咎。
酌量：牵拉车牙，煣屈边缘，没有灾咎。
Tempering: Pull the wheel rim, bend the edge (with fire); no blame.

六二：妇丧其茀，勿逐，七日得。
听凭：妇人失去了车上的帷障，不必追赶，七日后自然会得回。
Permit: The woman loses her carriage curtain; do not pursue it—within seven days it will be restored.

九三：高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。
渡过：高宗征伐鬼方，三年才战胜，小人不可用此。
Overcome: King Wuding attacked the Ghost Regions; three years were required to conquer them. Petty persons should not employ this.

六四：有衣袽，终日戒。
忧愁：寒衣有破旧的衣絮，整日警戒。
Anxiety: Winter garments have worn-out cloth rags; vigilant all day.

九五：东邻杀牛，不如西邻之禴祭，实受其福。
斋-庄敬：东邻杀牛（盛祭），不如西邻的（简约）禴祭，真正受福。
Solemnity: The eastern neighbor slaughters an ox (grand sacrifice), yet it is not as good as the western neighbor's (simple) spring offering, which truly receives blessing.

上六：濡其首，厉。
排挤：濡滞其蹈履，危险。
Ostracizing: Clog their tread; danger.
