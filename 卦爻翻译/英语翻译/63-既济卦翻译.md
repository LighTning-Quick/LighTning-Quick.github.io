《既济》<br>
既䍤-过滤 (Already Filtered) - 确定成功 (Ensured Success)<br>
<br>
既济：亨。小利贞。初吉终乱。<br>
既济：亨通，小心，宜于守正。在吉日朔日（斋戒），终将治理。<br>
all proceeds. Careful, persistence is favorable. (Fasting) on the auspicious new moon, eventually bringing order.<br>
参考义项：斋戒。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：水在火上，《既济》。君子以思患而豫防之。<br>
方正：水在火上，既济。君子由此伺察隐患并预先防堵。<br>
Upright: Water rests upon fire; this is Settlement. The superior person, contemplating this, watches for potential calamities and guards against them in advance.<br>
<br>
初九：曳其轮，濡其尾，无咎。<br>
酌量：牵拉车牙，煣屈边缘，没有错咎。<br>
Tempering: Pull the wheel rim, bend the edge (with fire); no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：妇丧其茀，勿逐，七日得。<br>
听凭：妇人失去了车上的帷障，不必追赶，七日后自然会得回。<br>
Permit: The woman loses her carriage curtain; do not pursue it—within seven days it will be restored.<br>
<br>
九三：高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。<br>
渡过：高宗征伐鬼方，三年才战胜，小人不可用此。<br>
Overcome: King Wuding attacked the Ghost Regions; three years were required to conquer them. Petty persons should not employ this.<br>
<br>
六四：有衣袽，终日戒。<br>
忧愁：寒衣有破旧的衣絮，整日警戒。<br>
Anxiety: Winter garments have worn-out cloth rags; vigilant all day.<br>
<br>
九五：东邻杀牛，不如西邻之禴祭，实受其福。<br>
斋-庄敬：东邻杀牛（盛祭），不如西邻的（简约）禴祭，最终受福。<br>
Solemnity: The eastern neighbor slaughters an ox (grand sacrifice), yet it is not as good as the western neighbor's (simple) spring offering, which finally receives blessing.<br>
<br>
上六：濡其首，厉。<br>
排挤：濡滞其蹈履，危险。<br>
Ostracizing: Clog their tread; danger.<br>
