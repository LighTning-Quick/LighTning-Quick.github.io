《谦》
咁乳 (Nurture / Suckling) - 嗛：颊囊 (Pouch / Storage)

亨。君子有终。
通达。君子能够成事。
There is free passage. The noble one is able to accomplish undertakings.
参考义项：兼，尽。

《象》曰：地中有山，《谦》。君子以裒多益寡，称物平施。
平-治理：山藏在地中，这就是谦。君子因此聚取多余、补益不足，衡量事物而评价施用。
Settle: A mountain lies within the earth; this is Qian. Accordingly, the noble one gathers from who has too much, adds to who has too little, weighs things, and assesses how they are to be employed.

初六：谦谦君子，用涉大川，吉。
谦卑：小小君子，渡过大河，吉祥。
Humility: A humble and unassuming noble one crosses the great river. Auspicious.

六二：鸣谦，贞吉。
慊-满足：有收成而满足，守正则吉祥。
Contentment: The harvest brings contentment. Perseverance is auspicious.

九三：劳谦君子，有终，吉。
兼-吞并：捞取兼并，君子佑助众人，吉祥。
Integration: Reaping and annexing, the noble one supports the multitude. Auspicious.

六四：无不利，撝谦。
减损：没有不利，指挥而行谦退。
Reduction: Nothing is unfavorable. Exercise command to humbly retreat. 

六五：不富以其邻，利用侵伐，无不利。
歉意：不相副使其惭愧，适宜征伐，没有不利。
Remorse: Failing to match another causes shame. It is favorable to wage a campaign. Nothing is unfavorable.

上六：鸣谦，利用行师征邑国。
慊恨：慊恨而勉强，适宜出动军队，征讨封邑之国。
Resentment: Pressing on cause of discontent and resentment, it is favorable to mobilize the army and campaign against the domain-state.
