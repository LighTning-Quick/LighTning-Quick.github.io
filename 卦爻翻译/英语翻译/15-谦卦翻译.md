《谦》<br>
咁乳 (Nurture / Suckling) - 嗛：颊囊 (Pouch / Storage)<br>
<br>
亨。君子有终。<br>
通达。君子能够成事。<br>
There is free passage. The noble one is able to accomplish undertakings.<br>
参考义项：兼，尽。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地中有山，《谦》。君子以裒多益寡，称物平施。<br>
平-治理：山藏在地中，这就是谦。君子因此聚取多余、补益不足，衡量事物而评价施用。<br>
Settle: A mountain lies within the earth; this is Qian. Accordingly, the noble one gathers from who has too much, adds to who has too little, weighs things, and assesses how they are to be employed.<br>
<br>
初六：谦谦君子，用涉大川，吉。<br>
谦卑：小小君子，渡过大河，吉祥。<br>
Humility: A humble and unassuming noble one crosses a great river. Auspicious.<br>
<br>
六二：鸣谦，贞吉。<br>
慊-满足：有收成而满足，守正则吉祥。<br>
Contentment: The harvest brings contentment. Perseverance is auspicious.<br>
<br>
九三：劳谦君子，有终，吉。<br>
兼-吞并：捞取兼并，君子佑助众人，吉祥。<br>
Integration: Reaping and annexing, the noble one supports the multitude. Auspicious.<br>
<br>
六四：无不利，撝谦。<br>
减损：没有不利，指挥谦退。<br>
Reduction: There are no disadvantages. Exercise command to humbly retreat. <br>
<br>
六五：不富以其邻，利用侵伐，无不利。<br>
歉意：不相副使其惭愧，适宜征伐，没有不利。<br>
Remorse: Failing to match another causes shame. It is favorable to wage a campaign. Nothing is unfavorable.<br>
<br>
上六：鸣谦，利用行师征邑国。<br>
慊恨：慊恨而勉强，适宜出动军队，征讨封邑之国。<br>
Resentment: Pressing on cause of discontent and resentment, it is favorable to mobilize the army and campaign against the domain-state.<br>
