《履》<br>
穿鞋 (Treading) - 品行 (Conduct)<br>
<br>
履虎尾，不咥人，亨。<br>
跟随在虎尾之后，老虎却不咬人，亨通。<br>
Treading behind the tiger's tail, one is not bitten. Prosperity.<br>
参考义项：䟿，恭、行<br>
<br>
《象》曰：上天下泽，《履》。君子以辩上下，定民志。<br>
扳-扭转：天在上，泽在下，这就是履卦。君子由此辨明上下名分，规定民众的心志。<br>
Reorientation: Heaven is above and the lake below; this is Lü. Thus the noble one distinguishes higher and lower positions and regulates the people's aims.<br>
<br>
初九：素履，往，无咎。<br>
来-赐予：品行纯诚，（而得）送致，没有过错。<br>
Bestow: Those of honest conduct, receive blessings. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：履道坦坦，幽人贞吉。<br>
品行：走在平坦宽广的道路上；幽居之人守正则吉。<br>
Conduct: One walks along a level and broad road. For the secluded person, constancy is auspicious.<br>
<br>
六三：眇能视，跛能履，履虎尾，咥人，凶。武人为于大君。<br>
执行：一目失明却可以看，跛脚却可以行走；踩到虎尾，咬人，凶。武人有求于大君。<br>
Execution: The one-eyed can see, and the lame can walk; one treads on the tiger's tail, and it bites. Misfortune. A warrior seeks favor from the great ruler.<br>
<br>
九四：履虎尾，愬愬，终吉。<br>
担任：靠近虎尾，戒惧警醒，最终吉利。<br>
Assume: Treading by the tiger's tail, one is apprehensive and vigilant. In the end, auspicious.<br>
<br>
九五：夬履，贞厉。<br>
来-归服：选择福禄；坚持有危险。<br>
Submission: Choose emolument; persistence brings danger.<br>
<br>
上九：视履，考祥其旋，元吉。<br>
踏勘：视察，审察周旋容止，大吉。<br>
Inspection: Inspect the conduct; examine the bearing displayed while turning and moving about. Supreme good fortune.<br>
