《复》<br>
免除 (Remit) - 返回 (Return)<br>
<br>
亨。出入无疾，朋来无咎。反复其道，七日来复。利有攸往。<br>
参考义项：丧礼（復/覆）<br>
通达。出入没有疾苦之色，朋友来不高冷疏远；回来恢复其悼念，七天后回来覆盖（棺材，填土）。适合有所前往。<br>
Smooth. No distress when going out and coming in, do not be distant when friends visit. Return and resume their mourning. Seven days later, come back and cover (the coffin, fill the grave). It is favorable to go forward.<br>
希腊文图式：epistrophe<br>
英文直译：return / turning back<br>
<br>
《象》曰：雷在地中，《复》。先王以至日闭关，商旅不行，后不省方。<br>
庇护：雷藏在地下，复卦。古代君王因此在冬至时关闭关口，使商旅不出行，君主也不巡察四方。<br>
Shield: Thunder is hidden within the earth; this is Fu. Therefore, on the winter solstice, the former kings closed the passes, merchants and travelers did not go out, and the ruler did not inspect the four directions.<br>
希腊文图式：kleisis<br>
英文直译：closure<br>
<br>
初九：不远复，无祗悔，元吉。<br>
恢复：不远就返回，不是举救凋零，大吉。<br>
Recover: If one returns without going far, not to save the withering. Great good fortune.<br>
希腊文图式：metanoeo<br>
英文直译：change one's mind / repent / turn back<br>

覆庇：荫庇之复，吉祥。<br>
Shelter / Consist: As the protection of shade, auspicious.<br>

宽宥：以宾客相待之复，虽然有危险，但还不至于有过错。<br>
Leniency: Treating someone as a guest carries danger, yet it does not amount to fault.<br>

上六：迷复，凶，有灾眚。用行师，终有大败，以其国君，凶，至于十年不克征。<br>
刚愎：沉迷，结果凶险，又有灾祸。若以此出兵，最终大败，罢黜国君，有凶祸，长期都不能取胜。<br>
Obstinacy: Delusion is ominous and brings calamity. If one sends out troops, there will ultimately be great defeat, deposing the ruler, there will be misfortune, and for a long time there will be no success in campaigns.<br>
