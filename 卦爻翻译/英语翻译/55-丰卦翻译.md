《丰》
威风 (Prestige) - 风教 (Moral Influence)

亨。王假之。勿忧，宜日中。
通达。君王凭借（威风），不要忧虑，正宜如日中天（形成风教）。
Success. The king relies on it (prestige). Do not worry; it is just right to be at high noon (for moral influence).
参考义项：封邦建国。

《象》曰：雷电皆至，丰。君子以折狱致刑。
致-表达：雷与电一齐来到，这就是丰。君子以此决断狱讼，评断刑罚。
Expression: Thunder and lightning arrive together; this is Feng. Therefore the noble one resolve legal disputes and judge punishments.

初九：遇其配主。虽旬，无咎，往有尚。[^1]
讽喻：遇到妃子涂抹（因来月事，以丹抹面）。唯有均平，没有过错；前往会逾越。
Allegory: Encountering a concubine applying rouge to her face (due to menstruation). Only fairness, without fault; going forward will lead to transgression. 

六二：丰其蔀，日中见斗。往得疑疾，有孚发若，吉。
礼-威仪：扩大蔽障，正午却看见北斗。给予则遭人疑惧嫌怨；俘虏奔逃。吉。
Dignity: The covering screen expands, yet the Northern Dipper is seen at noon. Giving away brings suspicion, fear, and resentment; captives flee in alarm. Auspicious.

九三：丰其沛，日中见沫，折其右肱，无咎。
体-容纳：遮蔽大，正午只见微弱星光；折伤右臂，但并非错咎。
Contain: The obscuring cover grows large, and at noon only faint starlight can be seen. The right arm being broken, yet it is not a blameworthy fault.

九四：丰其蔀，日中见斗，遇其夷主，吉。
礼敬：（仍）在大的遮蔽之中，正午见斗；但遇到与自己平等相应的主者，因此得吉。
Respect: Still within a great covering, one sees the Dipper at noon; yet one meets a lord who is equal and responsive, and so there is good fortune.

六五：来章，有庆誉，吉。
礼遇：犒劳彰明，于是有庆赏，也有声誉，吉。
Courtesy: Reward and distinction are brought forth clearly; thus there is celebration, honor, and good fortune.

上六：丰其屋，蔀其家，窥其户，阒其无人，三岁不觌，凶。
风-放逸：扩大屋宇，家中却被遮蔽。向门内窥看，寂静得不见一人，久久不复相见，凶。
Dissolute: The house is enlarged, yet the home itself is covered over. One peers through the doorway and finds a deep stillness with no one there; after long years there is no meeting again. Ominous.
