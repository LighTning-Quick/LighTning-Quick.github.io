《丰》
豊器 (Cornucopia) - 蒲草 (Rush)

亨。王假之。勿忧，宜日中。
通达。君王来到（其封国），不要忧虑，（菶菶绵延）正宜如日中天。
Success. The king comes to his fief. Do not worry; it is just right to be at high noon, with flourishing growth spreading in fullness.
参考义项：封邦建国，菶菶绵延；“王假之”兼有君王亲临与授予权柄两层意思。全卦写盛极之时，既光明炽盛，也潜藏遮蔽与转衰。

《象》曰：雷电皆至，丰。君子以折狱致刑。
多物：雷与电一齐来到，这就是丰。君子以此决断狱讼，施行（制止）刑罚。
Many Things: Thunder and lightning arrive together; this is Feng. Therefore the noble one decides lawsuits and carries out, or brings to a stop, punishments.

初九：遇其配主，虽旬无咎，往有尚。
满足：遇到相配的主人（女主人），虽然暂且委曲相从，也没有过错；前往会受到推重。
Contentment: One meets a matching host, a mistress of the house. Though one temporarily bends and accommodates, there is no blame; going on will bring esteem.

六二：丰其蔀，日中见斗。往得疑疾，有孚发若，吉。
大：扩大蔽障，正午却看见北斗。前往则遭人疑惧嫌怨；俘虏奔逃。吉。
Grandeur: The covering screen expands, yet the Northern Dipper is seen at noon. Going on brings suspicion, fear, and resentment; captives flee in alarm. Auspicious.

九三：丰其沛，日中见沫，折其右肱，无咎。
扩大：遮蔽大，正午只见微弱星光；如同折伤右臂，难以施用，但并非其本心之过。
Expansion: The obscuring cover grows large, and at noon only faint starlight can be seen. It is like the right arm being broken, leaving one unable to act, yet it is not a blameworthy fault.

九四：丰其蔀，日中见斗，遇其夷主，吉。
丰裕：（仍）在大的遮蔽之中，正午见斗；但遇到与自己平等相应的主者，因此得吉。
Affluence: Still within a great covering, one sees the Dipper at noon; yet one meets a lord who is equal and responsive, and so there is good fortune.

六五：来章，有庆誉，吉。
使丰足：犒劳彰明，于是有庆赏，也有声誉，吉。
Enrichment: Reward and distinction are brought forth clearly; thus there is celebration, honor, and good fortune.

上六：丰其屋，蔀其家，窥其户，阒其无人，三岁不觌，凶。
茂盛：扩大屋宇，家中却被遮蔽。向门内窥看，寂静得不见一人，久久不复相见，凶。
Luxuriance: The house is enlarged, yet the home itself is covered over. One peers through the doorway and finds a deep stillness with no one there; after long years there is no meeting again. Ominous.
