《丰》
威风 (Prestige) - 风教 (Moral Influence)

亨。王假之。勿忧，宜日中。
通达。君王凭借（威风），不要忧虑，正宜如日中天（形成风教）。
There is passage. The king relies on it (prestige). Do not worry; it is fitting to be like the sun at noon.
参考义项：封邦建国。

《象》曰：雷电皆至，丰。君子以折狱致刑。
致-表达：雷与电一齐来到，这就是丰。君子以此决断狱讼，评断刑罚。
Expression: Thunder and lightning arrive together; this is Feng. The noble one therefore decides legal cases to judge punishments.

初九：遇其配主。虽旬，无咎，往有尚。[^1]
讽喻：遇到妃子涂抹（因来月事，以丹抹面）。唯有均平，没有过错；前往会逾越。
Allegory: Encountering a concubine applying rouge to her face (due to menstruation). Only fairness, there is no blame; going forward will lead to transgression. 

六二：丰其蔀，日中见斗。往得疑疾，有孚发若，吉。
礼-威仪：扩大蔽障，正午却看见北斗。（继续）前往则遭人疑惧嫌怨；俘虏奔逃。吉。
Dignity: The screen grows so dense that the Dipper is visible at noon. Going forward brings suspicion, fear, and resentment; captives flee in alarm. There is good fortune.

九三：丰其沛，日中见沫，折其右肱，无咎。
体-容纳：遮蔽大，正午只见微弱星光；折伤右臂，但并非错咎。
Contain: The obscuring curtain grows dense, and faint stars are visible at noon. The right arm is broken (to fit in), yet there is no blame.

九四：丰其蔀，日中见斗，遇其夷主，吉。
礼敬：（仍）在大的遮蔽之中，正午见斗；但遇到与自己平等相应的主者，因此得吉。
Respect: The screen grows dense, and the Dipper is visible at noon. One meets a lord of equal standing; good fortune.

六五：来章，有庆誉，吉。
礼遇：犒劳彰明，有庆赏，也有声誉，吉。
Courtesy: Reward and distinction are brought forth clearly; there is celebration and renown. Good fortune.

上六：丰其屋，蔀其家，窥其户，阒其无人，三岁不觌，凶。
风-放逸：扩大屋宇，家中却被遮蔽。向门内窥看，寂静得不见一人，三年不复相见，凶。
Dissolute: The house is enlarged, yet the household is screened off. One peers through the doorway and finds a deep stillness with no one there; for three years no one is seen. Misfortune.
# 注疏

[^1]: 《象》曰：“虽旬无咎”，过旬灾也。
