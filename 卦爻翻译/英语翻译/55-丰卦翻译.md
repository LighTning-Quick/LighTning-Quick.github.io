威风 (Prestige) - 风教 (Moral Influence)<br>
<br>
亨。王假之。勿忧，宜日中。<br>
通达。君王凭借它（威风）。疏忽于忧惧（没能点燃烽火），大概如日蚀。<br>
There is passage. The king relies on it (prestige). Neglecting to be mindful of fear (failing to ignite the flames of war) is perhaps like a solar eclipse.<br>
参考义项：烽火。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：雷电皆至，丰。君子以折狱致刑。<br>
致-表达：雷与电一齐来到，这就是丰。君子以此决断狱讼，评断刑罚。<br>
Expression: Thunder and lightning arrive together; this is Feng. The noble one uses the judgment of legal cases to assess punishments.<br>
<br>
初九：遇其配主。虽旬，无咎，往有尚。[^1]<br>
讽喻：遇到妃子（因来月事）以丹抹面。要依从，没有过错；（如此）以往有佑助。<br>
Allegory: Encountering a concubine applying rouge to her face (to refuse sex due to menstruation). Comply, there is no blame; going forward will receive blessings. <br>
<br>
六二：丰其蔀，日中见斗。往得疑疾，有孚发若，吉。<br>
礼-威仪：扩大蔽障，正午却看见北斗。（继续）前往则遭人疑惧嫌怨；使人信从，如同宣告。吉。<br>
Dignity: The screen grows so dense that the Dipper is visible at noon. Going forward brings suspicion, fear, and resentment; to command obedience as if making a declaration. There is good fortune.<br>
<br>
九三：丰其沛，日中见沫，折其右肱，无咎。<br>
体察：遮蔽大，正午可见微弱星光；曲折右臂，没有错咎。<br>
Experience and Observe: The obscuring curtain grows dense, and faint stars are visible at noon. The right arm is bent; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九四：丰其蔀，日中见斗，遇其夷主，吉。<br>
礼敬：（仍）在大的蔽障之中，正午看见北斗；但遇到与自己平等相应的主者，因此得吉。<br>
Respect: The screen grows dense, and the Dipper is visible at noon. One meets a lord of equal standing; good fortune.<br>
<br>
六五：来章，有庆誉，吉。<br>
礼遇：犒劳彰明，有庆赏，也有声誉，吉。<br>
Courtesy: Reward and distinction are brought forth clearly; there is celebration and renown. Good fortune.<br>
<br>
上六：丰其屋，蔀其家，窥其户，阒其无人，三岁不觌，凶。<br>
风-放逸：扩大屋宇，家中却被遮蔽。向门内窥看，寂静得不见一人，三年不复相见，凶。<br>
Dissolute: The house is enlarged, yet the household is screened off. One peers through the doorway and finds a deep stillness with no one there; for three years no one is seen. Misfortune.<br>
