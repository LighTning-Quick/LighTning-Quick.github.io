《无妄》
无虚妄 (Real) - 不迷失 (Steadfast)

元亨，利贞。其匪正有眚，不利有攸往。
大为亨通，利于守正。若不整治，就会有灾眚，不利于有所前往。
Great success; it is favorable to remain steadfast. If things are not set right, calamity will follow; it is not favorable to go anywhere.
参考义项：荒芜

《象》曰：天下雷行，物与，《无妄》。先王以茂对时育万物。
丰茂：雷行天下，万物与之相应，这就是无妄。先王由此勉力配合天时，培育万物。
Flourishing: Thunder moves beneath heaven, and all things respond; this is Wuwang. Thus the former kings diligently accorded with the seasons and nurtured all beings.

初九：无妄，往吉。
外出：不沉迷，前往，吉祥。
Going Out: Free from obsession, go forward; auspicious.

六二：不耕，获；不菑，畬；则利有攸往。
非分：不耕作而收获，不开荒而得粗耕熟田，奸贼；利于有所前往。
Undeserved Gain: Reaping without plowing and gaining a roughly cultivated mature field without first breaking new ground, like a treacherous villain; it is favorable to go forward.

六三：无妄之灾，或系之牛，行人之得，邑人之灾。
亡-扰乱：无法怨望的灾祸：有人拴了一头牛，过路人得到了它，却给邑中人带来损失。
Loss—Disruption: A calamity for which no one can be blamed: someone tethers an ox; a passerby gains it, while the people of the settlement suffer the loss.

九四：可贞，无咎。
盟约：要坚守，没有灾咎。
Covenant: One must hold fast; there is no blame.

九五：无妄之疾，勿药有喜。
寻常：不终止的病，不要用药，采用酒食。
Ordinary: For an non-stop illness, avoid medication and instead use food; there will be joy.

上九：无妄行，有眚，无攸利。
忘-终止：不寻常的行动，会有灾眚，无所利。
Terminate: Nonordinary act, brings calamity, and nothing will be favorable.
