《无妄》<br>
无虚妄 (Real) - 不迷失 (Steadfast)<br>
<br>
元亨，利贞。其匪正有眚，不利有攸往。<br>
大为亨通，利于守正。若不整治，就会有灾眚，不利于有所前往。<br>
Great success; it is favorable to remain steadfast. If things are not set right, calamity will follow; it is not favorable to go anywhere.<br>
参考义项：荒芜<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天下雷行，物与，《无妄》。先王以茂对时育万物。<br>
丰茂：雷行天下，万物与之相应，这就是无妄。先王由此勉力配合天时，培育万物。<br>
Flourishing: Thunder moves beneath heaven, and all things respond; this is Wuwang. Thus the former kings diligently accorded with the seasons and nurtured all beings.<br>
<br>
初九：无妄，往吉。<br>
外出：不沉迷，前往，吉祥。<br>
Going Out: Free from obsession, go forward; auspicious.<br>
<br>
六二：不耕，获；不菑，畬；则利有攸往。<br>
非分：不耕作而收获，不开荒而得粗耕熟田，奸贼；利于有所前往。<br>
Undeserved Gain: Reaping without plowing and gaining a roughly cultivated mature field without first breaking new ground, like a treacherous villain; it is favorable to go forward.<br>
<br>
六三：无妄之灾，或系之牛，行人之得，邑人之灾。<br>
亡-扰乱：无法怨望的灾祸：有人拴了一头牛，过路人得到了它，却给邑中人带来损失。<br>
Loss—Disruption: A calamity for which no one can be blamed: someone tethers an ox; a passerby gains it, while the people of the settlement suffer the loss.<br>
<br>
九四：可贞，无咎。<br>
忘-怠忽：呵斥整肃，没有灾咎。<br>
Negllect: Berate and rectify; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：无妄之疾，勿药有喜。<br>
寻常：不终止的病，不要用药，采用酒食。<br>
Ordinary: For an terminal illness, avoid medication and instead use food.<br>
<br>
上九：无妄行，有眚，无攸利。<br>
狂乱：不寻常的行动，会有灾眚，无所利。<br>
Unrestrained: Nonordinary act, brings calamity, and nothing will be favorable.<br>
