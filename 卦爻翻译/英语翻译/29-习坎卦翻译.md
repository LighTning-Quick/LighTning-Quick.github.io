《习坎》<br>
练习挖坑 (Practice Hole Digging) - 习惯监禁 (Institutionalizing)<br>
<br>
有孚维心，亨。行有尚。<br>
（掩盖挖坑）有俘获，算计内心，通达。以谠正行事（符合约束）。<br>
(Covering the pit-digging) There are captives taken, scheming the inner heart; success. Act with uprightness (in accord with restraint).<br>
参考义项：憸，奸邪阴险；检，品行，约束<br>
<br>
《象》曰：水洊至，习坎。君子以常德行，习教事。<br>
正常：水流相续而至，这就是习坎。君子通过端正德行，学习效力职事。<br>
Normal: The water flows continuously, and this is Xikan. The noble one, by rectifying his moral conduct, learns the fulfillment of his duty.<br>
<br>
初六：习坎，入于坎窞，凶。<br>
歛-给予：（给死人）穿袭衣入殓，放入墓穴深坑，凶险。<br>
Give / Trouble: Dressing the deceased in graveclothes for encoffining, putting into the burial pit; misfortune.<br>
<br>
九二：坎有险，求小得。<br>
敛-收获：用艰险磨砺，最终暂时满意。<br>
Harvest / Traverse: Tempered by perils and hardships, ultimately achieving a sense of temporary satisfaction.<br>
<br>
六三：来之坎坎，险且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。<br>
滥-过度：勤勉挖坑，幽深且深陷，落入坑深处，不可有所作为。<br>
Excessive / Trench: Diligently digging a pit—deep and steep—only to fall to its very bottom; not to accomplish anything.<br>
<br>
六四：樽酒簋贰用缶，纳约自牖，终无咎。<br>
收敛：一樽酒、两簋食，使用瓦器盛装，通过窗户放进绳子。终无过错。<br>
Restraint / Trough: One vessel of wine and two baskets of grain are presented in earthenware, passed in with rope through the window. In the end there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。<br>
敛藏：瘗坎不盈满，（填埋后的）土坻仅得持平，无过错。<br>
Conceal / Tempering: The sacrificial pit is not filled; the earthen mound after burial is only level. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
上六：系用徽纆，寘于丛棘，三岁不得，凶。<br>
聚敛：被绳索捆绑，弃置在荆棘丛中，三年不知道，凶险。<br>
Amass / Tribulation: Bound with cords and placed among thorns, one does not know for three years; misfortune.<br>
