《习坎》<br>
练挖坑 (Practice Hole Digging) - 惯囚槛 (Institutionalizing)<br>
<br>
有孚维心，亨。行有尚。<br>
有付与，仅（在）中心，通达。行为有常法。<br>
An offering is made only at the center; there is passage. Conduct follows an established pattern.<br>
参考义项：瘗埋/习惯<br>
<br>
《象》曰：水洊至，习坎。君子以常德行，习教事。<br>
袭-掩盖：水流相续而至，这就是习坎。君子以此崇尚德行，训练教化之事。<br>
Cover: Water flows on in succession; this is Xi Kan. The noble one therefore reveres virtue and trains in matters of education.<br>
<br>
初六：习坎，入于坎窞，凶。<br>
敛-给予：掩盖坑穴，向里面献纳，凶险。<br>
Give / Trouble: Covering a pit and making an offering into it; misfortune.<br>
<br>
九二：坎有险，求小得。<br>
滥-涌出：挖坑，有艰难，所寻求小有所得。<br>
Gushing / Traverse: To digg a hole, with difficulty; one gains only a little of what one seeks.<br>
<br>
六三：来之坎坎，险且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。<br>
滥-过度：来时咣咣（挖坑），艰难且深陷，落入坎坑深处，不可有所作为。<br>
Excessive / Trench: Came (to dig) with a thud, dangerous and deeply entrenched. Falling into the depths of the pit, one must not act.<br>
<br>
六四：樽酒簋贰用缶，纳约自牖，终无咎。<br>
收敛：一樽酒、两簋食，使用瓦器盛装，通过窗户放进绳子。终无过错。<br>
Restraint / Trough: One vessel of wine and two baskets of grain are presented in earthenware, passed in with rope through the window. In the end there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。<br>
敛藏：瘗坎不盈满，（填埋后的）土坻仅得持平，无过错。<br>
Conceal / Tempering: The sacrificial pit is not filled; the earthen mound after burial is only level. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
上六：系用徽纆，寘于丛棘，三岁不得，凶。<br>
聚敛：被绳索捆绑，放置在荆棘丛中，三年不得解脱，凶险。<br>
Amass / Tribulation: Bound with cords and placed among thorns, one cannot escape for three years; misfortune.<br>
