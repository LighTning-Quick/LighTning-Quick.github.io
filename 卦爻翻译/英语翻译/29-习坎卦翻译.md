《习坎》
练挖坑 (Practice Hole Digging) - 惯囚槛 (Institutionalizing)

习坎：有孚维心，亨。行有尚。
翻译：有付与，仅（在）中心，通达。行为有常法。
英文：Sacrifice is given only at the center, free passage. Actions follow routines.
参考义项：瘗埋/习惯

《象》曰：水洊至，习坎。君子以常德行，习教事。
袭-掩盖：水流相续而至，这就是习坎。君子以此崇尚德行，训练教化之事。
Cover: Water flows continuously, one after another; this is The Abyss. The noble one thereby reveres virtue and trains in matters of education.

初六：习坎，入于坎窞，凶。
给予：掩盖坑穴，向里面献纳，旁入（陷落），凶险。
Submit / Trouble: to cover up a pit, by offering to it, only to fall into it; dangerous.

九二：坎有险，求小得。
涌出：挖坑，有艰难，所寻求小有所得。
Gushing / Traverse: To digg a hole, with difficulty, achieving some success in what one seeks.

六三：来之坎坎，险且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。
过度：来时咣咣（挖坑），艰难且深陷，落入坎坑深处，不可有所作为。
Excessive / Trench: Came to dig with a thud, perilous and sinking deep; falling into the depths of the pit. one cannot take action.

六四：樽酒簋贰用缶，纳约自牖，终无咎。
收敛：一樽酒、两簋食，使用瓦器盛装，通过窗户放进绳子。终无过错。
Restraint / Trough: One vessel of wine, two baskets of grain, using earthenware containers, delivering it with rope through the window. ultimately there is no blame.

九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。
敛藏：瘗坎不盈满，（填埋后的）土坻仅得持平，无过错。
Containment / Tempering: The sacrificial pit is not filled; the earthen mound after burial is merely level; there is no blame.

上六：系用徽纆，寘于丛棘，三岁不得，凶。
聚敛：被绳索捆绑，放置在荆棘丛中，三年不得解脱，凶险。
Amass / Tribulation: Bound with ropes and placed among thorns; unable to escape for three years. there is misfortune.
