吉。原筮元。永贞无咎。不宁方来，后夫凶。<br>
翻译：吉利。推究占卜，行动情状体，长久守正无过错。（时局）不平静，正在招徕，迟来的有凶险。<br>
英文：Auspicious. Examine the divination, situation aspect of activity, long-lasting uprightness has no blame. In turbulent times, currently inviting; those who come late face grave danger.<br>
参考义项：太卜/朝会<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地上有水，《比》。先王以建万国，亲诸侯。<br>
亲近：地面上有水，这就是比卦。先王以此建立万国，更新诸侯。<br>
Endearment: Water is on the earth; this is Bi. Therefore the earlier kings by this established ten thousand states and renewed the feudal lords.<br>
<br>
初六：有孚比之，无咎。有孚盈缶，终来有它，吉。<br>
治理：使人信从，聚集他们，没有过错。付出充满陶罐，众人来佑助他，吉利。<br>
Regulation / Fitting: Make people trust and follow, gather them, no blame. Contributions fill the earthenware vessel; the multitude comes to assist him, auspicious.<br>
希腊文图式：pistis<br>
英文直译：faith / trust / confidence<br>
<br>
六二：比之自内，贞吉。<br>
效法：比照他们，接纳，坚持则吉利。<br>
Imitation / Profit: Compare with them, and accept, if this persists, auspicious.<br>
希腊文图式：mimetes<br>
英文直译：imitator / follower<br>
