矢栝 (Nocking) - 篦顺 (Combing)<br>
<br>
吉。原筮元。永贞无咎。不宁方来，后夫凶。<br>
翻译：吉利。推究占卜，玩味。长久守正无过错。不告假而放纵，治理后对抗，有凶险。<br>
英文：Auspicious. Examine the divination, to savor, long-lasting uprightness has no blame. Indulgence without permission, resistance after being disciplined, dangerous.<br>
参考义项：（太卜）笓，排列 ；批，触犯，抛弃<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地上有水，《比》。先王以建万国，亲诸侯。<br>
亲近：地面上有水，这就是比卦。先王以此建立万国，辛苦诸侯。<br>
Endearment: Water is on the earth; this is Bi. The earlier kings established ten thousand states and make the feudal lords put in a lot of hard work.<br>
<br>
初六：有孚比之，无咎。有孚盈缶，终来有它，吉。<br>
庀-治理：使人信从，聚集他们，没有过错。付出充满陶罐，众人来佑助他，吉利。<br>
Administer / Fitting: Make people trust and follow, gather them, no blame. Contributions fill the earthenware vessel; the multitude comes to assist him, auspicious.<br>
希腊文图式：pistis<br>
英文直译：faith / trust / confidence<br>
<br>
六二：比之自内，贞吉。<br>
效法：用引进来治理事情。<br>
Imitation / Profit: Adopting to administer affairs.<br>
希腊文图式：mimetes<br>
英文直译：imitator / follower<br>
<br>
六三：比之匪人。<br>
卑-鄙薄：睥睨行为不当的人。<br>
Contempt / Unfit: To look down upon those whose conduct is improper.<br>
希腊文图式：parasitos<br>
英文直译：hanger-on / toady<br>
<br>
六四：外比之，贞吉。<br>
裨-辅佐：外人弥补他。<br>
Supplementary / Outfit: Something from an outside source that compensates for or complements what is lacking.<br>
希腊文图式：symmaches<br>
英文直译：ally / companion<br>
<br>
九五：显比，王用三驱，失前禽，邑人不诫，吉。<br>
亲近：和乐之比。君王用三驱之礼，失掉前来的猎物，邑人不警诫，吉利。<br>
Drawing Near / Benefit: A state of harmonious rapport. A ruler employs the ritual of the "three-sided hunt"—allowing game coming toward him to escape—and the local people take no alarm at this; auspicious.<br>
希腊文图式：philia<br>
英文直译：friendship / love / affection<br>

勾结：亲近而不治理。<br>
Collusion / Overfit: Drawing near without exercising proper governance or discipline.<br>
