《颐》<br>
抚养 (Foster) - 使役 (Employ)<br>
<br>
贞吉。观颐，自求口实。<br>
守正吉祥。鼓励颐养，自行求取填饱肚子。<br>
Steadfastness is auspicious. Encourage them to take care of themselves and fill their mouths. <br>
参考义项：姬，通居，安置，治理<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山下有雷，《颐》。君子以慎言语，节饮食。<br>
阻截：山下有雷，这就是颐。君子由此谨慎言语，节制饮食。<br>
Block: Thunder lies beneath the mountain; this is Yi. Thus the noble one is cautious in speech and moderate in food and drink.<br>
<br>
初九：舍尔灵龟，观我朵颐，凶。<br>
痒-烦乱：舍弃你占卜灵龟，反而看着我鼓动面颊（吃东西），凶险。<br>
Itching: You cast aside your sacred tortoise used for divination and instead watch me move my cheeks as I eat. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
六二：颠颐，拂经于丘颐，征凶。<br>
供养： 因为嘴里空空，经由（不正当）捷径填满嘴巴；前往有凶险。<br>
Dependency: Because the mouth was empty, the mouth was filled through a (illegal) shortcut; going forward brings misfortune.<br>
<br>
六三：拂颐，贞凶，十年勿用，无攸利。<br>
教养：矫正面颊，固守有凶险；十年不可施用，无所利。<br>
Cultivation: Correct the cheeks. Remaining steadfast brings misfortune; for ten years it must not be put to use. Nothing is favorable.<br>
<br>
六四：颠颐，吉。虎视眈眈，其欲逐逐，无咎。<br>
助长：填满下巴，吉祥；像老虎专注凝视，欲求接连而来，没有过错。<br>
Encouragement: Fill the lower jaw; auspicious. One watches intently like a tiger, desires following one after another. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
六五：拂经，居贞吉，不可涉大川。<br>
主持：放置经线，治理，守正则吉；不可渡过大河。<br>
Presiding: Setting the warp threads, to govern. Remaining steadfast is auspicious; one must not cross the great river.<br>
<br>
上九：由颐，厉，吉。利涉大川。<br>
详-审慎：谋画；危险，吉祥。利于渡过大河。<br>
Sedate: Planning; there is danger, yet it is auspicious. It is favorable to cross the great river.<br>
