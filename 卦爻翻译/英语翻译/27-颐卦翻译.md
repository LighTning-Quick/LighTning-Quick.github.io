《颐》<br>
补养 (Nourish) - 疗养 (Convalesce)<br>
<br>
贞吉。观颐自，求口实。<br>
守正吉祥。观察探取原因，纠察叩击结果。<br>
Steadfastness is auspicious. Observe and probe to uncover the causes; examine and scrutinize to assess the outcomes.<br>
参考义项：敞，敞开，撞击。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山下有雷，《颐》。君子以慎言语，节饮食。<br>
蓄养-引_划定：山下有雷，这就是颐。君子通过依顺责言体悟，节制饮食。<br>
Retain-Delimit: Thunder lies beneath the mountain; this is Yi. Thus the noble one, by complying with reproachful words and attaining realization, moderates food and drink.<br>
<br>
初九：舍尔灵龟，观我朵颐，凶。<br>
痒-烦乱：舍弃你占卜灵龟，劝我鼓动面颊（吃东西），凶险。<br>
Itching: You cast aside your sacred tortoise used for divination and persuade me to move my cheeks as I eat. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
六二：颠颐，拂经于丘颐，征凶。<br>
取得： 因为嘴里空空，经由（不正当）捷径填满嘴巴；前往有凶险。<br>
Taking: Because the mouth was empty, the mouth was filled through a (illegal) shortcut; going forward brings misfortune.<br>
<br>
六三：拂颐，贞凶，十年勿用，无攸利。<br>
储藏：放置，固守有凶险；十年没有施用，无所适宜。<br>
Storing: To place. Remaining steadfast brings misfortune; for ten years it is not put to use. It is suitable for nothing.<br>
<br>
六四：颠颐，吉。虎视眈眈，其欲逐逐，无咎。<br>
上/享-进献：进献，吉祥；老虎凝视威猛深邃，它的欲求混沌不测，没有过错。<br>
Offering: To consecrate; auspicious. The tiger's gaze is fierce and profound, its desires chaotic and unfathomable. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
六五：拂经，居贞吉，不可涉大川。<br>
执掌：违背常道，治理，守正则吉；不可渡过大河。<br>
Presiding: Departing from the normal course, (and therefore requires to be) brought under control. Remaining steadfast is auspicious; one must not cross the great river.<br>
<br>
上九：由颐，厉，吉。利涉大川。<br>
详平：准则，执法，吉祥。利于渡过大河。<br>
Fair: Standard, enforcing the law; auspicious. It is favorable to cross the great river.<br>
