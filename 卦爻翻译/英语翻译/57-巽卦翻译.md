《巽》<br>
願：倾慕 (Aspiration) - 还：罢歇 (Pausation)<br>
<br>
巽：小亨。利有攸往，利见大人。<br>
小亨通。利于有所前往，利于进见大人。<br>
Small passage. Favorable for having somewhere to go, favorable for seeing the great man.<br>
参考义项：倾慕/罢歇。愻，谦逊恭顺<br>
<br>
《彖》曰：《巽》，说也。刚巽乎中正而志行。柔皆顺乎刚，是以"小亨，利有攸往，利见大人"。<br>
《象》曰：随风，《巽》。君子以申命行事。<br>
身-身体力行：风相随，巽卦之象。君子以之申明名义，兼摄职事。<br>
Enactment: Wind following wind, the image of Xun. The superior man uses it to declare the name and act as a deputy concurrently.<br>
<br>
初六：进退，利武人之贞。<br>
派遣：进或退，利于武人的贞正。<br>
Dispatch: Advancing or retreating, favorable for the warrior's correctness.<br>
<br>
九二：巽在床下，用史巫纷若，吉，无咎。<br>
卑顺：孙子放置在床，仆倒，任用各种各样的卜史、巫师，吉，没有咎错.<br>
Obedience: Placing the descendant on the bed, falling prostate, employing various diviners and shamans, auspicious, no blame.<br>
<br>
九三：频巽，吝。<br>
逊色：宾服，有吝惜。<br>
Inferiority: Submitting, there is stinginess.<br>
<br>
六四：悔亡，田获三品。<br>
置办：悔恨消失，田猎获得三样东西。<br>
Procurement: Regret vanishes, hunting yields three kinds of game.<br>
<br>
九五：贞吉，悔亡，无不利，无初有终。先庚三日，后庚三日，吉。<br>
恭顺：贞正有吉，悔恨消失，没有不利。没有开始而成就。在更替前后三日（恭顺），吉。<br>
Deference: Correctness brings auspiciousness, regret vanishes, nothing unfavorable. No beginning but achievement. In the three days before and after the change, auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：巽在床下，丧其资斧，贞凶。<br>
逃避：辞让主宰的坐位，离开，耗费钱财，坚持有凶。<br>
Evasion: Declining the master's seat, departing, wasting money, persistence brings misfortune.<br>
