《贲》[^1]<br>
贲：横膈膜 (Diaphragm) - 奔星 (Meteor Streak)<br>
<br>
亨。小利有攸往。<br>
通达。（辩论）减少，适合有所往。<br>
Accomplishment.  (Debating) To reduce, suitable for going somewhere.<br>
参考义项：辩论，说事分明以治理、纠正<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山下有火，《贲》。君子以明庶政，无敢折狱。<br>
勤勉：山下有火，这就是贲卦。君子因明辨各种征验，不冒然裁断讼狱。<br>
Strive: Under the mountain there is fire; this is Ben. The noble one, since clearly discerning the various signs and evidence, does not rashly adjudicate legal disputes. <br>
<br>
初九：贲其趾，舍车而徒。<br>
变通：改变典礼，保留车而涂饰。<br>
Adaptation: Modify the ceremony, while retaining the vehicle and refinishing it.<br>
<br>
六二：贲其须。<br>
辨认：胡须斑白。<br>
Identify: grizzled beard.<br>
<br>
九三：贲如濡如，永贞吉。<br>
颁布：如班赐如润泽，长远贞正，吉祥。<br>
Promulgate: As if bestowing, as if enriching; lasting rectitude brings auspiciousness.<br>
<br>
六四：贲如皤如，白马翰如，匪寇，婚媾。<br>
奔-奋勇：斑驳洁白，白马飞驰啊，不是寇盗，是婚媾。<br>
Courage: Mottled yet pure; white horses galloping; not bandits, but a marriage match.<br>
<br>
六五：贲于丘园，束帛戔戔。吝，终吉。<br>
奔赴：奔向坟丘墓园，（只为了献上）微薄束帛。有吝惜，最终吉祥。<br>
Hastening: Rushing to the burial mound, (only to offer) a modest gift of silk. There is a sense of reluctance, yet ultimately auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：白贲，无咎。<br>
偾发：逼迫（而发），没有灾咎。<br>
Burst: Forced; there is no blame.<br>
