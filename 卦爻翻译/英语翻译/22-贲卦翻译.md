《贲》[^1]<br>
分辨 (Distinguish) - 扳：挽引 (Pull)<br>
<br>
亨。小利有攸往。<br>
通达。小有所往则利。<br>
Accomplishment. Small ventures are favorable.<br>
参考义项：辩论，说事分明以驳正<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山下有火，《贲》。君子以明庶政，无敢折狱。<br>
折-摘取：山下有火，这就是贲卦。君子以此估定百姓的征税，不敢誓戒讼狱。<br>
Pluck: Under the mountain there is fire; this is Ben. A wise man uses this to assess various taxes and levies of the common people, and dares not to warn with lawsuits. <br>
<br>
初九：贲其趾，舍车而徒。<br>
本-探究：辨认其踪迹，舍弃车舆而徒步。<br>
Examine: Discern their tracks, abandon the carriage and walk on foot.<br>
<br>
六二：贲其须。<br>
班赐：颁布需求。<br>
Reward: Promulgate the need.<br>
<br>
九三：贲如濡如，永贞吉。<br>
装饰：华美润泽，歌咏，守正则吉。<br>
Adornment: Adorned and lustrous, celebrated; maintaining rectitude brings auspiciousness.<br>
<br>
六四：贲如皤如，白马翰如，匪寇，婚媾。<br>
奔-奋勇：斑驳洁白，白马飞驰啊，不是寇盗，是婚媾。<br>
Courage: Mottled yet pure; white horses galloping; not bandits, but a marriage match.<br>
<br>
六五：贲于丘园，束帛戔戔。吝，终吉。<br>
征聘：聘问于丘园，束帛微薄。有吝惜，最终吉祥。<br>
Engage: Drop in the hill garden; the bundles of silk are meager. There is regret, but ultimately auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：白贲，无咎。<br>
坟-顺从：白色（土壤）肥沃坟起，没有灾咎。<br>
Docile: White (soil), fertile and raised; there is no blame.<br>
