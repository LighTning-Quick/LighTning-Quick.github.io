《损》<br>
损坏 (Impairment) - 伤害 (Affliction)<br>
<br>
《损》：有孚，元吉，无咎，可贞，利有攸往。曷之用，二簋可用享。[^1]<br>
有诚信，大吉，没有咎害，可以守正，利于有所前往。遏止其媚悦，两簋就可用以享祀。<br>
Decrease. With sincerity there is supreme good fortune and no blame; one may persevere, and it is fitting to go somewhere. By curbing the desire to curry favor, a mere two vessels of grain suffice for the sacrificial offering.<br>
参考义项：远，疏远久长。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山下有泽，《损》。君子以惩忿窒欲。<br>
恎-狠戾：山下有泽（泽自损以润山），这就是损卦。君子因此惩治忿怒、堵塞嗜欲。<br>
Ruthless: Water stands beneath the mountain; this is Sun. Therefore the noble one curbs his wrath and dams his desires.<br>
<br>
初九：巳事遄往，无咎。酌损之。[^2]<br>
自制：祭祀之事迅速送致，没有过错；斟酌着减少它。<br>
Self-restraint: The sacrificial offerings are swiftly conveyed; there is no blame; decrease them with measured deliberation.<br>
<br>
九二：利贞。征凶，弗损，益之。<br>
损辱：利于守正。出征就有凶险；不降抑阻碍它。<br>
Humiliation: Perseverance is favorable. Marching out brings danger; do not depress or obstruct it.<br>
<br>
六三：三人行则损一人，一人行则得其友。<br>
损失：三人同行就损害一人，一人独行就得到朋友。<br>
Loss: When three walk together, one of them is harmed; walking alone, one gains a friend.<br>
<br>
六四：损其疾，使遄有喜，无咎。<br>
櫛_剔除：减轻他的疾病，使之卑顺又欢喜，没有过错。<br>
Combing: Alleviate his ailment, making him obedient and joyful; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六五：或益之十朋之龟，弗克违，元吉。[^3]<br>
减损：有人埋没他价值十朋的大宝龟，无法违抗，大吉。<br>
Decrease: Someone buries a great tortoise worth ten strings of cowries for him; it cannot be defied—supreme good fortune.<br>
<br>
上九：弗损，益之，无咎，贞吉。利有攸往，得臣无家。<br>
减轻：不清除，限制他，没有过错，守正吉祥。利于有所前往，得到没有家（归附）的臣仆。<br>
Alleviation: Not clearing away but restraining him—no blame; perseverance brings good fortune. It is fitting to go somewhere, gaining servants who have no home (to attach themselves to).<br>
# 注疏<br>
<br>
[^1]: 闻一多："曷"读为"丐"，犹言遗之以二簋。诚则少祭可利往。<br>
[^2]: 巳（祀）事：祭祀之事；快祭，利己损人之初，故须酌损不过度。<br>
[^3]: 别人主动给（不用自己损害），却之不恭。古者货贝而宝龟，元龟当大贝十朋。<br>
