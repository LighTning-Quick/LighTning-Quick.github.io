《困》
閫：郭门 (Outer City Gate) - 閫：宫中道路 (Inner Court Alley)

亨。贞大人吉，无咎。有言不信。
通达。守正于大人之道则吉，无咎。虽有盟约（以约束），却难以取信。
Passage. Constancy in the way of the great person brings good fortune and no blame. Though there are pledges (to bind each other), they are hard to make trusted.
参考义项：围城：从因被困而得不到，到因得到而被困。要盟（城下之盟）。

《象》曰：泽无水，《困》。君子以致命遂志。
实-结果：泽中无水，这是困卦。君子因此招致盟友，完成其志。
Result: The marsh has no water; this is Kun. The noble one therefore summons allies and carries through the will.

初六：臀困于株木，入于幽谷，三岁不觌。
阻碍：臀部被刑杖敲打，进入幽暗山谷，三年不得见。
Block: The buttocks are beaten by a punishment rod; one enters a dark valley and is not seen for three years.

《象》曰：“入于幽谷”，幽不明也。
“入于幽谷”，是因为幽暗而不明。
"Entering a dark valley" means being shut in obscurity, without light.

九二：困于酒食，朱绂方来。利用享祀。征凶，无咎。
困难：匮乏酒食，高贵的朱绂正赐予。利于举行享祀。出征则凶，但无咎。
Difficulty: Wine and food are lacking; the noble with vermilion knee-cover is just bestowing. It is suitable to make offerings (to the people). To go on campaign is misfortune, yet there is no blame.

《象》曰：“困于酒食”，中有庆也。
“困于酒食”，是因为中位有可庆之事。
"Lacking wine and food" means there is celebration within the center.

六三：困于石，据于蒺藜，入于其宫，不见其妻，凶。
围困：被石头阻碍，抓着蒺藜。进入自己的宫室，却不会见其妻，凶。
Siege: Blocked by stone, grabbing onto thorny caltrops. One enters one's own dwelling but does not meet the wife. Misfortune.

《象》曰：“据于蒺藜”，乘刚也。“入于其宫，不见其妻”，不祥也。
“据于蒺藜”，是柔乘刚而失当。“入于其宫，不见其妻”，是不祥。
"Grabbing onto caltrops" means the soft rides upon the firm and is out of place. "Entering the dwelling and not seeing the wife" is inauspicious.

九四：来，徐徐困于金车，吝，有终。
疲惫：归来，在兵车之中安睡，有吝难，朋友众多。
Exhausted: Coming back, sound asleep within the war chariot. There is regretful difficulty, yet many friends are present.

《象》曰：“来徐徐”，志在下也。虽不当位，有与也。
“来徐徐”，是志向在下。虽位置不当，仍有所应与。
"Coming slowly" means the intent is below. Though the position is not proper, there is still someone to join with.

九五：臲卼，困于赤绂，乃徐有说，利用祭祀。
困窘：处于不安危殆之中，又被赤绂礼服捆缚。随后渐渐有所解脱，利于祭祀。
Distress: Unsteady and precarious, bound by the red knee-cover as ritual dress. Then slowly there is release. It is suitable to make sacrifices (to the god).

《象》曰：“臲”，志未得也。“乃徐有说”，以中直也。“利用祭祀”，受福也。
“臲”，是志尚未得伸。“乃徐有说”，是因为中正而直。“利用祭祀”，是受福。
"Precarious" means the will has not yet been fulfilled. "Then slowly there is release" is due to central uprightness. "Useful to make sacrifices" means receiving blessing.

上六：困于葛藟，于臲卼，曰动悔有悔，征吉。
穷尽：（棺材）用葛藤捆着，与割鼻断足比较，于是触动愚昧，有悔意，出征有吉。
Exhaustion: (The coffin) Bound with kudzu vines, set against the choice of nose-cutting and foot-cutting, thereupon ignorance is stirred; there is remorse, going on campaign brings good fortune.

《象》曰：“困于葛藟”，未当也。“动悔有悔”，吉行也。
“困于葛藟”，是尚未得当。“动悔有悔”，是吉祥的行动。
"Constrained by tangled vines" means things are not yet fitting. "ignorance is stirred, remorse is present" means the movement is auspicious.
