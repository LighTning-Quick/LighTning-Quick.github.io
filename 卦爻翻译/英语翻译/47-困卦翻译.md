《困》<br>
閫：郭门 (Outer City Gate) - 閫：宫中道路 (Inner Court Alley)<br>
<br>
亨。贞大人吉，无咎。有言不信。<br>
通达。守正于大人之道则吉，无咎。虽有盟约（以约束），却难以取信。<br>
Passage. Constancy in the way of the great person brings good fortune and no blame. Though there are pledges (to bind each other), they are hard to make trusted.<br>
参考义项：围城：从因被困而得不到，到因得到而被困。要盟（城下之盟）。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽无水，《困》。君子以致命遂志。<br>
实-结果：泽中无水，这是困卦。君子因此招致盟友，完成其志。<br>
Result: The marsh has no water; this is Kun. The noble one therefore summons allies and carries through the will.<br>
<br>
初六：臀困于株木，入于幽谷，三岁不觌。<br>
阻碍：臀部被刑杖敲打，进入幽暗山谷，三年不得见。<br>
Block: The buttocks are beaten by a punishment rod; one enters a dark valley and is not seen for three years.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“入于幽谷”，幽不明也。<br>
“入于幽谷”，是因为幽暗而不明。<br>
"Entering a dark valley" means being shut in obscurity, without light.<br>
<br>
九二：困于酒食，朱绂方来。利用享祀。征凶，无咎。<br>
困难：匮乏酒食，高贵的朱绂正赐予。利于举行享祀。出征则凶，但无咎。<br>
Difficulty: Wine and food are lacking; the noble with vermilion knee-cover is just bestowing. It is suitable to make offerings (to the people). To go on campaign is misfortune, yet there is no blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“困于酒食”，中有庆也。<br>
“困于酒食”，是因为中位有可庆之事。<br>
"Lacking wine and food" means there is celebration within the center.<br>
<br>
六三：困于石，据于蒺藜，入于其宫，不见其妻，凶。<br>
围困：被石头阻碍，抓着蒺藜。进入自己的宫室，却不见其妻，凶。<br>
Siege: Blocked by stone, grabbing onto thorny caltrops. One enters one's own dwelling but does not see his wife. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“据于蒺藜”，乘刚也。“入于其宫，不见其妻”，不祥也。<br>
“据于蒺藜”，是柔乘刚而失当。“入于其宫，不见其妻”，是不祥。<br>
"Grabbing onto caltrops" means the soft rides upon the firm and is out of place. "Entering the dwelling and not seeing the wife" is inauspicious.<br>
<br>
九四：来，徐徐困于金车，吝，有终。<br>
疲惫：归来，在兵车之中安睡，有吝难，朋友众多。<br>
Exhausted: Coming back, sound asleep within the war chariot. There is regretful difficulty, yet many friends are present.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“来徐徐”，志在下也。虽不当位，有与也。<br>
“来徐徐”，是志向在下。虽位置不当，仍有所应与。<br>
"Coming slowly" means the intent is below. Though the position is not proper, there is still someone to join with.<br>
<br>
九五：臲卼，困于赤绂，乃徐有说，利用祭祀。<br>
困窘：处于不安危殆之中，又被赤绂礼服捆缚。随后渐渐有所解脱，利于祭祀。<br>
Distress: Unsteady and precarious, bound by the red knee-cover as ritual dress. Then slowly there is release. It is suitable to make sacrifices (to the god).<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“臲”，志未得也。“乃徐有说”，以中直也。“利用祭祀”，受福也。<br>
“臲”，是志尚未得伸。“乃徐有说”，是因为中正而直。“利用祭祀”，是受福。<br>
"Precarious" means the will has not yet been fulfilled. "Then slowly there is release" is due to central uprightness. "Useful to make sacrifices" means receiving blessing.<br>
<br>
上六：困于葛藟，于臲卼，曰动悔有悔，征吉。<br>
穷尽：（棺材）用葛藤捆着，与割鼻断足比较，于是触动愚昧，有悔意，出征有吉。<br>
Exhaustion: (The coffin) Bound with kudzu vines, set against the choice of nose-cutting and foot-cutting, thereupon ignorance is stirred; there is remorse, going on campaign brings good fortune.<br>
<br>
《象》曰：“困于葛藟”，未当也。“动悔有悔”，吉行也。<br>
“困于葛藟”，是尚未得当。“动悔有悔”，是吉祥的行动。<br>
"Constrained by tangled vines" means things are not yet fitting. "ignorance is stirred, remorse is present" means the movement is auspicious.<br>
