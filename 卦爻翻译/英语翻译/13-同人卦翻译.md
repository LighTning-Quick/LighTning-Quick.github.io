《同人》<br>
尊重人 (Respecting People) - 触动人 (Moving People)<br>
<br>
同人于野，亨。利涉大川。利君子贞。<br>
在屋檐下随同他人，通达。利于涉过大河，利于君子守持正固。<br>
Following others under the eaves, brings success. It is favorable to cross the great river, and favorable for the noble one to hold fast to what is upright.<br>
参考义项：佣役。随同而不同。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天与火，同人。君子以类族辨物。<br>
戾戾-分明：天与火相与，这就是同人。君子以此遵循族属治理万物。<br>
Distinct: Heaven together with fire forms Tongren. Therefore the noble one governs things according to their clan affiliation.<br>
<br>
初九：同人于门，无咎。<br>
威重：在大门通告人，没有过错。<br>
Gravity / Accord: One announces the people at the gate; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：同人于宗，吝。<br>
通-交往：在宗族多施仁德（亲仁），会有局促与憾惜。<br>
Intercourse / Concord: One shows many kindness within the clan (kin-centered humaneness), there will be constraint and regret.<br>
<br>
九三：伏戎于莽，升其高陵，三岁不兴。<br>
疏通：在草莽中驾驭兵车，要登上高陵，长久不能兴兵。<br>
Dredging / Discordance: One drives war chariots through the overgrowth and seeks to ascend the high ridge, yet for a long time cannot raise the troops.<br>
<br>
九四：乘其墉，弗克攻，吉。<br>
慎重：登上对方的城墙，却没能攻占，吉。<br>
Prudence / Record: One climbs the other side's wall yet fails to capture it; this is auspicious.<br>
<br>
九五：同人先号咷而后笑，大师克相遇。<br>
统一：会合人，先哭号，后笑；大帅（克敌之后）终得相遇。<br>
Unification / Coordinator: Rendezvous; first cry out and weep, then laugh. Generals can finally meet (after overcoming the enemy).<br>
<br>
上九：同人于郊，无悔。<br>
种-培植：在郊外操作锄头，没有悔恨。<br>
Cultivation / Cardiac Transplant: One works the hoe in the outskirts; there is no regret.<br>
