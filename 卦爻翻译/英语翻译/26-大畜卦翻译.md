《大畜》<br>
大牲畜 (Livestock) - 需要养育 (Needs Cultivation)<br>
<br>
利贞。不家食吉。利涉大川。<br>
利于守正。不家家饲养，吉祥。利于渡过大河。<br>
It is favorable to remain steadfast. Not rearing livestock household by household is auspicious. It is favorable to cross the great river.<br>
参考义项：𤲸，田畜<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天在山中，《大畜》。君子以多识前言往行，以畜其德。<br>
职能：天藏在山中，这就是大畜。君子由此广泛记取前人的言论与行事，以蓄养自己的德行。<br>
Function: Heaven is held within the mountain; this is Daxu. Thus the noble one learns widely from the words and deeds of the past in order to cultivate virtue.<br>
<br>
初九：有厉，利已。<br>
喜爱：有危险，利于停止。<br>
Like: There is danger; it is favorable to stop.<br>
<br>
九二：舆说輹。<br>
积蓄：车舆脱落承载之輹木。<br>
Accumulation: The carriage loses its load-bearing crosspiece.<br>
<br>
九三：良马逐，利艰贞，日闲舆卫。利有攸往。<br>
勤劳：良马竞逐，利于跟随良善，每日娴熟预备承侍；利于有所前往。<br>
Diligence: Like a fine horse racing, it is beneficial to follow the virtuous, to be skilled (in running) and preparing to serve each day; it is beneficial to go somewhere.<br>
<br>
六四：童牛之牿，元吉。<br>
驯服：给小牛装上牿木，大吉。<br>
Domesticated: Fit a young ox with a restraining bar; great good fortune.<br>
<br>
六五：豮豕之牙，吉。<br>
怀藏：阉猪的牙，吉祥。<br>
Possessing: The tusks of a castrated boar; auspicious.<br>
<br>
上九：何天之衢，亨。<br>
容纳：承荷上天的大道，亨通。<br>
Accommodation: the main thoroughfares that carry the weight of the heavens; success.<br>
