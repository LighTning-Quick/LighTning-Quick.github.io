《大畜》
大牲畜 (Livestock) - 需要养育 (Needs Cultivation)

利贞。不家食吉。利涉大川。
利于守正。不家家饲养，吉祥。利于渡过大河。
It is favorable to remain steadfast. Not rearing livestock household by household is auspicious. It is favorable to cross the great river.
参考义项：𤲸，田畜

《象》曰：天在山中，《大畜》。君子以多识前言往行，以畜其德。
职能：天藏在山中，这就是大畜。君子由此广泛记取前人的言论与行事，以蓄养自己的德行。
Function: Heaven is held within the mountain; this is Daxu. Thus the noble one learns widely from the words and deeds of the past in order to cultivate virtue.

初九：有厉，利已。
喜爱：有危险，利于停止。
Like: There is danger; it is favorable to stop.

九二：舆说輹。
积蓄：车舆脱落承载之輹木。
Accumulation: The carriage loses its load-bearing crosspiece.

九三：良马逐，利艰贞，日闲舆卫。利有攸往。
勤劳：良马竞逐，利于跟随良善，每日娴熟预备承侍；利于有所前往。
Diligence: Like a fine horse racing, it is beneficial to follow the virtuous, to be skilled (in running) and preparing to serve each day; it is beneficial to go somewhere.

六四：童牛之牿，元吉。
驯服：给小牛装上牿木，大吉。
Domesticated: Fit a young ox with a restraining bar; great good fortune.

六五：豮豕之牙，吉。
怀藏：阉猪的牙，吉祥。
Possessing: The tusks of a castrated boar; auspicious.

上九：何天之衢，亨。
容纳：承荷上天的大道，亨通。
Accommodation: the main thoroughfares that carry the weight of the heavens; success.
