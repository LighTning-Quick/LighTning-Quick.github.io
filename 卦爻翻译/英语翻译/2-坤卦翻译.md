《坤》
循顺 (Follow/Sexton) - 巡察 (Patrol/Surveyor)

元亨。利牝马之贞。君子有攸往，先迷后得主。利西南得朋，东北丧朋。安贞吉。
大通亨达。利于如牝马那样柔顺而守正。君子有所前往，开始迷失，后来找到主导者(便能得其常道)。往东南西北四方出走（巡狩），得到或失去钱财，安定、安抚（百姓），守正则吉。
Great flourishing and free passage. It is favorable to be gentle and steadfast like a mare. If the noble one has somewhere to go, going first on his own is easy to lose the way; afterward, he can find the master as the constant course. Going east, south, west, or north, he may gain or lose wealth to bring stability and to soothe the people, steadfastness brings good fortune.
参考义项：驯顺，天子巡狩。

《象》曰：地势坤，君子以厚德载物。
载-施行：大地之势柔顺而广厚，这就是坤。君子因此庆赏丰厚，巡察众人。
Implement: The earth's configuration is yielding and broad; this is Kun. Therefore the noble one bestowed generous rewards and inspected the people.

初六：履霜，坚冰至。
谨慎：脚下已踏到霜，就知坚冰将至。
Prudent / Sequel: Treading on frost, one knows that hard ice is on the way.

六二：直、方、大，不习，无不利。
安定：正直方正，光大宏大，领受，无所不利。
Stability / Obsequious: Upright and square, bright and grand; in receiving, nothing is unfavorable.

六三：含章，可贞。或从王事，无成有终。
放纵：容忍显著（恶行），应该匡正。若放纵王的政事，没有成功，只能去死。
Indulgence / Sequence: Tolerating blatant (wrongdoing, which) should be corrected. If the king's affairs are left unchecked and not accomplished, death is the only outcome.

六四：括囊，无咎无誉。
柔顺：包容敛藏，因此无咎亦无誉。
Gentleness / Subsequent: Enclose and store things away; thus there is neither blame nor praise.

六五：黄裳，元吉。
训导：黄色坐裳，故大吉。
Instruction / Consequence: A yellow lower garment (to convey pride and purpose); great auspiciousness.

上六：龙战于野，其血玄黄。
降服：龙在天际战栗，流赤黑、明黄色的血。
Subdue / Exequy: The dragon trembles at the edge of the sky, shedding dark-red and bright-yellow blood.

用六：利永贞。
通六 顺从：利于长久守正。
All Six (Compliance): It is favorable to remain steadfast over the long term.
