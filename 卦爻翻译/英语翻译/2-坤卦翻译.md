《坤》<br>
循：安抚 (Placate/Chaplain) - 巡察 (Patrol/Celebrant)<br>
<br>
元亨。利牝马之贞。君子有攸往，先迷后得主。利西南得朋，东北丧朋。安贞吉。<br>
大通亨达。利于如牝马那样柔顺而守正。君子有所前往，先驱安抚，后来者感恩崇尚。往东南西北四方出走（巡狩），得到或失去钱财，安定、安抚（百姓），守正则吉。<br>
Great flourishing and free passage. It is favorable to be gentle and steadfast like a mare. The noble one has somewhere to go. The vanguard pacifies the region, while those who follow are filled with gratitude and admiration. Going east, south, west, or north, he may gain or lose wealth to bring stability and to soothe the people, steadfastness brings good fortune.<br>
参考义项：驯顺，天子巡狩。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地势坤，君子以厚德载物。<br>
载-施行：大地之势柔顺而广厚，这就是坤。君子因此庆赏丰厚，巡察众人。<br>
Implement: The earth's configuration is yielding and broad; this is Kun. Therefore the noble one bestowed generous rewards and inspected the people.<br>
<br>
初六：履霜，坚冰至。<br>
循行：霜来到，坚冰将至。<br>
Follow / Sequel: Frost coming, one knows that hard ice is on the way.<br>
<br>
六二：直方，大不习，无不利。<br>
循-遵守：执持以防备，持守，尊奉、习练，无所不利。<br>
Abide / Obsequious: Hold to guard, to uphold, to venerate and practice, nothing is unfavorable.<br>
<br>
六三：含章，可贞。或从王事，无成有终。<br>
顺从：显露章法，承荷担当。如果总领王的职事，不并列，能终了。<br>
Obey / Sequence: Revealing the structure, undertaking and assuming responsibility. If one leads the king's affairs, one should not be ranked alongside the king, but should be able to end. <br>
<br>
六四：括囊，无咎无誉。<br>
谨慎：阻滞敛藏，无咎亦无誉。<br>Cautious / Subsequent: Enclosed and store things away; there is neither blame nor praise.<br>
<br>
六五：黄裳，元吉。<br>
训导：黄色坐裳，欣赏，大吉。<br>
Instruction / Consequence: A yellow lower garment, appreciated; great auspiciousness.<br>
<br>
上六：龙战于野，其血玄黄。<br>
降服：龙在天际战栗，流赤黑、明黄色的血。<br>
Subdue / Exequy: The dragon trembles at the edge of the sky, shedding dark-red and bright-yellow blood.<br>
<br>
用六：利永贞。<br>
通六 和顺：利于长久守正。<br>
All Six (Mild): It is favorable to remain steadfast over the long term.<br>
