《坤》<br>
揗：安抚 (Conciliate/Cleric) - 驯服 (Tame/Paladin)<br>
<br>
元亨。利牝马之贞。君子有攸往，先迷后得主。利西南得朋，东北丧朋。安贞吉。<br>
恭谨，亨通。利于如牝马那样柔顺而守正。君子佑助忧思者，使人归向；教导安抚，厚待施恩，（使人）归属。
往东南西北四方出走（巡狩），得到或失去钱财，安定、安抚（百姓），守正则吉。<br>
Reverence, success. It is favorable to be gentle and steadfast like a mare. The noble one supports those burdened by worry, drawing them near; through guidance, comfort, and generous acts of kindness, he fosters a sense of belonging. Going east, south, west, or north, he may gain or lose wealth to bring stability and to soothe the people, steadfastness brings good fortune.<br>
参考义项：循顺（驯顺），天子巡狩。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地势坤，君子以厚德载物。<br>
顺从-载_施行：大地被种植，这就是坤。君子通过厚施恩德，省察众人。<br>
Obey-Implement: The earth is cultivated—this is *Kun* (the Receptive). The noble one, by bestowing generous favors, inspects for the people.<br>
<br>
初六：履霜，坚冰至。<br>
顺应：霜来到，坚冰将至。<br>
Follow / Sequel: Frost coming, one knows that hard ice is on the way.<br>
<br>
六二：直方，大不习，无不利。<br>
循-遵守：执持以防备，持守，尊奉、习练，无所不利。<br>
Abide / Obsequious: Hold to guard, to uphold, to venerate and practice, nothing is unfavorable.<br>
<br>
六三：含章，可贞。或从王事，无成有终。<br>
巡视：隐藏表彰，反而歌颂担当，有时奖劝或匡正职事；不要要求，而应佑助民众。<br>
Inspection / Pursuit: Rather than singling out individuals for commendation, focus on extolling a sense of responsibility; use the process to offer encouragement or to provide corrective guidance in the performance of duties. Do not make demands; instead, seek to support and assist the people.<br>
<br>
六四：括囊，无咎无誉。<br>
荐-进献：包括囊括，无咎，不要犹豫。<br>
Present / Ensue: Include and encompass, no blame; do not hesitate.<br>
<br>
六五：黄裳，元吉。<br>
训导：黄色坐裳，欣赏，大吉。<br>
Instruction / Consequence: A yellow lower garment, appreciated; great auspiciousness.<br>
<br>
上六：龙战于野，其血玄黄。<br>
降服：龙在天际战栗，流赤黑、明黄色的血。<br>
Subdue / Exequy: The dragon trembles at the edge of the sky, shedding dark-red and bright-yellow blood.<br>
<br>
用六：利永贞。<br>
通六 顺序：利于长久守正。<br>
All Six (Sequence): It is favorable to remain steadfast over the long term.<br>
