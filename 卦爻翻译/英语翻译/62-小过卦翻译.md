《小过》
短暂过往 (Fleeting Past) - 稍微超过 (Slightly Overshoot)

亨，利貞，可小事，不可大事。飛鳥遺之音，不宜上宜下，大吉。
通达，利于坚守。可以小办，不可大操大办。飞鸟留下的声音，不宜变大，宜于降低，将要吉祭。
Smooth and favorable to perseverance. Small matters may be be small-scale, not extravagant. The flying bird leaves its sound; it is not favorable to become large, but favorable to diminish. There will be ritual of Ji (Resumption of normal life from funeral). 
参考义项：大祥（葬礼两年后恢复正常生活，饭食中可用调味品）

《彖》曰：小過，小者過而亨也。過以利貞，與時行也。柔得中，是以小事吉也。剛失位而不中，是以不可大事也。有飛鳥之象焉，有飛鳥遺之音，不宜上宜下，大吉；上逆而下順也。

《象》曰：山上有雷，小過；君子以行過乎恭，喪過乎哀，用過乎儉。
庸-更替：雷在山上，小过卦。君子因此行事过于恭敬，丧事过于哀痛，用度过于节俭。
Alternation: Thunder on the mountain constitutes Small Excess. The superior person, therefore, is more than respectful in conduct, more than grieving in mourning, and more than economical in expenditure.

初六：飛鳥以凶。
受祸：飞蝗成灾，有凶。
Receiving misfortune: Excessive locust swarms; there is danger.

六二：過其祖，遇其妣；不及其君，遇其臣；無咎。
前往：拜访祖父，遇见祖母；未达君王处，遇见臣子；没有过错。
Proceeding: Visiting one's grandfather and meeting one's grandmother; failing to reach the ruler but meeting the minister; there is no blame.

九三：弗過防之，從或戕之，凶。
责备：没有犯错就预防，若放纵可能伤害他，凶。
Reproach: One guards against fault before any error has occurred; if indulges, one may instead harm him; there is danger.

九四：無咎，弗過遇之。往厲必戒，勿用永貞。
过失：没有错咎，不责备而礼遇他。过往的危厉必须警惕，不要长期固守。
Fault: There is no blame; one is not reproached but instead received with courtesy. The danger of the past must be kept in mind. Do not persist indefinitely.

六五：密云不雨，自我西郊，公弋取彼在穴。
经过：浓云密布却未降雨，从我西郊而来。王公射猎，在深穴中射取它（飞鸟）。
Passing by: Dense clouds form but no rain falls, coming from my western suburbs. The duke shoots and takes it (the bird) from deep cavity.

上六：弗遇過之，飛鳥離之，凶，是謂災眚。
作祸：未能求得而经过它，用网罗捕捉飞鸟，凶，这叫灾难罪过。
Creating calamity: Failing to meet, simply passed by—choosing instead to capture a flying bird with a net; there is danger. This is called disaster caused by fault.
