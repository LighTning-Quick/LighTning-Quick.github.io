《小过》<br>
短暂过往 (Fleeting Past) - 稍微超过 (Slightly Overshoot)<br>
<br>
亨，利貞，可小事，不可大事。飛鳥遺之音，不宜上宜下，大吉。<br>
通达，利于坚守。可以小办，不可大操大办。飞鸟留下的声音，不宜变大，宜于降低，将要吉祭。<br>
Smooth and favorable to perseverance. Small matters may be be small-scale, not extravagant. The flying bird leaves its sound; it is not favorable to become large, but favorable to diminish. There will be ritual of Ji (Resumption of normal life from funeral). <br>
参考义项：大祥（葬礼两年后恢复正常生活，饭食中可用调味品）<br>
<br>
《彖》曰：小過，小者過而亨也。過以利貞，與時行也。柔得中，是以小事吉也。剛失位而不中，是以不可大事也。有飛鳥之象焉，有飛鳥遺之音，不宜上宜下，大吉；上逆而下順也。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：山上有雷，小過；君子以行過乎恭，喪過乎哀，用過乎儉。<br>
庸-更替：雷在山上，小过卦。君子因此行事过于恭敬，丧事过于哀痛，用度过于节俭。<br>
Alternation: Thunder on the mountain constitutes Small Excess. The superior person, therefore, is more than respectful in conduct, more than grieving in mourning, and more than economical in expenditure.<br>
<br>
初六：飛鳥以凶。<br>
受祸：飞蝗成灾，有凶。<br>
Receiving misfortune: Excessive locust swarms; there is danger.<br>
<br>
六二：過其祖，遇其妣；不及其君，遇其臣；無咎。<br>
前往：拜访祖父，遇见祖母；未达君王处，得到臣庶；没有过错。<br>
Proceeding: Visiting one's grandfather and meeting one's grandmother; failing to reach the ruler but getting the subject; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九三：弗過防之，從或戕之，凶。<br>
责备：没有犯错就预防，若放纵可能伤害他，凶。<br>
Reproach: One guards against fault before any error has occurred; if indulges, one may instead harm him; there is danger.<br>
<br>
九四：無咎，弗過遇之。往厲必戒，勿用永貞。<br>
过失：没有错咎，不责备而礼遇他。过往的危厉必须警惕，不要长期固守。<br>
Fault: There is no blame; one is not reproached but instead received with courtesy. The danger of the past must be kept in mind. Do not persist indefinitely.<br>
<br>
六五：密云不雨，自我西郊，公弋取彼在穴。<br>
经过：浓云密布却未降雨，从我西郊而来。王公射猎，在深穴中射取它（飞鸟）。<br>
Passing by: Dense clouds form but no rain falls, coming from my western suburbs. The duke shoots and takes it (the bird) from deep cavity.<br>
<br>
上六：弗遇過之，飛鳥離之，凶，是謂災眚。<br>
作祸：不能遇合反而祸害它，用网罗捕捉飞鸟，凶，这叫灾难罪过。<br>
Creating calamity: Failing to please but instead harm it—to capture a flying bird with a net; there is danger. This is called disaster caused by fault.<br>
