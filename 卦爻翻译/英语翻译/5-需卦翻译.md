<br>
有孚，光亨，贞吉。利涉大川。<br>
（孺子）亲附，（儒者）推广，亨通；守正则吉。适宜渡过大河。<br>
(The young ones) Draw close (to the learned scholar); (the scholars, in turn,) propagate (their teachings), success. Remaining steadfast and upright brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross a great river.<br>
参考义项：孺，黏而亲附；儒，软而得民<br>
<br>
《象》曰：云上于天，《需》。君子以饮食宴乐。<br>
隐-安稳：云气升到天上，这就是需卦。君子通过殷盛地给与食物，来安定地治疗。<br>
Steady: Clouds rise above heaven; this is Xu. Thus the noble one heals people peacefully and calmly by providing them with abundant food.<br>
<br>
初九：需于郊，利用恒，无咎。<br>
须-必要：向郊祀供给，适宜有规律，没有灾咎。<br>
Must: In providing for suburban sacrifices, one should observe proper order and regularity; this ensures there will be no misfortune or calamity.<br>
<br>
九二：需于沙，小有言，终吉。<br>
须-必定：因趿拉迟缓，略有责言，最终吉祥。<br>
Certainty: A sense of sluggishness arises from shuffling along, leading to some mild reproach; yet, in the end, the outcome is auspicious.<br>
<br>
九三：需于泥，致寇至。<br>
濡忍：需要阻滞，导致盗寇到来。<br>
Forbearance: A need for obstruction of it, which leads to the arrival of bandits.<br>
<br>
六四：需于血，出自穴。<br>
需索：因谨慎而濡忍，用遵循来发布。<br>
Exact: Forbear with caution, and issue by following instructions.<br>
<br>
九五：需于酒食，贞吉。<br>
需要：需索酒食；守正则吉。<br>
Demand: Requesting the wine and food. Constancy brings good fortune.<br>
<br>
上六：入于穴，有不速之客三人来，敬之终吉。<br>
须-依靠：有三位不请自来的客人从锁着的门进来，使他震惊，最终吉祥。<br>
Reliance: Through a locked door, three uninvited guests arrive, leaving him startled—yet the outcome is ultimately auspicious.<br>
