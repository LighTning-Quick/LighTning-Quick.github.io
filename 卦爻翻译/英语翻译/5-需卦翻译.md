《需》<br>
糯：黏性 (Viscosity) - 輭：柔软 (Malleability)<br>
<br>
有孚，光亨，贞吉。利涉大川。<br>
（儒者）令人信从，光明，亨通；守正则吉。适宜渡过大河。<br>
(Pre-Confucian ritual masters) To make people believe and follow, radiant, and successful; constancy brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river.<br>
参考义项：儒，以道得民<br>
<br>
《象》曰：云上于天，《需》。君子以饮食宴乐。<br>
晏-温和：云气升到天上，这就是需卦。君子由此饮食宴乐。<br>
Mildness: Clouds rise above heaven; this is Xu. Thus the noble one dines and feasts.<br>
<br>
初九：需于郊，利用恒，无咎。<br>
等待：在远郊滞留，适宜有恒心，没有灾咎。<br>
Waiting: Remain in the distant outskirts. It is favorable to have perseverance; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：需于沙，小有言，终吉。<br>
需要：由于沙地缓慢，略有责言，最终吉祥。<br>
Need: The sandy ground causes delay. There is some minor reproach, but in the end there is good fortune.<br>
<br>
九三：需于泥，致寇至。<br>
懦弱：由于拘泥而迟疑，招致盗寇到来。<br>
Weakness: Hesitating because of rigid attachment draws raiders near.<br>
<br>
六四：需于血，出自穴。<br>
需索：被血液濡湿，从坑穴中脱出。<br>
Demand: Soaked with blood, one emerges from the pit.<br>
<br>
九五：需于酒食，贞吉。<br>
濡-润泽：供应酒食；守正则吉。<br>
Moistening: Provide wine and food. Constancy brings good fortune.<br>
<br>
上六：入于穴，有不速之客三人来，敬之终吉。<br>
须-任用：进入坑穴，有三位不请自来的客人；敬重他们，最终吉祥。<br>
Appointment: Enter the pit, where three uninvited guests arrive. Treat them with respect, and in the end there is good fortune.<br>
