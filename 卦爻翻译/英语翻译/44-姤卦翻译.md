《姤》
篝：竹笼 (The Bamboo Cage) - 缿：钱罐 (The Coin Slot)

女壮，勿用取女。
（嫁女时）女子装扮。不可用（花言巧语）来娶妻。
When a woman is dressed for marriage, rhetoric cannot be used for taking wife.
参考义项：巧言令色。诟，花言巧语。

《象》曰：天下有风，《姤》。后以施命诰四方。
求告：天下有风，是姤卦之象。君主以此发布政令，宣告四方。
Petition: Wind under heaven, Encountering. The ruler proclaims orders to the four quarters.

初六：系于金柅，贞吉。有攸往，见凶，羸豕孚蹢躅。
邂逅：系于金柅，贞正则吉。若有所往，碰见，凶。瘦弱的猪浮躁不安，徘徊跳跃。
Encounter: Tied to a golden brake, uprightness brings fortune. If you go forth, you meet with misfortune. The lean sow leaps restlessly, pacing and wavering.

《象》曰："系于金柅"，柔道牵也。
"系于金柅"，柔弱之道受到牵引。
"Tied to a golden brake," the soft way is restrained.

九二：包有鱼，无咎，不利宾。
谋求：聚集以渔取，无咎过，但不利于宾服。
Strategizing: Gathering to fish, no blame, but it does not benefit submitting as guests.

《象》曰："包有鱼"，义不及"宾"也。
"包有鱼"，按义理不给予宾客。
"Having fish in the kitchen," its principle does not extend to guests.

九三：臀无肤，其行次且，厉，无大咎。
知晓：臀部无肤，行走艰难，有危险，但无大咎过。
Know: The buttocks are without flesh, walking is difficult—there is danger, but no great blame.

《象》曰："其行次且"，行未牵也。
"其行次且"，行走未被牵引。
"Walking is difficult," the movement is not guided.

九四：包无鱼，起凶。
求购：厨房中没有鱼，求取，凶。
Purchase: There is no fish in the kitchen—seeking to acquire, brings misfortune.

《象》曰："无鱼"之"凶"，远民也。
"无鱼"之"凶"，是远离民众的结果。
"The misfortune of having no fish" comes from keeping distance from the people.

九五：以杞包瓜，含章，有陨自天。
媾和：用杞柳包裹瓜果，内藏文采，获得自天而陨落之物。
Compromise: Wrapping melons with willow wood, containing beauty within, obtaining what falls from heaven.

《象》曰："九五含章"，中正也。"有陨自天"，志不舍命也。
"九五含章"，居中守正。"有陨自天"，其志愿不舍弃盟友。
"Nine-five contains beauty," it holds the center and correctness. "Something falls from heaven," its will does not abandon its allies.

上六：姤其角，吝，无咎。
妒忌：角逐中落后，有吝难，但无咎过。
Jealousy: Falling behind in the contention brings difficulty, yet there is no blame.

《象》曰："姤其角"，上穷"吝"也。
"姤其角"，居上而穷困，故生吝难。
"Encountering its horns," at the top and exhausted, thus arises difficulty.
