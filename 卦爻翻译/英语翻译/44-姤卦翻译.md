《姤》
钩梯 (The Hook Ladder) - 鱼钩 (The Fish Hook)

女壮，勿用取女。
嫁女时装扮她。不可用（花言巧语勾着够不着的）来娶妻。
Dress her up for her wedding. Don't use (sweet talk to seduce someone you can't reach) to marry her.
参考义项：阿谀嫁娶，巧言令色。诟，花言巧语。

《象》曰：天下有风，《姤》。后以施命诰四方。
求告：天下有风，是姤卦之象。君主以此发布政令，宣告四方。
Petition: Wind under heaven, Encountering. The ruler proclaims orders to the four quarters.

初六：系于金柅，贞吉。有攸往，见凶，羸豕孚蹢躅。
邂逅：系于金属辖軎，贞正则吉，有所往。碰见，凶。瘦弱的猪浮躁不安，徘徊跳跃。
Encounter: Tied to a metal axle stopper, persistence brings fortune, going forth. Meeting, misfortunate. The lean sow leaps restlessly, pacing and wavering.

《象》曰："系于金柅"，柔道牵也。
"系于金柅"，柔弱之道受到牵引。
"Tied to a metal axle stopper," the soft way is restrained.

九二：包有鱼，无咎，不利宾。
谋求：聚集以渔取，无咎过，但不利于招待宾客。
Strategizing: Gathering to fish, no blame, but it is not suitable for entertaining guests.

《象》曰："包有鱼"，义不及"宾"也。
"包有鱼"，按义理不这样给予宾客。
"Gathering to fish," according to moral principles, this is not how guests should be treated (with calculation).

九三：臀无肤，其行次且，厉，无大咎。
遭遇：臀部无肤，行走艰难，有危险，但无大咎过。
Encountering: The buttocks are without flesh, walking is difficult—there is danger, but no great blame.

《象》曰："其行次且"，行未牵也。
"其行次且"，行走未被牵引。
"Walking is difficult," the movement is not guided.

九四：包无鱼，起凶。
求购：厨房中没有鱼，求取，凶。
Purchase: There is no fish in the kitchen—seeking to acquire, brings misfortune.

《象》曰："无鱼"之"凶"，远民也。
"无鱼"之"凶"，是远离民众的结果。
"The misfortune of having no fish" comes from keeping distance from the people.

九五：以杞包瓜，含章，有陨自天。
讲求：用杞柳包裹瓜果，包容防范，获得自天而陨落之物。
Hold fast: Wrapping melons with willow wood, inclusive and preventative, obtaining what falls from heaven.

《象》曰："九五含章"，中正也。"有陨自天"，志不舍命也。
"九五含章"，居中守正。"有陨自天"，其志愿不舍弃天命。
"Nine-five inclusive and preventative," it holds the center and correctness. "Something falls from heaven," its will does not abandon the mandate.

上六：姤其角，吝，无咎。
媾和：谋求角逐，有吝难，但无咎过。
Compromise: Strategically manage the competition, brings difficulty, yet there is no blame.

《象》曰："姤其角"，上穷"吝"也。
"姤其角"，居上而穷困，故生吝难。
"Strategically manage the competition," at the top and exhausted, thus arises difficulty.
