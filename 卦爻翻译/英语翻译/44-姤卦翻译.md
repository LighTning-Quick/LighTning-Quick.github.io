钩梯 (The Hook Ladder) - 鱼钩 (The Fish Hook)<br>
<br>
女壮，勿用取女。<br>
嫁女时装扮她。不可用（花言巧语勾引）来娶妻。<br>
Dress her up for her wedding. Don't use (sweet talk to seduce) to marry her.<br>
参考义项：阿谀嫁娶，巧言令色。诟，花言巧语。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天下有风，《姤》。后以施命诰四方。<br>
求告：天下有风，是姤卦之象。君主以此发布政令，宣告四方。<br>
Petition: Wind moves beneath heaven; this is Gou. The ruler proclaims commands to the four quarters.<br>
<br>
初六：系于金柅，贞吉。有攸往，见凶，羸豕孚蹢躅。<br>
邂逅：系于金属辖軎，贞正则吉，有所往。碰见，凶。瘦弱的猪浮躁不安，徘徊跳跃。<br>
Encounter: Tied to a metal axle stopper, steadfastness brings good fortune. If meets, misfortune appears. The lean sow restlessly paces and wavers.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："系于金柅"，柔道牵也。<br>
"系于金柅"，柔弱之道受到牵引。<br>
"Tied to a metal axle stopper," the soft way is restrained.<br>
<br>
九二：包有鱼，无咎，不利宾。<br>
谋求：用渔取侵占来聚集，无咎错，但不利于招待宾客。<br>
Seeking: To amass through plunder. There is no blame, but it is not suitable for receiving guests.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："包有鱼"，义不及"宾"也。<br>
"包有鱼"，按义理不这样给予宾客。<br>
"To amass through plunder," according to moral principles, this is not how guests should be treated (with calculation).<br>
<br>
九三：臀无肤，其行次且，厉，无大咎。<br>
遭遇：臀部无肤，行走艰难，有危险，但无大咎过。<br>
Encountering: The buttocks are without flesh and walking is difficult. There is danger, but no great blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："其行次且"，行未牵也。<br>
"其行次且"，行走未被牵引。<br>
"Walking is difficult"; the movement is not guided.<br>
<br>
九四：包无鱼，起凶。<br>
求购：囊括而不统御，征召，凶。<br>
Purchase: To encompass without governing, to conscript,brings misfortune.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："无鱼"之"凶"，远民也。<br>
"无鱼"之"凶"，是远离民众的结果。<br>
The misfortune of "not governing" comes from being distant from the people.<br>
<br>
九五：以杞包瓜，含章，有陨自天。<br>
讲求：用杞柳包裹瓜果，包容防范，（天坛，即圜丘）设圆界利用天时。<br>
Hold fast: Wrap the melon with willow, inclusive and preventative; set up (the Temple of Heaven, aka the Circular Mound Altar with) a circular boundary to make use of the timing of heaven.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："九五含章"，中正也。"有陨自天"，志不舍命也。<br>
"九五含章"，居中守正。"有陨自天"，其志愿不舍弃天命。<br>
"The nine in the fifth place is inclusive and preventative," being centered and correct. "set up a circular boundary to make use of the timing of heaven"; its purpose does not abandon the mandate.<br>
<br>
上六：姤其角，吝，无咎。<br>
媾和：谋求角逐，有吝难，但无咎过。<br>
Compromise: Strategically manage the competition, brings difficulty, yet there is no blame.<br>
<br>
《象》曰："姤其角"，上穷"吝"也。<br>
"姤其角"，居上而穷困，故生吝难。<br>
"Strategically manage the competition," means being exhausted at the top, hence arises difficulty.<br>
