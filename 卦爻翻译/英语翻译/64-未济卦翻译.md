《未济》
未霽-阴郁 (Murkiness) - 不停 (Persistence)

未济，亨。小狐汔济，濡其尾，无攸利。
未济，亨通。小狐将要渡河，却沾湿了尾巴，无所利。
When matters are unsettled, all proceeds. The small fox is about to cross the river, yet wets its tail—nothing is advantageous.
参考义项：

《象》曰：火在水上，《未济》。君子以慎辨物居方。
改变：火在水上，未济。君子由此谨慎备办事物，攻克妨碍。
Change: Fire rests above water; this is Unsettled. The superior person, contemplating this, carefully prepares affairs and overcomes obstacles.

初六：濡其尾，吝。
利用：烹煮尾巴，吝惜。
Exploitation: Boil the tail; Stingy.

九二：曳其轮，贞吉。
整治：（弓檠）拴缚它的轮廓，守正则吉。
Rectification: (The bow's lintel) binds its outline, persistence brings good fortune.

六三：未济，征凶，利涉大川。
齐备充足：未成功，出征有凶，但利于涉越大川。
Redundancy: Unsettled, to advance brings misfortune, yet it is favorable to cross great rivers.

九四：贞吉，悔亡。震用伐鬼方，三年有赏于大国。
跻-登上到达：守正则吉，悔恨消失。整治，征伐鬼方，三年后在大国受赏。
Ascension: Persistence brings good fortune, regret vanishes. Reorganizing, to attack the Ghost Regions; after three years, rewarded by the great state.

六五：贞吉，无悔。君子之光，有孚，吉。
救济：守正则吉，没有悔恨。君子的宽广，使人信服，吉。
Redemption: Persistence brings good fortune, no regret. The superior person's breadth inspires trust; auspicious.

上九：有孚于饮酒，无咎。濡其首，有孚失是。
增补助益：因饮酒有所辅助，没有灾咎。（但若）淹没其头，（则）有所背离，（因）淫泆过甚。
Augmentation: It helps because of drinking wine; no blame. But if it drowns one's head, then there will be adverse effects because of excessive indulgence.
