《未济》
未霽-阴郁 (Murkiness) - 不停 (Persistence)

未济，亨。小狐汔济，濡其尾，无攸利。
未济，亨通。小狐勉强渡河（从漩涡生还），（吓得）尿到尾巴：不适宜做事
When matters are unsettled, all proceeds. The little fox barely crossed the river (surviving the whirlpool), and (frightened) peed on its tail: unsuitable for doing anything. 
参考义项：齐，通“ 臍 “，漩涡

《象》曰：火在水上，《未济》。君子以慎辨物居方。
改变：火在水上，未济。君子由此谨慎备办事物，攻克妨碍。
Change: Fire rests above water; this is Unsettled. The superior person, contemplating this, carefully prepares affairs and overcomes obstacles.

初六：濡其尾[^3]，吝。
利用：烹煮尾巴，吝惜。
Exploitation: Boil the tail; Stingy.

九二：曳其轮[^4]，贞吉。
整治：（弓檠）拴缚它的轮廓，守正则吉。
Rectification: (The bow's lintel) binds its outline, persistence brings good fortune.

六三：未济，征凶，利涉大川。
齐备充足：未成功，出征有凶，但利于涉越大川。
Redundancy: Unsettled, to advance brings misfortune, yet it is favorable to cross great rivers.

九四：贞吉，悔亡。震用伐鬼方[^5]，三年有赏于大国。
跻-登上到达：守正则吉，悔恨消失。整治，征伐鬼方，三年超过大国。
Ascension: Persistence brings good fortune, regret vanishes. Reorganizing, to attack the Ghost Regions; surpassing the great state in three years.

六五：贞吉，无悔。君子之光，有孚，吉。[^6]
救济：守正则吉，没有悔恨。君子的宽广，使人信服，吉。
Redemption: Persistence brings good fortune, no regret. The superior person's breadth inspires trust; auspicious.

上九：有孚于饮酒，无咎。濡其首，有孚失是。[^7]
增益：饮酒有帮助，没有灾咎。（但若）沾湿头发，增加（则）轻忽职守。
Augmentation: Drinking alcohol can be helpful; it will not bring misfortune. However, if one's hair gets wet, to drink more will lead to neglect of one's duties.
