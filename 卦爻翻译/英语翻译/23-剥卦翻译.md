《剥》
胞衣 (Afterbirth) - 服马 (Wheel Horse)

不利有攸往。
不利于前往做事。
It is not favorable to go forward with affairs.
参考义项：服丧（仆/朴）

《象》曰：山附于地，《剥》。上以厚下安宅。
亲厚：山贴附在地上，这就是剥卦。居上位的人以此后离开，遏止（军队）分崩离析。
Kinship: The mountain clings to the earth; this is Bo. Therefore, those above use this to bring up the rear during a withdrawal, stopping (the army) from falling apart.

初六：剥床以足蔑，贞凶。
负-依凭：击打床坐来制止轻蔑，这样下去会有凶险。
Reliance / Suppose: Strike the seat to check contempt; if this continues, there will be danger.

六二：剥床以辨蔑，贞凶。
朴实：掊挖底部来辨明污蔑，这样下去会有凶险。
Plainness / Propose: Dig into the base to identify slander; if this continues, there will be danger.

六三：剥之，无咎。
驳-相异：背离它，没有错咎。
Divergence / Compose: Deviate from it; there is no blame.

六四：剥床以肤，凶。
服-承担：依凭于床来仆服，凶险。
Bearing / Depose: Rely on the bed to lie prostrate; this is dangerous.

六五：贯鱼以宫人，宠，无不利。
服御：射中鱼靠宫人，宠劳，没有不利。
Harnessing / Dispose: Shooting fish relies on palace attendants; bestowing favor, brings no disadvantage.

上九：硕果不食，君子得舆，小人剥庐。
保护：大果子还没有被吃掉。君子得到党与，小人持着矛、戟的柄。
Protection / Expose: large fruits are not yet eaten. The noble one can gain favor and support, while the petty hold the handles of spears and halberds.
