《鼎》<br>
贞固 (Firm) - 贞美 (Chaste)<br>
<br>
鼎：元吉，亨。<br>
鼎：至为吉祥，（烹煮）亨通。<br>
Ding: Greatly auspicious; (cooking) with success.<br>
参考义项：顽强，烹煮。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：木上有火，《鼎》。君子以正位凝命。<br>
拟定：木上有火，这是鼎卦。君子因此端正其位，巩固天命。<br>
Drafting: Fire above wood; this is Ding. The noble one thereby rectifies one's station and consolidates the mandate.<br>
<br>
初六：鼎颠趾，利出否。得妾以其子，无咎。<br>
贞信：鼎腿填实，利于制止贪吝。因得妾而有其子，没有咎害。<br>
Integrity / Fundament / Maqām: The cauldron's legs are packed solid—favorable for curbing greed and miserliness. A concubine is gained on account of her son; no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：鼎有实，我仇有疾，不我能即，吉。<br>
担当：鼎中盛有粮食，我的怨偶患有疾患，不能靠近我，吉祥。<br>
Structure / Found / Qiyām: The cauldron is filled with grain; my adversary has an affliction and cannot come at me—good fortune.<br>
<br>
九三：鼎耳革，其行塞，雉膏不食，方雨，亏，悔，终吉。<br>
坚贞：鼎耳改换（不能插入鼎铉），行动受阻，雉膏不得食用；正逢下雨，亏损，掩蔽，最终吉祥。<br>
Fortitude / Infound / Istiqāmah: The cauldron's ears are altered (the carrying bar cannot be inserted) and its movement is blocked; the pheasant fat is not eaten. Just as rain falls, there is loss, (still) concealing, in the end, good fortune.<br>
<br>
九四：鼎折足，覆公餗，其形渥，凶。<br>
匡正：鼎折断足，倾覆王公的餗食，受剭刑（屋诛），凶险。<br>
Correction / Defound / Taqwīm: The cauldron's legs break, overturning the duke's stew; one suffers house-execution; misfortune.<br>
<br>
六五：鼎黄耳金铉，利贞。<br>
正直：鼎有黄耳与金铉，宜于守正。<br>
Mastery / Confound / Qawām: The cauldron with yellow ears and a metal carrying bar—favorable to persist.<br>
<br>
上九：鼎玉铉，大吉，无不利。<br>
安定：鼎有玉铉（一说玉盖），大吉，没有不利。<br>
Solidarity / Profound / Qā'im: The cauldron with a jade carrying bar (some read: a jade cover)—great good fortune; nothing is unfavorable.<br>
