《中孚》<br>
衷心姇悦 (Heartfelt Felicity) - 适合发孚 (Aptly Germinating)<br>
<br>
豚鱼吉，利涉大川，利贞。<br>
猪鱼（䱐，江豚）吉祥。（衷心姇悦）利于渡涉大川。（适合发孚）利于保持。<br>
Porcine fish (river dolphin) are auspicious. Favorable to cross great rivers (Heartfelt Felicity); favorable to maintain (Aptly Germinating).<br>
参考义项：孕育，妊娠、哺乳；䱐，江豚；脬，膀胱<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽上有木，《中孚》。君子以议狱缓死。<br>
名义的：泽上有木，这就是中孚卦。君子因此审议狱案，宽缓死刑。<br>
Virtual: Wood above the marsh; this is Zhongfu. The superior man deliberates on legal cases and postpones executions.<br>
<br>
初九：虞吉，有它不燕。<br>
孵化：准备，吉祥。宽宥蜿蜒蛇行，而勿燕安逸乐。<br>
Incubation: Preparation, auspicious. Condoning the slithering path, yet never settling for idle ease.<br>
<br>
九二：鸣鹤在阴，其子和之。我有好爵，吾与尔靡之。<br>
信任：鹤在隐蔽处鸣叫，其配偶与之应和。我有美酒，与你共饮。<br>
Trust: A crane calls in hiding; its spouse responds. I have fine wine; I will drink with you.<br>
<br>
六三：得敌，或鼓或罢，或泣或歌。<br>
敷-散布：获得敌人，有时击鼓，有时停歇；有时哭泣，有时歌颂。<br>
Dissemination: Capture the enemy; sometimes drum, sometimes stop; sometimes weep, sometimes praise.<br>
<br>
六四：月几望，马匹亡。<br>
虚浮：月亮达到望月，马与鸭子狂乱。<br>
Frivolous: When the moon reaches its full phase, horses and ducks go wild. <br>
<br>
九五：有孚挛如，无咎。<br>
信用：使信从，如系联，没有过错。<br>
Trustworthiness: To make people believe and follow, as if linked; no blame.<br>
<br>
上九：翰音登于天，贞凶。<br>
付与：鸡进献于天，坚持下去有凶险。<br>
Offering: A chicken offered to Heaven; perseverance brings misfortune.<br>
