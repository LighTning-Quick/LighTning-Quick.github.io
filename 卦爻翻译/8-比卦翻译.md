《比》
篦顺 (Combing) - 矢栝 (Nocking)

吉。原筮元。永贞无咎。不宁方来，后夫凶。
参考义项：太卜/朝会
翻译：吉利。推究占卜，行动情状体，长久守正无过错。不安宁的正在来归附，迟来的有凶险。
英文：Auspicious. Examine the divination, situation aspect of activity, long-lasting uprightness has no blame. Restless ones are coming to submit; those who come late face danger.

《象》曰：地上有水，《比》。先王以建万国，亲诸侯。
亲近：地面上有水，这就是比卦。先王以此建立万国，亲近诸侯。
Intimacy: Water is on the earth; this is Bi. Therefore the former kings by this established ten thousand states and drew near to the feudal lords.

初六：有孚比之，无咎。有孚盈缶，终来有它，吉。
具备：有俘虏，聚集他们，没有过错。有俘虏捧着陶罐，最终有负载，吉利。
Preparation / Fitted Out: Have captives, gather them, no blame. Captives hold the earthenware vessel; finally there is a load, auspicious.
希腊文图式：pistis
英文直译：faith / trust / confidence

六二：比之自内，贞吉。
亲近：从内部亲近，坚守正道吉利。
Intimacy / Fitting: Drawing near from within, maintain uprightness, auspicious.
希腊文图式：philos
英文直译：friend / beloved

六三：比之匪人。
寄托：比附不当的人，受到伤害。
Entrustment / Refit: Attach to inappropriate people; this brings harm.
希腊文图式：parasitos
英文直译：hanger-on / toady

六四：外比之，贞吉。
比附：对外亲近，坚守正道吉利。
Alignment / Outfit: Drawing near from outside, maintain uprightness, auspicious.
希腊文图式：symmaches
英文直译：ally / companion

九五：显比，王用三驱，失前禽，邑人不诫，吉。
效法：显明亲近，君王用三驱之礼，失掉前来的猎物，邑人不警诫，吉利。
Imitation / Benefit: Manifest drawing near. The king uses the three-drive ritual, loses the game coming forward, the townspeople are not warned, auspicious.
希腊文图式：mimetes
英文直译：imitator / follower

上六：比之无首，凶。
勾结：无道地亲近，凶险。
Collusion / Overfit: Drawing near without principle, dangerous.
希腊文图式：sumphonia
英文直译：conspiracy / collusion
