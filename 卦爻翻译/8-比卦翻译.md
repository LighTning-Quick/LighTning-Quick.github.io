翻译：吉利。推究占卜，行动情状体，长久守正无过错。（时局）不平静，正在招徕，迟来的有凶险。
英文：Auspicious. Examine the divination, situation aspect of activity, long-lasting uprightness has no blame. In turbulent times, currently inviting; those who come late face grave danger.
参考义项：太卜/朝会

《象》曰：地上有水，《比》。先王以建万国，亲诸侯。
亲近、更新：地面上有水，这就是比卦。先王以此建立万国，更新诸侯。
Endearment / renewal: Water is on the earth; this is Bi. Therefore the earlier kings by this established ten thousand states and renewed the feudal lords.
