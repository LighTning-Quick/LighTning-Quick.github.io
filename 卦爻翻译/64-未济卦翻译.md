《未济》
未霽-阴郁 (Murkiness) - 未停 (Persistence)

未济，亨。小狐汔济，濡其尾，无攸利。
未济，亨通。小狐将要渡河，却沾湿了尾巴，无所利。
When matters are unsettled, all proceeds. The small fox is about to cross the river, yet wets its tail—nothing is advantageous.
参考义项：

《象》曰：火在水上，《未济》。君子以慎辨物居方。
改变：火在水上，未济。君子由此谨慎备办事物，攻克妨碍。
Change: Fire rests above water; this is Unsettled. The superior person, contemplating this, carefully prepares affairs and overcomes obstacles.

初六：濡其尾，吝。
利用：尾巴被沾湿了，有憾。
The tail gets wet; there is regret.

九二：曳其轮，贞吉。
整治：拖住车轮，守正则吉。
Dragging the wheel, persistence brings good fortune.

六三：未济，征凶，利涉大川。
跻-登上到达：未渡之时，出征有凶，但利于涉越大川。
Ascent - To Arrive by Climbing: Unsettled, to advance brings misfortune, yet it is favorable to cross great rivers.

九四：贞吉，悔亡。震用伐鬼方，三年有赏于大国。
齐备充足：守正则吉，悔恨消失。振奋征伐鬼方，三年后在大国受赏。
Persistence brings good fortune, regret vanishes. Aroused strength to attack the Ghost Regions; after three years, rewarded by the great state.

六五：贞吉，无悔。君子之光，有孚，吉。
救济：守正则吉，没有悔恨。君子的宽广，使人信服，吉。
Persistence brings good fortune, no regret. The superior person's breadth inspires trust; auspicious.

上九：有孚于饮酒，无咎。濡其首，有孚失是。
调剂弥补：心中有把握而饮酒，没有灾咎。（但若因此）濡湿其首，便是虽有诚信却失了正道。
With confidence one drinks wine; no blame. (But if this leads to) soaking one's head, then even with sincerity, one loses the proper way.
