《复》
土窟 (Cave Dwelling)-夹衣 (Layered Clothing)

亨。出入无疾。朋来无咎。反复其道，匕曰来复。利有攸往。
参考义项：复魂（覆/復）
翻译：通达。往来都没有灾病，朋友来了，没有过错。人回魂则回到正道上；(否则)用木匙承载牲体，是招致复苏、回返的动作。适合有所前往。
英文：Smooth. Going out and coming in with no illness or harm. When friends come, there is no fault. When the soul returns, the ritual returns to the right course; otherwise, carry the sacrificial body with a wooden spoon, which is an act that brings about revival and return. It is favorable to go forward.
希腊文图式：epistrophe
英文直译：return / turning back

《象》曰：雷在地中，《复》。先王以至日闭关，商旅不行，后不省方。
闭藏：雷藏在地下，这就是复卦。古代君王因此在冬至时关闭关口，使商旅不出行，君主也不巡察四方。
Closure: Thunder is hidden within the earth; this is Fu. Therefore, on the winter solstice, the former kings closed the passes, merchants and travelers did not go out, and the ruler did not inspect the four directions.
希腊文图式：kleisis
英文直译：closure

初九：不远复，无祗悔，元吉。
恢复：（走出去）不远就能回来，没有大的悔恨，大吉。
Restoration / Assist: If one does not go far before returning, there is no great regret. Great good fortune.
希腊文图式：metanoeo
英文直译：change one's mind / repent / turn back

六二：休复，吉。
覆庇：荫庇之复，吉祥。
Shelter / Consist: As the protection of shade, is auspicious.
希腊文图式：splagchnizomai
英文直译：be moved with compassion / be sheltered in mercy

六三：频复，厉，无咎。
宽宥：以宾客相待之复，虽然有危险，但还不至于有过错。
Leniency / Resist: As the manner of treating someone as a guest, carries danger, yet it still does not amount to fault.
希腊文图式：apheimi
英文直译：release / let go / forgive

六四：中行独复。
重复：在中道上熟练地践行。
Iteration / Persist: One practices it skillfully while walking the middle course.
希腊文图式：kaleo
英文直译：call / summon

六五：敦复，无悔。
丰厚：敦厚之复，所以没有悔恨。
Plenitude / Subsist: As fullness, brings no regret.
希腊文图式：karpos / perisseuo
英文直译：fruit / abound

上六：迷复，凶，有灾眚。用行师，终有大败，以其国君，凶，至于十年不克征。
刚愎：沉迷之复，结果凶险，又有灾祸。若以此出兵，最终会因为国君大败，有凶祸，长期都不能取胜。
Obstinacy / Insist: As delusion, is ominous and brings calamity. If one uses this to send out troops, in the end there will be a great defeat because of the ruler, there will be misfortune, and for a long time there will be no success in campaign.
希腊文图式：sklerokardia
英文直译：hardness of heart
