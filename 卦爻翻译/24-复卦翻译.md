《复》
肚腹 (Incubator) - 夹衣 (Lined Garment)

亨。出入无疾。朋来无咎。反复其道，匕曰来复。利有攸往。
参考义项：复魂（覆/復）
翻译：通达。往来都没有灾病，朋友来了，没有过错。人回魂则回到正道上；(否则)用木匙承载牲体，是要使之颠倒。适合有所前往。
英文：Smooth. Coming and going brings no illness. Friends arrive without fault. When the soul returns, one returns to the right path; (otherwise), carrying the sacrificial body with a wooden spoon is to overturn it. It is favorable to go forward.
希腊文图式：epistrophe
英文直译：return / turning back

《象》曰：雷在地中，《复》。先王以至日闭关，商旅不行，后不省方。
庀，治理：雷藏在地下，复卦。古代君王因此在冬至时关闭关口，使商旅不出行，君主也不巡察四方。
Governance: Thunder is hidden within the earth; this is Fu. Therefore, on the winter solstice, the former kings closed the passes, merchants and travelers did not go out, and the ruler did not inspect the four directions.
希腊文图式：kleisis
英文直译：closure

初九：不远复，无祗悔，元吉。
遏止：不远就归还，没有大的悔恨，大吉。
Curb: If one is returned without going far, there is no great regret. Great good fortune.
希腊文图式：metanoeo
英文直译：change one's mind / repent / turn back

六二：休复，吉。
覆庇：荫庇之复，吉祥。
Shelter / Consist: As the protection of shade, auspicious.
希腊文图式：splagchnizomai
英文直译：be moved with compassion / be sheltered in mercy

六三：频复，厉，无咎。
宽宥：以宾客相待之复，虽然有危险，但还不至于有过错。
Leniency: Treating someone as a guest carries danger, yet it does not amount to fault.
希腊文图式：apheimi
英文直译：release / let go / forgive

六四：中行独复。
重复：适合巡视审慎考求。
Iteration / Persist: It is suitable to make inspections and careful inquiries.
希腊文图式：kaleo
英文直译：call / summon

六五：敦复，无悔。
覆盖：覆焘之复，所以没有悔恨。
Cover: As covering over, it brings no regret.
希腊文图式：karpos / perisseuo
英文直译：fruit / abound

上六：迷复，凶，有灾眚。用行师，终有大败，以其国君，凶，至于十年不克征。
刚愎：沉迷，结果凶险，又有灾祸。若以此出兵，最终大败，罢黜国君，有凶祸，长期都不能取胜。
Obstinacy: Delusion is ominous and brings calamity. If one sends out troops, there will ultimately be great defeat, deposing the ruler, there will be misfortune, and for a long time there will be no success in campaigns.
希腊文图式：sklerokardia
英文直译：hardness of heart
